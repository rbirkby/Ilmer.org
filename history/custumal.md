In the eleventh year of the reign of King Edward the Third after the conquest of England, on Thursday in Easter week, in the year of the Lord one thousand three hundred and thirty-seven.

An inquisition taken at Ilmer on the Wednesday next after the feast of the Epiphany of the Lord in the eleventh year of the reign of King Edward the Third after the Conquest, by virtue of a certain writ of the lord king directed to the justices to inquire about the customs and services of the tenants of the manor of Ilmer, and who the tenants are, and how much land each holds, and by what services.

John, son of Nicholas, holds one virgate of land in bondage and owes per year for the lord's work by custom and service, namely for the Thressemann one division of hay at the valley of Ilmer between the land of Richard le Fevre and the land formerly of Robert le Chapman. Also he ought to mow with the whole tenement of the lord the next week after the feast of Saint John the Baptist for one meal from the lord, and whenever he mows with the whole tenement of the lord he ought to have one sheaf of grain. Also he ought at the task of the lord to carry grain for four days with the lord's meal, and if he does not eat then he ought to have one obol. Also he ought at three boon-works of autumn with the lord's meal, and if he does not eat then he ought to have one obol. Also he ought for the lord's carriage the day after the feast of Saint Martin to Warhull to the amount of one cart with four beasts and one man for one meal from the lord, and if he does not eat then he ought to have one obol. Also he owes for the sheriff's aid two pence at the view of the steward. Also he owes merchet for his daughter and heriot after death, and he cannot sell wood nor cut down nor fell without the lord's license.

Summary of tenants in bondage: five virgates of land and four cottages.

Concerning the free tenants.

The Prior of Tickford holds for one hide of land with appurtenances by service per year for two suits of court.

William Trussel holds for half a hide of land with appurtenances by service per year for one suit of court.

John de Molyns holds for half a hide of land with appurtenances by service per year for one suit of court.

Geoffrey de Hotham holds for one virgate of land with appurtenances by service per year for one suit of court.
