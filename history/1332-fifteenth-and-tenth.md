## ILMER WITH ASTON SANDFORD

#### Subsidy 242/4, membrane 18 Ilmere cum Aston Bernard Subtaxers: Robert Norich and Henry Luci

Thomas Blaket had 2 beasts 40d., 7 oxen 24s., 6 qr. wheat 20s., 5 qr.\
drage 12s. 6d., 4 qr. beans 10s., 1 cart with fittings 2s., hay and fodder 6s.\
**Sum** £3 17s. 10d. Thence 15th. 5s. 2¾d.

Richard de Kingesport had 1 ox 6s., 1 cow 5s., 1 qr. maslin 3s., 1 qr.\
drage 2s. 6d., 2 qr. beans 5s., hay and fodder 4s.\
**Sum** 25s. 6d. Thence 15th. 20¼d.

John Ernald had 1 beast 2s., 1 heifer 3s., 1 cow 5s., 40 sheep 40s., ½ qr.\
wheat 20d., ½ qr. drage 15d., hay and fodder 12d.\
**Sum** 53s. 11d. Thence 15th. 3s. 7¼d.

Prioress of Stodleye had 1 beast 2s., 1 ox 6s., 1 heifer 4s., 2 qr. wheat\
6s. 8d., 3 qr. drage 7s. 6d., 2 qr. beans 5s., hay and fodder 12d.\
**Sum** 32s. 4d. Thence 15th. 2s. 0¾d.

John Ustard had 1 beast 20d., 1 cow 7s., ½ qr. wheat 20d., ½ qr. beans\
15d., fodder 6d.\
**Sum** 12s. M. Thence 15th. 9¼d.

Thomas Cok had 1 mare 4s., 1 ox 8s., 2 qr. wheat 6s. 8d., 1 qr. drage\
2s. 6d., 1 qr. beans 2s. 6d., 40 sheep 4os., hay and fodder 5 s.\
**Sum** £3 8s. 8d. Thence 15th. 4 s. 6¾d.

Catrina Oripede had 1 beast 2s., 2 oxen 15s., 1 cow 6s., 2 qr. wheat\
6s. 8d., 3 qr. drage 7s., 2 qr. beans 5s., 2o sheep 2os., hay and fodder 4s.\
**Sum** £3 5s. 8d. Thence 15th. 4 s. 4½d.

Richard Janeksons had 1 beast 3s., 1 ox 8s., 1 cow 5s., 1 qr. wheat 40d.,\
1 qr. drage 2s. 6d., 1 qr. beans 2s. 6d., 6 sheep 6s., hay and fodder 18d.\
**Sum** 41s. 10d. Thence 15th. 2s. 10¾d.

Walter Zouger had 1 beast 2s., 1 ox 8s., 1 cow 6s., 2 qr. wheat 6s. 8d.,\
1 qr. beans 2s. 6d., hay and fodder 3 s.\
**Sum** 28s. 2d. Thence 15th. 22¾d.

Richard Aleinie had 1 beast 2s., 1 ox 6s., 1 cow 6s., 1 qr. wheat 40d.,\
1 qr. drage 2s. 6d., 1 qr. beans 2s. 6d., hay and fodder 12d.\
**Sum** 23s. 4d. Thence 15th. 18¾d.

John Taylour had 1 beast 2s., 1 ox 8s., 1 cow 4s., 1 qr. wheat 40d., 1 qr.
drage 2s. 6d., 1 qr. beans 2s. 6d., hay and fodder 12d.\
**Sum** 23s. 4d. Thence 15th. 18¾d.

**Sum** of the aforesaid village £22 12s. 8d.\
Thence 15th. £1 10s. 2½d.
