## Illmor Land tax

### Ashendon

### 1st Division

An a Sesment Mead the 4 Day of Jven 1782
oan the parish of Illmor In the Covntey
of Bux a fore Said By Thomas Kingham
a poainted a Sesor for the Said parish for
Raising the Sum of nintey povnds Charged
oan the Said parish By a Vartey of act of
parlement mead and pased In the 22 Year of the
Raing of over Sovering Lord Gorg the third
Intiteled an act for granteng Is maistey an
aid by a Land tax to be Raised in Great Briton
for the Sarves for for _[sic]_ the Year 1782 as followeth

| Occupier / Assessment Entry         |      £ : s : d |
| :---------------------------------- | -------------: |
| Thomas Kingham for Ser John Roaper  |    28 : 16 : 8 |
| John Kingham for Lord Cherstfeld    |     26 : 5 : 5 |
| Hanah Kingham for Lord Cherstfeld   |    19 : 10 : 0 |
| Saro timberlack for Jeams Wells     |     3 : 13 : 0 |
| _[v]_ Thomas Grvas for John Badrick |      1 : 7 : 7 |
| Joh Darvel for Lord Cherstefeld     |     8 : 5 : 10 |
| John Gomm for Lord Cherstefeld      |      2 : 1 : 6 |
| **Thomas Kingham a Ssesor**         | **90 : 0 : 0** |
