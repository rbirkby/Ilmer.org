source: Bucks Archives, PR_114/3/1 (a 1692 copy), D-X_706/19.

Ilmer in the Deanary of Waddesdon
in the County of Bucks upon the 25.
day of October 1607. & in the yeare of
the Reigne of our Gracious Soveraigne
Lord James by the Grace of God King
of England ffrance & Ireland the 5th
& of Scotland the One & fourtieth.

A Survey or Terrier of all the Possessions belonging to
the Vicaridge of Ilmer made & taken by the view ~
perambulation & Estimat of the Minister Church
Wardens & other Inhabitants there whose Names
are subscribed, being thereunto nominated & appointed
by William ffolkingham Gentleman, General Surveyour
of Church Glebe & Possessions within the Diocess of
Lincoln by virtue of a Commission decreed by the
Reverend ffather in God William Lord Bishop of Lincoln
in Execution of the Canon in that behalf established.

Imprimis The Home stall or scite of the Vicaridge, scituate
on the East part of the Church consisting of 4. Bayes
built all with wood & covered with Tyles. Two Bayes ~
thereof being chambered & boarded, disposed into foure
Roomes (viz) A Hall, a Kitchin, a Parlour & a low Chamber

A Little Close next the street. The Close called the Vicars Woodyard.
One stable. A little Close with an Apple Tree in
it, joyning to the Vicaridge House. All these containing by
Estimation One half Acre.

Item of Meadow Pasture & Arable Twenty Acres
of Glebe Lands. whereof One Acre is of Sum
Leas, the other of Meadow ground.

The other Eighteen Acres are of Arable Lands lying in the
ffeilds of Ilmer aforesaid which are well known to the
Parishioners as diverse of the same Parish which have had
lawfull occupation since the vicar that now is
bin instituted there can witness. (viz)

The Parties that have taken it to halves are these

James Wildgoose\
Thomas Walker\
James Hester\
John Robbins\
Edmund Brooke\
Stephen Dorrill &\
John Tripp. who now\
husband the same arable Land

Item there belongs unto the said Vicaridge all Tythes
whatsoever (except Tythe Corne) viz. Tythe Hay, Wooll
& Lamb, Calf, Milk & of all whatsoever doth grow out of
the Garden or Orch-yard, with Eggs, Piggs, Geese, Hemp
and Honey

Test

                      Martin Wright vicar\
                      Stephen Dorrill, John Tripp } Church Wardens
