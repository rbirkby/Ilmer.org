At a Meeting of the Land Owners and Tithe Owners within the parish of Illmire in the County of Buckingham duly convened and held at the Church in Illmire aforesaid on Monday the 17th day of September 1838 for the purpose of making an agreement for the general Commutation of Tithes within the limits of the said Parish pursuant to the Provisions of the Act of Parliament[^1] passed in the sixth and seventh years of the Reign of his late Majesty William the fourth entitled An Act for the Commutation of Tithes in England and Wales

[^1]: [Tithe Act, 1836](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/Will4/6-7/71/contents/enacted)

Land Owners and Tithe Owners present

Moreton John Edward Frewen Esquire by James Grace Gentleman his Attorney\
Vice Admiral Henry Raper Esquire by Joseph Gentleman his Attorney\
The Reverend William Edwards Partridge Clerk Vicar by the said Joseph Rose his Attorney\
John Tapping Trustee of Church Land

Mr Grace was elected Chairman who thereupon proceeded by means of the Poor Rate to ascertain the Interest of the Land Owners and Tithe Owners present in person or by Agent which amounted to the whole of such Interests respectively

Bucks Herald, 25th August 1838

The Notice for convening the Meeting was then produced signed by the said James Grace as Agent for the said Moreton John Edward Frewen which appeared to have been affixed by John Eggleton junr on the principal outer Door of the Church within the said Parish on the twenty second day of August last and three Newspapers were produced in which the said Notice was inserted Vizt The Bucks Herald of the twenty fifth day of August last and of the first and eighth days of September instant

Mr. Rose on behalf of the said William Edwards Partridge stated his proposals for the intended Commutation of the small Tithes and demanded the annual sum of One hundred and four pounds ten shillings by way of Rent Charge subject to Variation and the other provisions of the said Act as a Commutation for his Tithes being the Tithe of Hay and all the small Tithes of the said Parish of which sum six shillings should be considered as the apportioned sum on the Glebe Land of the said William Edwards Partridge as such Vicar within the said Parish

The Land owners present by Agents or in person took this proposal into consideration and resolved that it be accepted and that the said sum of One hundred and four pounds ten shillings be apportioned among the different Land Owners as follows vizt

At a Meeting of the Land Owners and Tithe Owners within the parish of Illmire in the County of Buckingham held by adjournment at the White Hart Inn in Aylesbury in the said County on Thursday the eighteenth day of October 1838 for the purpose of making an agreement for the general Commutation of Tithes within the limits of the said Parish pursuant to the Provisions of the Act of Parliament passed in the sixth and seventh years of the Reign of his late Majesty William the fourth entitled An Act for the Commutation of Tithes in England and Wales

Land Owners and Tithe Owners present

Moreton John Edward Frewen Esquire by James Grace Gentleman his Attorney
Vice Admiral Henry Raper Esquire by Joseph Rose Gentleman his Attorney
The Reverend William Edwards Partridge Clerk Vicar and Trustee of the Church Land by the said Joseph Rose his Attorney

Mr Grace was elected Chairman who thereupon proceeded by means of the Poor Rate to ascertain the Interest of the Land Owners and Tithe Owners present in person or by Agent which amounted to the whole of such interests respectively

Resolved that the said James Grace be appointed the Valuer for the purpose of making the apportionment of the Rent Charges agreed to at the original Meeting as required by the said Act and that he shall adopt the principles of such Agreement in making such apportionments.

Resolved that this Meeting be adjourned to Tuesday the eighth day of January next to be then held at this place at eleven o'Clock in the forenoon

James Grace Chairman

The Minutes of the last adjourned Meeting were read and confirmed

Mr Rose reported to the Meeting that he had sent to the Tithe Commissioners a Copy of the Draft Agreement adopted at the original Meeting in which various alterations had been made and others suggested by them and that in consequence he had had an interview with the Commissioners' Assistant at Somerset House and received several explanations in conformity with which he has redrawn the Agreement and at this Meeting the same ingrossed on parchment is duly executed by the Agents and parties now present

Resolved that Mr Rose be instructed to take measures for obtaining the necessary consent of the Earl of Chesterfield the Patron of the Vicarage of Illmire to the Agreement now executed and such further steps as may be necessary for procuring the confirmation thereof by the Tithe Commissioners

Resolved that Mr William Hussey of Hugendon Green near High Wycombe Bucks be employed to make the necessary plans with a reference of particulars of the Parish to accompany the instrument of apportionment to the Commissioners

Resolved that this meeting be adjourned to Thursday the eleventh day of April next to be then held at this place at eleven o'Clock in the forenoon

James Grace Chairman

Articles of Agreement for the commutation of Tithes of the parish of Illmire in the County of Bucks in pursuance of the Act for the commutation of Tithes in England and Wales made and executed at a meeting duly called and holden in the said parish & adjourned and holden by adjournment on the eighth day of January in the year of our Lord One thousand eight hundred & thirty nine By and Between the several persons Owners of land within the said parish by whom or by whose agents duly authorized in that behalf respectively these presents are executed and the interest of which Land of the said parish is the whole of the Lands Owners in the Lands therein subject to Tithes of the first part The Reverend William Edwards Partridge Vicar of the said parish and Owner of the Tithe of Hay and of all the small Tithes thereof of the second part and the several Landowners in the parish who are respectively Owners of the whole of the Great Tithes except Tithe of hay arising from their several lands within the said parish and whose respective portions of the sd Great Tithes are hereinafter set forth of the third part

It is hereby agreed That the annual sum of One hundred and four pounds ten shillings by way of rent charge (subject to the provisions of the said Act) shall be paid to the said William Edwards Partridge as Vicar of the said parish and to his Successors instead of the Tithe of Hay and the small Tithes of the Glebe the Rent Charge in lieu of which it is hereby agreed shall be fixed at the sum of six shillings and shall be apportioned exclusively upon the said Glebe Land and instead of all Moduses and Compositions real and prescriptive and customary payments payable to such Vicar in respect of all the Lands of the said parish or the produce thereof a summary description of which Lands is contained in the first Schedule here unto annexed.

And it is further agreed that the annual sum of Thirty nine pounds two shillings by way of Rent Charge subject to the provisions of the said Act shall be paid to Moreton John Edward Frewen of Chevely in the County of Cambridge Esquire and to his Heirs and Assigns instead of all the Great Tithes except Tithe of Hay arising from those Lands of the said parish which are specified in the Second Schedule hereunto annexed.

And it is further agreed that the annual sum of Thirty two pounds eight shillings by way of rent Charge subject to the provisions of the said Act shall be paid to Henry Raper Esquire a vice Admiral in Her Majesties Royal Navy instead of all the Great Tithes except the Tithe of Hay arising from those lands of the said parish which are specified in the Third Schedule here unto annexed.

And it is further agreed that the annual sum of ten shillings by way of rent charge subject to the provisions of the said Act shall be paid to the Vicar and Churchwarden of the said parish for the time being as Trustees of the Church Land instead of all the Great Tithes except Tithe of Hay arising from those Lands of the said parish which are specified in the Fourth Schedule hereunto annexed.

And it is further agreed that all the Lands included in this agreement shall be discharged from the payment of Tithes from and after the first day of January next preceding the confirmation of the Instrument of apportionment to be made in pursuance of this agreement.

In Testimony whereof the said parties to these presents or their respective Agents thereunto duly authorized in their names and on their behalf have to these presents subscribed and set their respective hands and seals at the said adjourned Meeting.

First Schedule to the above Articles of Agreemts

The whole Parish of Illmire aforesaid contains Six hundred and seventy acres two roods and thirty perches of land by estimation exclusive of Public Roads and Waste the whole of which are subject to the payment of Tithes.

The whole quantity of land subject to Tithes within the said parish which is cultivated solely as Arable Land is Two hundred and forty acres two roods and ten perches by estimation.

The whole quantity of land subject to Tithes within the said parish cultivated solely as Meadow or Pasture Land is Four hundred and twenty two acres one rood and nine perches by estimation

The whole quantity of Roads and Waste Land within the said parish is three acres two roods and thirty three perches by estimation and the Scites of Parish Cottages thirty six perches by estimation

The whole quantity of land subject to Tithes in the said parish occupied as Homesteads and not included in any of the above mentioned quantities is Six Acres three roods and eight perches by estimation.

The Glebe Lands of the said parish including the Church Yard which if not in the hands of the Owner or Owners would be subject to Tithes amount to One acre and three perches by estimation which belong to the said William Edwards Partridge as such Owner.

Second Schedule
specifying those Lands of the said parish of the Great Tithes of which except the Tithe of Hay Moreton John Edward Frewen Esquire is entitled

The several Farms Closes Lands and Gardens now in the respective occupations of John Eggleton Ann Wooster widow and Anne Gomme as Tenants of the said Moreton John Edward Frewen or their respective undertenants containing together by estimation Four hundred and thirty six acres one rood and thirty three perches

Third Schedule
specifying those Lands of the said parish to the Great Tithes of which except the Tithe of Hay Vice Admiral Henry Raper is entitled

The farm Closes Lands & Gardens now in the occupation of John Tapping (or his Undertenants) as Tenant of the said Henry Raper containing by estimation Two hundred and thirty one acres and four perches.

Fourth Schedule
specifying those Lands of the said parish to the Great Tithes of which except except _[sic]_ the Tithes of Hay the Vicar and Churchwardens of the said parish for the time being as Trustees of the Church Land are entitled

Land containing by estimation One acre three roods and thirty four perches now in the occupation of the said John Tapping.

**QUESTIONS.**

10\. State Quantity and Value of Glebe?

11\. Describe the Parish, and the quality of the Lands, the system of Farming, and whether the quantity of produce has been affected by any extraordinary instances of high or low Farming?

There is a tract of low Meadow Land part of which is good and part cold and inferior

The Gardens are not large but well cultivated there is a considerable quantity of Orchard Ground – and many Pigs and Calves are reared on the "skim milk".

**ANSWERS.**

_[10.]_ The present quantity of Glebe including the Churchyard is 1a . 3r . 0p – The house a small Cottage – the Annual value about £6 – the ancient Terriers describe other Glebe, all trace of which is lost.

_[11.]_ Illmire is a small Parish in the Vale of Aylesbury – a short distance from the foot of the Chiltern Hills – it is entirely on a substratum of Clay and quite level – The Village consists only of 4 Farm houses and a few Cottages

_The Arable Land_ is all very heavy working "Wheat and Bean" Land requiring great labour but growing good crops both as to quantity and quality when well farmed the system generally adopted being a 4 field

_The Grass Land_ is used principally in the Butter Dairy a very large quantity being made in the District[^3] for the London Market[^2] – there are some pastures exceedingly well adapted for this purpose – indeed the proportion of inferior Land is very small

[^2]: ['Aylesbury Butter' and the decline of the Bucks butter industry](https://www.bahs.org.uk/AGHR/PDFS/ARTICLES/35n1a5.pdf)

[^3]: [Board of Agriculture survey of Buckinghamshire, 1810](https://archive.org/details/bub_gb_mQjpGm3t6x8C/page/n337/mode/2up?q=butter)

**ANSWERS.**

_[19.]_ The Schedule is from a Map on a large scale of recent date – and is doubtless sufficiently accurate for the purpose

No moduses or exemptions are claimed

**QUESTIONS.**

20\. State your opinion as to the fairness of the Agreement; if any objections are made to the amount of Rent-charge, or to any proceeding relative thereto, state them, the Names and Interests of the objecting Parties, and your opinion thereon?

**ANSWERS.**

_[20.]_ There are no objectors—all the parties have signed the Agreement – it is no doubt sufficiently favourable to the Landowners as the Tithes would be of great value if strictly taken in kind from the large number of Cows milked in the Parish

I consider however that the Agreement has been made on fair principles and that the Rentcharge is equitable all things considered

As the Tithes of Corn ~~and Hay~~ belong to each Landowner – I have not thought it necessary to notice that Tithe at all the Rentcharge affecting no other partiess
