Longwick Cum Ilmer Parish meeting Held in Schoolroom Longwick March 20th 1956 at 7 40pm To Read & Confirm minutes of Last meetg any Other Business Present Councillor W J Harper Chairman D Claydon, E J Girling, O Tapping F M Walker, F Williams & The Clerk The minutes of the Last Parish meeting Where Read & Confirmd There being no other Parish Business Brought Forward The Chairmn Declared the meetg Closed. W J Harper March 20/57
