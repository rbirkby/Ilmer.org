[Margin: ANNUAL PARISH MEETING 1936] The Annual Parish Meeting was held in the Schoolroom, Longwick on Monday March 23rd 1936 at 7 pm. To read and confirm Minutes of last meeting. To consider any business which may be brought before the meeting. Present:- Messrs H. S. Pope (in the chair) A. J. Goodchild, W. J. Harper. H. Rogers. F. Williams and Robert Bailey (Clerk) Minutes of last meeting read and confirmed. There being no other business brought forward, the Chairman declared the meeting closed. Wm A Walker Feby 22nd 1937 [Stamp: DISTRICT AUDIT 1 5 MAY 1936]
