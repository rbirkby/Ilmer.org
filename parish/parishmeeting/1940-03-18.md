[Margin: ANNUAL PARISH MEETING 1940] Annual Parish Meeting held in the Schoolroom, Longwick, on Monday March 18th 1940 at 6.30 pm. To read and confirm Minutes of last meeting. To consider any Parish business which may be brought before the meeting. Present:- Messrs Wm A. Walker (Chairman) H. S. Pope, H. W. Lewis F. Williams, W. J. Harper. A. J. Goodchild, E. J. Girling and Robert Bailey (Clerk)

[Margin: Minutes] Minutes of last meeting read and confirmed.

[Margin: Dangerous Fence near Anderdon Farm and flooding at Bar Lane corner] It was agreed to draw the attention of the Divisional Highways Surveyor, to the dangerous condition of the Fence on the Roadside verge, near the Brook at Anderdon Farm, Longwick, also to the flooding which occurs at the corner of Bar Lane with the Longwick Road. There being no other business brought before the meeting, the Chairman declared the meeting closed. Wm A Walker
