Longwick Cum Ilmer Parish meetg Held in Schoolroom Longwick March 20th 1957 at 7 40pm To Read & Confirm minutes of Last meetg ay Other Business Present Councillor W J Harper Chairman, D Claydon E J Girling, O Tapping, F M Walker F Williams & The Clerk The minutes of the Previous Parish meetg Where Read & Confirmd no Other Parish Business Was Brought Forward. the Chairman Declared the meetg Closed 7-50 pm W J Harper March 26/58.
