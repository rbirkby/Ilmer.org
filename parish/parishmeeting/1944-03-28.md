[Margin: ANNUAL PARISH MEETING 1944] The Annual Parish Meeting was held in the Schoolroom, Longwick on Tuesday March 28th 1944 at 7.30 pm. To read and confirm Minutes of last meeting. Consideration of any Parish business which may be brought before the meeting. Present:- Messrs Wm A. Walker (Chairman) H. S. Pope. H. W Lewis, E. J. Girling, W. J. Harper. F. Williams, A. J. Goodchild and Robert Bailey (Clerk)

[Margin: Minutes] The Minutes of the last Annual Parish Meeting were read and confirmed. There being no other business brought before the meeting the Chairman declared the meeting closed. Wm A Walker.
