[Margin: ANNUAL PARISH MEETING 1939] Annual Parish Meeting held in the Schoolroom, Longwick on Monday March 27th 1939. at 7 pm. To read and confirm Minutes of last meeting. To consider any Parish business which may be laid before the meeting. Present:- Messrs Wm A. Walker (Chairman) H. S. Pope Albert. Jas Goodchild. Wm Joseph Harper, Fred Williams, Edward John Girling, Herbert. Wm Lewis and Robert Bailey (Clerk)

[Margin: Minutes] Minutes of last meeting read and confirmed. There being no other business brought before the meeting, the Chairman declared the meeting closed. Wm A Walker
