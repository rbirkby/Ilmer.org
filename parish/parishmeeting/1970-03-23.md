ANNUAL PARISH MEETING HELD IN LONGWICK VILLAGE HALL ON MONDAY 23RD MARCH 1970 at 7.30 p.m.

[Margin: Present.] Cllrs Brock, Walker, Underwood, Angus & Thomas. [Margin: Absent.] Apologies Cllrs Gomme & Tapping. [Margin: Minutes.] Minutes of previous meeting read and confirmed.

[Margin: Matters Arising. Proposed Electricity Sub. Station.] It was now known that the Electricity Sub. Station would not after all be at Longwick. A letter of thanks to Mr Rupert Lawrence for his advise and help in this matter was proposed by Cllr Walker.

[Margin: Wing Airport.] Although still in the balance, Foulness was now thought to be the probable site for the airport.

No Other Business.

Meeting closed 8.10 P.M. Dorothy Brock. 2/4/71.
