[Margin: ANNUAL PARISH MEETING 1941] The Annual Parish Meeting was held in the Schoolroom, Longwick on Monday March 24th 1941 at 6.30 pm. To read and confirm Minutes of last meeting. To consider any Parish business which may be brought before the meeting. Present:- Messrs Wm A. Walker (Chairman) H. S. Pope, F Williams, W. J. Harper. A. J. Goodchild, E. J. Girling and Robert Bailey (Clerk)

[Margin: Minutes] Minutes of last meeting read and confirmed. There being no other business brought before the meeting, the Chairman declared the meeting closed. Wm A Walker [Stamp: DISTRICT AUDIT]
