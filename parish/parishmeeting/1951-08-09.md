[Margin: PARISH MEETING.] A Parish meeting was summoned for eight o'clock in the Schoolroom Longwick on Thursday August 9th to present the Survey of the Parish Footpaths as drawn up by the Parish Council. All the members of the Parish Council, Councillors W. J. Harper, Chairman, D. Claydon, E. J. Girling; A. J. Goodchild, A. Ancliffe, Mrs D. M. Walker, F. Williams and the Clerk were present. In view of the fact that no Parishioners presented themselves at the meeting the Chairman declared the meeting closed at 8.10 p.m. W. J Harper March 20/52
