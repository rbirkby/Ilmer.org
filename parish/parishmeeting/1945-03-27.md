[Margin: ANNUAL PARISH MEETING 1945] The Annual Parish Meeting was held in the Schoolroom, Longwick on Tuesday March 27th 1945 at 7.30 pm. Present:- Messrs Wm A. Walker (Chairman) H. S. Pope E. J. Girling. W. J. Harper. F. Williams. A. J. Goodchild and Robert Bailey. (Clerk) The Minutes of the last meeting were read and confirmed. There being no other business brought before the meeting, the Chairman declared the meeting closed. Wm A Walker
