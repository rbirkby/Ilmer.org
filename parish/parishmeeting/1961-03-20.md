ANNUAL PARISH MEETING HELD IN THE SCHOOL ROOM LONGWICK MONDAY 20TH MARCH 1961 Present Councillors Harper, Claydon, Gomme Tappin and Mrs Brock Minutes The minutes of the last meeting were read and confirmed Meadle Seventeen parishioners from Meadle village attended the meeting to complain of caravans being parked in the village, and which were assumed that this may become permanent, Complaints were also made about the amount of rubbish and litter in and around Meadle. The clerk was instructed by the chairman to raise these points with Wycombe R.D.C. as a matter of urgency Meeting closed at 8.10 pm W J Harper 22/3/62
