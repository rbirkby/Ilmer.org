[Margin: ANNUAL PARISH MEETING 1942] The Annual Parish Meeting was held in the Schoolroom, Longwick, on Monday March 23rd 1942 at 7 pm. To read and confirm Minutes of last meeting. To consider any Parish business which may be brought before the meeting. Present:- Messrs Wm A. Walker (Chairman) F. Williams, W. J. Harper, A. J. Goodchild, and E. J. Girling and Robert Bailey (Clerk)

[Margin: Minutes] Minutes of the last annual Parish Meeting were read and confirmed. There being no other business brought before the meeting, the Chairman declared the meeting closed. H S Pope. 22/3/1943.
