[Margin: ANNUAL PARISH MEETING 1935] Annual Parish Meeting held in the Schoolroom, Longwick on Monday March 25th 1935 at 7 pm. To read and confirm Minutes of last meeting. To consider any business which may be brought forward. Present:- Messrs Wm A. Walker (in the chair) Edward J. Girling, Albert Jas Goodchild, Wm Joseph Harper, Henry Rogers, Herbert Sydney Pope Fred Williams & Robert Bailey (Clerk) Minutes of last meeting read and confirmed. There being no other business brought forward, the Chairman declared the meeting closed. H S Pope. Mch. 23rd 1936
