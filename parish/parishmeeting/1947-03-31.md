[Margin: ANNUAL PARISH MEETING 1947] The Annual Parish Meeting was held in the Village Hall, Longwick. on Monday March 31st 1947. at 7.45. pm. To read and confirm Minutes of last meeting. Consideration of any other business which may be brought before the meeting. Present:- Councillors F. Williams (Chairman) W. J. Harper, D. Claydon E. J. Girling. A. J. Goodchild. H. W Lewis and the Clerk. (Robert Bailey)

[Margin: Minutes] Minutes of last meeting were read and confirmed. There being no other business brought before the meeting, the Chairman declared the meeting closed. W J Harper 31/3/48.
