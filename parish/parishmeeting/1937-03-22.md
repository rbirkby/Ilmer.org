[Margin: ANNUAL PARISH MEETING 1937] Annual Parish Meeting held in the Schoolroom, Longwick on Monday March 22nd 1937 at 7 pm. To read and confirm Minutes of last meeting. To consider any business which may be brought before the meeting. Present:- Messrs Wm A. Walker. S. H. Pope, F. Williams, H. Rogers. A. J. Goodchild, W. J. Harper and Robert Bailey (Clerk).

The Minutes of Parish Meetings held on February 22nd and March 15th 1937 were read and confirmed. There being no other business brought before the meeting the Chairman declared the meeting closed. Mar 28th 1938 Wm A Walker [Stamp: DISTRICT AUDIT 301]
