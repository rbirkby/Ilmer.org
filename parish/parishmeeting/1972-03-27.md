ANNUAL PARISH MEETING HELD IN LONGWICK VILLAGE HALL ON MONDAY 27TH MARCH 1972 AT 7.30 P.M.

[Margin: Present] Cllr W. Underwood (in the chair) Cllrs B. Thomas, J. Walker, O. Tapping and F. Gomme. [Margin: Absent] Cllr D. Brock absent through illness. [Margin: Minutes] Minutes of previous meeting were read and confirmed.

[Margin: Playing Field Insurance] Although covered by the Municipal Insurance for liability to the public for accidents arising from use of the Playing Field, the council decided a notice should be dis-played to the effect that any equipment is used at the publics own risk.

[Margin: Green Lane at Owlswick.] Cllr Tapping remarked on the amount of rubbish in Green Lane at Owlswick a letter to Wycombe R.D.C. was asked for.

No Other Business

Meeting Closed 7.45 P.M. James W Walker.
