The Annual Parish Meeting Was held in the School Room Longwick on Wednesday May 5th 1954 at 7-30 pm To Read & Confirm minutes of Last Parish meeting To Consider any Parish Business brought Before the meeting. Present Councillor Harper Chairman Councillors D Claydon, F Williams, L W Claridge F M Walker, O Tapping & The Clerk.

[Margin: minutes] The minutes of the Last Parish meetg Where Read & Confirmed There being no other Business Brought before the meeting The Chairman Declared the meetg Closed W J Harper March 24/55
