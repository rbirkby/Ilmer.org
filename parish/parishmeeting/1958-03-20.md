Longwick Cum Ilmer Parish meeting Held in School Room Longwick March 20th 1958 . 7 40pm Present Councillors W J Harper Chairman, D Claydon L Claridge, O Tapping, Miss F M Walker, F Williams & The Clerk. The minutes of the Last Parish meeting Where Read & Confirmd no Other Parish Business Brought Forward the Chairman Closed the meety 7-50 pm W J Harper March 26 / -
