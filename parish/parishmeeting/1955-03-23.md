The Annual Parish meetg Was held in the School Room Longwick on Wednesday march 23rd 1955 To Read & Confirm minutes of Last Parish meetg To Consider any Parish business Brought before the meetg Present Councillor Harper Chairman Councillors L W Claridge, D Claydon, F M Walker, O Tapping F Williams & The Clerk.

[Margin: minutes] The minutes of the Last Parish meetg Where Read & Confirmed There being no other Business the Chairman Declared the meetg Closed. W J Harper March 20/56.
