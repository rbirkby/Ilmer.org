[Margin: ANNUAL PARISH MEETING 1943] The Annual Parish Meeting was held in the Schoolroom, Longwick on Monday March 22nd 1943 at 7.30. pm. To read and confirm Minutes of last meeting. Consideration of any Parish business which may be brought before the meeting. Present:- Messrs H. S. Pope, F. Williams, A. J. Goodchild. W. J. Harper and Robert Bailey (Clerk). Agreed that Mr H. S. Pope take the chair. Minutes of the last Annual Parish Meeting were read and confirmed. There being no other business brought before the meeting the Chairman declared the meeting closed. Wm A Walker
