Annual Parish Meeting held in Longwick Village Hall Wednesday 31st March 1965. at 7.30 p.m.

Present. In the chair Cllr Brock. Cllr Leuw Cllr Walker and Cllr Tapping. Absent. Apologies for absent Cllr Wootton & Cllr Gomme. Cllr Harper regrettable ill. Mins. Minutes of the previous meetings were read and confirmed. No other business. W J Harper March 28th/66.
