The Annual Parish meeting was held in Longwick C of E. Schoolroom on thursday February 26th 1953 To Read & Confirm minutes of the Last Parish meeting To Consider any Parish Business Brought Before the meeting Present Councillor Harper Chairman D Claydon, O. Tapping. E J. Girling F Williams & The Clerk.

The minutes of the Last Parish meeting Where Read & Confirmed. Where being no other Business Brought Before the meeting The Chairman Declared the meetg Closed. W J Harper 5/5/54
