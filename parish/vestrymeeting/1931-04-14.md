April 14. 1931. The annual combined meeting of the Vestry & Parochial Electors was held on this date. The Vicar stated that he was glad to say that Mr Albert Goodchild had consented to act again as his warden & Mr W.F. Harper was reelected Peoples warden. Mr Quint was reelected sidesman - subject to his acceptance. Mr Wood was elected as sidesman.

The statement of accounts for the year 1930 showed a balance of £2-18-8. On the proposition of the Vicar the organist's salary was raised from 12/- to 15/- a quarter.

This was all the business.

A M Berry. 5.4.32.
