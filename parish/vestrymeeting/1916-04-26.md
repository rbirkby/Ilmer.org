Vestry Meeting Apl 26th 1916. was held in the Parish Room at 6.30 pm. Present the Rev H.M. Gorham, Mr F. Kingham.

The Church warden accounts for the past year showed a total expenditure of £7.19.1 and the receipts £7.19.1 so that there is no balance, but the deficit has now been paid off. The sum of £1.12.11 on Whitsunday was given to S.P.G. and on 14/9 Harvest festival to Rl Bucks. Hospital, £1.12.2. The 22nd S after Trinity the Rev Dexter Allen preached for the S.P.G. (Borneo) and was given the offertory of 8/4.

Mr F.W. Kingham kindly consented to remain Church warden for the ensuing year.

There was no further business.

H.M. Gorham. April 9th 1917.
