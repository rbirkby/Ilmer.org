Vestry Meeting Apr 24 1914. Present the Rev H.M. Gorham, Mr F.W. Kingham, Miss Phillips.

The Church Warden presented his account which showed that £9.0.10 had been raised for church expenses during the past year, and that payments to the amount of £8.18.10 had been incurred, leaving a balance of 2/-.

The Rev H.M. Gorham proposed and Miss Phillips seconded that Mr F.W. Kingham be appointed Ch Warden for the ensuing year. Mr Kingham consented to act and there was no opposition, so he was elected.

There being no further business the meeting terminated.

H.M. Gorham.

_[Note at bottom left:]_ the 20/11 owing is paid off. there is still 16/ owing.
