A meeting of the Parochial Church Council was held on Sunday Oct. 27. 1929. The Vicar in the Chair, explained that his impending departure from the parish necessitated the appointment of a temporary Secretary & suggested to the meeting that they should appoint the Rev. A.M. Berry as temporary Secretary till he was appointed to the benefice. This was done & Mr Berry accepted office.

This was all the business.

A M Berry. 25.4.30.
