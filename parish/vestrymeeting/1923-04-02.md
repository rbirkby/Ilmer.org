Ilmer Parish 1923. The joint meeting of the Vestry & Parochial electors was held on Monday April 2nd/23 in the Church Hall. There were present: The Rev. W.M. Tuke, vicar, in the chair.

[Margin: Mr Fritz Kingham churchwarden; Mr Sidney Boswell; Mrs Boswell; Miss Richards]

The vicar nominated Mr Fritz Kingham as his churchwarden for the ensuing year.

Miss Richards moved & Mr Kingham seconded Mr Sidney Boswell as peoples warden. This was agreed to. Mr Boswell was unanimously elected Representative to the Ruri-decanal Conference.

It was resolved that all adult communicants of the Parish should be members of the P.C.C.

This was all the business.

Mr Boswell was elected Sec to the PCC

W.M. Tuke. 24.4.1924.
