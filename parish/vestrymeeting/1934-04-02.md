A vestry meeting was held in the Parish Room on April 2nd, 1934. Present the Rev E Horner, in the chair, Mr Harper, Churchwarden and clerk, Mrs West and Mrs Kingham.

The Church a/c for 1933 were read and approved. Mr Horner proposed Mr White as Vicar Warden. Mrs West proposed and Mrs Kingham seconded Mr Harper be Peoples warden and clerk for the ensuing year. It was agreed to give Mrs Kingham 10/- half yearly for looking after the stove.

Mr Horner proposed and Mrs West seconded that a Whist Drive be held at Long Wick on April 25th, proceeds for the Mothers Union and church expenses. This was agreed to.

That concluded the meeting.

W. O'Connor, Vicar. April 24, 1935.
