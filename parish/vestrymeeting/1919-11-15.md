Ilmer Parish. A Meeting of the Vestry was held in the Parish Room on the 15th of November, 1919, at 6 p.m. There were present - the Rev. W. O'Connor, Vicar, in the Chair; Mr. Fritz W. Kingham, Churchwarden; Mr. W. Claydon.

It was proposed by Mr. F.W. Kingham, seconded by Mr. W. Claydon, and passed:- "That whereas the Parishioners of Ilmer desire to place a Tablet in the Parish Church to the memory of those belonging to the Parish who have fallen in the late War, the Vestry hereby approve of the erection of the said Tablet, and desire to petition the Diocesan authorities for a Faculty authorising them to have the memorial erected in the Church".

W. O'Connor, Vicar, Chairman. April 9, 1920.
