Ilmer Parish 1926. The combined Easter Vestry meeting & meeting of Parochial Electors was, after due notice, held in the Parish Hall on Thursday April 8 at 6 p.m. There were present the vicar in the chair, Mr S.G. Boswell, churchwarden, Mr Boswell, Mr Fritz Kingham, Mr Wm Kingham, Mr Albert Goodchild.

The minutes of last meeting were read & approved. The churchwardens accounts were submitted. From which it appeared there was a balance to the good of 5/11 making with the balance of 1924 (3/4) a total balance of 9/3.

The vicar nominated Mr Fritz Kingham as his churchwarden for the ensuing year. Mr S.G. Boswell was unanimously elected peoples warden. All adult communicants were, as in former years, placed on the roll of the P.C.C. Number 15.

W.M. Tuke. Ap 19 1927. vicar.
