Vestry Meeting April 9th 1917 held in the Parish Room at 6.30 pm. Present Rev H.M. Gorham, Mr F.W. Kingham.

The minutes of the last vestry were read and confirmed and signed.

The Churchwarden account for the past year showed Receipts £7.12.3½ of which 11/1½ was sent from the Harvest Festival to the Rl Bucks Hospital, Aylesbury, and 5/- was paid to the Parochial Fund. Expenses amounted to £7.2.8½ leaving a balance of 9/7 to 1917-18.

The Rev H.M. Gorham nominated Mr F.W. Kingham his Church warden and he kindly consented to accept office.

W. O'Connor. April 6, 1918.
