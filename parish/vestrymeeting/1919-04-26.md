Ilmer Parish. The Easter Vestry Meeting was held at the Vicarage Cottage, Ilmer, on Saturday, the 26th of April, 1919, at 6.30, p.m. There were present - the Rev. W. O'Connor, Vicar, in the Chair, Mr. and Mrs. Fritz W. Kingham.

The Minutes of last Meeting (April 6, 1918) were read and signed.

The Churchwarden's account for Church Expenses were exhibited as follows:- Receipts, £6-4-8; Expenditure, - Cleaning &c. £3, Organist £2, Bell-ringer £1, Wine 4/-, Oil 2/-, Coal £1-1-11. Total expenditure, £7-7-11, showing a deficit of £1-3-3. Special Contributions amounted to £2-14-8.

W. O'Connor. Nov 15. 1919.
