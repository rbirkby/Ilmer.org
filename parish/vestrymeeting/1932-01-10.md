Jan. 10. 1932. A meeting was held in church on this date. The Vicar stated that according to notice the business was to elect Representatives. He stated that at the meeting at Horsenden held that morning, all the representatives had been re-elected, viz. On ruridecanal conference. Mr Jaques, Miss Gambell & As lay representative on R.D. Finance Bd. - Miss Gambell. As rep. on R.D. Bd of Missions - Miss Gambell.

The Vicar further stated that he hoped that Mr A. Goodchild would be reelected by the Ilmer electors, which they did unanimously. The Vicar stated that the balance on the accounts for 1931 would be about £4.

This was all the business.

A M Berry. Ap. 5. 32.
