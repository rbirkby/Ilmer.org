A church meeting was held in the Parish room on July 15th. Mr Pearce in the chair. The clerk reported that the two whist Drives showed a profit of £12.15s. It was proposed by Mrs Pursel and seconded by Mrs West that the church roof be repaired.

The clerk reported that the Electric Light Company were willing to put slot meters in the church and Parish Room under a minimum of 17/- guarantee from each place. The Church warden signed the guarantee and the meters were duly changed.

This concluded the meeting.

E W C Horner. 4th April 1934.
