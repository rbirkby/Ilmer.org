Ilmer Parish 1927. The combined Easter Vestry meeting & meeting of Parochial Electors was after due notice held in the Parish Hall on Tuesday April 19th 1927 at 6 p.m. Unfortunately there was a very small attendance. Mr & Mrs Fritz Kingham & Mr Charles Belgrove.

The vicar nominated Mr John Claydon Senr as his churchwarden. Mr Fritz Kingham was unanimously appointed peoples warden.

As in former years it was decided that all adult communicants should be members of the Parochial Church Council.

This was all the business.

W.M. Tuke, vicar. 12/4/28.
