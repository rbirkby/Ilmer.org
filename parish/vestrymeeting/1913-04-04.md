Vestry Meeting, Apl 4th 1913. Present the Rev H.M. Gorham, Mr F.W. Kingham, Miss A. Phillips.

Mr F. Kingham's accounts showed that £5.11.6 had been raised for church expenses and £6.7.6 had been paid leaving a deficit of 16/- due to the Churchwarden.

The Rev H.M. Gorham proposed and Miss Phillips seconded that Mr Kingham be appointed Church Warden for the ensuing year. Mr Kingham having kindly consented to hold the office and there being no opposition, that was carried.

There was no further business. The meeting then terminated.

H.M. Gorham.
