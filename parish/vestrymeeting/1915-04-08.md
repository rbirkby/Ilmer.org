Vestry Meeting 8th Ap 1915. Present Rev. H.M. Gorham, F.W. Kingham.

The Church warden a/c showed that £8.17.6 had been raised during the year and expenses came to £8.17.6 leaving 2/- balance the same as last year.

Mr Kingham consented to remain Church Warden for the ensuing year.

There was no further business.

H.M. Gorham. Ap 28 1916.

_[Note at bottom left:]_ There is still owing to Ch Warden £1.. Ap 8. 1915.
