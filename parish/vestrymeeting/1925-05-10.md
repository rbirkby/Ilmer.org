Ilmer Parish. A meeting of the Parochial Church Council was held in the Parish Hall on Sunday May 10th 1925 at 7 p.m. There were present the vicar in the chair, Mrs Fritz Kingham, Mrs W Kingham, Mrs Adams, Mrs Boswell, Mr Fritz Kingham, Mr Sydney G Boswell (Secretary).

Churchwardens accounts were presented & passed. The vicar produced vouchers for collections for special objects. It was resolved to spend part of Ilmer Close fund in having churchyard put in order. The vicar proposed & Mr Boswell seconded to ask Miss Gambell to play the harmonium in the mean while. The vicar reported that the piano had been tuned.

...funds had been passed by the Charity Commissioners.

This was all the business.

W.M. Tuke. 8.4.26.
