PRESENT: Cllr Mrs V McPherson (Chairman), Cllrs I Walker, G Walters, Mrs J Rogers and B Richards, Mrs S Griffiths (Clerk), County Cllr B Bendyshe-Brown and Wycombe District Councillor Steve Lacey.

No apologies were received. Cllr Valerie McPherson was re-elected Chairman (proposed Jane Rogers, seconded Graham Walters) and Cllr Brian Richards Vice Chairman. Representations: NW Chilterns Local Area Forum – Cllr Jane Rogers; WDALC – Cllr Ian Walker; Allotment Charities – Cllr J Rogers.

Matters arising: a list of landowners with riparian ditch responsibilities has been compiled; BCC recommended the Parish Council supply addresses for the County to contact directly rather than approach landowners itself, given SPS/DEFRA and Environment Agency considerations. A £120 quotation to mend the play area gate post hinge and a £105 quotation to hire ditch-clearing machinery for the Playing Field were both approved. IW/SG. The Playing Field weed treatment had been applied as requested.

FINANCE: Routine payments including Clerk's salary, internal audit fee, grass cutting, a newsletter print run, the 2014/2015 Devolved Services payment (£2,400) and BMKALC subscription were examined and signed off. Bank Balance £16,538.19 after the WDC precept and a further £2,400 devolved services credit.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No issues in the April Risk Inspections. Cllr Graham Walters agreed to source a hedge-cutting quotation. GW. Agreed to allow laminated dog-waste signs requested by a resident.

ROADS: The Clerk to report a graffitied bridge to Network Rail and chase replacement of two fallen Thame Road signs. SG. Following flooding of neighbouring properties last winter, the Council asked BCC to clear the section of ditch forming the northern boundary of Longwick School once the Playing Field ditch was cleared. SG

CORRESPONDENCE: Agreed to a £64 RoSPA playground inspection and to accept a resident's offer to paint the War Memorial railings if the Council supplies paint. Agreed to register for notification of common land declarations under the Commons Act 2006. Cllr Brian Richards agreed to attend the (postponed) Community Covenant signing for Wycombe District on 23rd June. BR

12/ FILLING THE VACANCY FOR A COUNCILLOR BY CO-OPTION

Cllr Graham Walters proposed co-opting Mrs Sally Whitworth to fill the vacancy following George O'Neill's retirement. Seconded by Cllr Jane Rogers. Passed 4 votes to 1 abstention. SG

13-14/ INTERNAL AUDIT AND ACCOUNTS FOR 2013/2014

The Internal Auditor confirmed the 2013/2014 audit was in order. All present agreed to approve the accounts and Annual Governance Statement for 2013/2014.

15/ UPDATING THE PARISH COUNCIL WEBSITE

Agreed to contact the Webmaster to request a free website makeover. SG

16-17/ ILMER VILLAGE GREEN AND THE NOTICE BOARD

Cllr Graham Walters confirmed the Ilmer Village Green land is registered with BCC's Commons Registration Dept. There were no imminent plans requiring the notice board to be resited.

18-19/ FOOTWAY LIGHTING RESPONSIBILITY

Cllr Brian Richards researched the Council's minutes and found that in 1984, following a fatal accident, the Council had installed a sodium lamp outside the Village Hall (precepted for until 1989); no record survived of ongoing utility payments, but BCC had last repaired it in 2009. All present agreed the Council should precept for replacement bulbs for this lamp only. SG. Separately, having traced footway lighting references back to 1983 (including the Centenary Cottages development and various developer-installed lights), Cllr Richards proposed the Council accept no responsibility for lighting elsewhere in the parish. All present agreed.

20/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

New applications with no objections: 14/06271/FUL (Winton House, Thame Road — change of use to office, alternative scheme); 14/06140/FUL (Mistlethrush Barn, Rose Farm — detached garage); 14/06139/FUL (The Cartshed, Rose Farm — detached garage). No comments: 14/06112/FUL (OS Parcel 9547, Owlswick — barn conversion, alternative scheme to 13/05291/FUL).

Approved: 14/05785/FUL (Noahs Ark, Thame Road); 14/05276/FUL (Sheredon, Thame Road); 14/05370/FUL (13 Bell Crescent).

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 17th June 2014 at 8.00pm. The meeting closed at 10.05pm.
