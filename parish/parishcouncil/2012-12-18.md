Cllr G Harper thanked Cllr G O'Neill for organising the school Carol Service and Mrs J Rogers for providing the refreshments at the service.

6. CAPITAL ITEMS AND COMMUNITY FACILITIES

A. Playing Field. Cllr G Harper confirmed that the three trees for the playing field had been purchased as agreed in the October minutes and planted. Cllr I Walker confirmed that he had purchased the laurel hedging, which had also been planted. The Clerk is requested to contact the police concerning: shoes being placed in a tree in the playing field; an abandoned car in the Village Hall car park; inconsiderate parking on the corner of the road leading to the Village Hall and the Thame Road. Cllr G Harper confirmed that he had actioned points raised in the November Risk Assessment inspection report: the seat on the aerial runway, reported as a low risk for wear and tear, has been repaired; the broken light cover on the fence, also low risk, is to be replaced. The Clerk is requested to obtain a quotation to repair the surface where the paviors are sinking and may cause a very minor trip hazard.

B. Children's Play Area. The Clerk is requested to obtain the prices of a Wendy house for the play area. Cllr G Harper confirmed that there were no issues highlighted for the Children's Play Area.

7. ROADS, VERGES AND RIGHTS OF WAY

Cllr J Rogers informed the members that she had received a request for the Parish Council to support a farmer who had a complaint made against him accusing him of planting a crop over footpath 17; Cllr J Rogers confirmed this was not the case and that grass had in fact been laid. Cllr G Harper agreed to investigate the allegation and report back at the next meeting. The Clerk is requested to contact B.C.C. concerning Bridleway 5, which needs resurfacing from the end of Walnut Tree Lane to the Railway Bridge. The Clerk is requested to obtain a progress report on the replacement sign post with only one sign, which should have three, previously reported under reference number 341490.

8. CORRESPONDENCE

Quotation for River Banks works on Field. This quotation was considered to be too expensive.

Quotation for Tree works on the River Bank. Cllr G Harper agreed to obtain an alternative quotation to cut down the willow to knuckle and remove the bow closest to the path.

A copy of the revised WDC Planning Enforcement Statement of Service Provision. Noted.

Notification of the death of a resident who was an active volunteer in the community. All present agreed to send a letter of condolence to his widow.

E-mail from NASLC re the localisation of Council Tax. E-mail regarding the Government's response to its recent consultation on the localisation of council tax support and the impact this has on local (parish and town) councils. Noted.

10. WHETHER TO AGREE TO WYCOMBE DISTRICT COUNCIL'S REQUEST FOR THE PARISH COUNCIL TO MAINTAIN THE GREEN SPACE IN WALKERS ROAD IN EXCHANGE FOR THEIR CONSIDERATION OF THE PARISH COUNCIL'S PROPOSAL FOR THE GREEN SPACE ON THE THAME ROAD

All present agreed to respond that the Parish Council is not prepared to maintain both open spaces, as there is a concern over the costs involved for a small Parish Council. The Clerk is requested to obtain some indications as to the cost of legal fees in preparing a lease agreement between the Parish Council and District Council.

11. A RESPONSE TO THE CONSULTATION ON WYCOMBE DISTRICT COUNCIL'S REVIEW OF THE LOCAL PLAN

All present agreed to defer this agenda item until the next meeting, as comments are not required until the 1st February 2013.

12. AN UPDATE ON THE PARISH EMERGENCY PLAN

All present agreed to defer this agenda item until the next meeting.
