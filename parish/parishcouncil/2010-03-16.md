PRESENT: Cllr G Harper (Chairman), Cllrs I Walker, Mrs V McPherson, Mrs J Rogers and G O'Neill, Mrs S Griffiths (Clerk).

Apologies were received and accepted from Cllrs Mrs P Priestley and Mrs D Brock. The Minutes of the previous meeting were approved and signed. None declared an interest.

Matters arising: registration of the 1983 Williams playing field land gift with the Land Registry was complete. The Development Control officer confirmed building work under 08/07382/FUL complied with permission. The 7th March litter pick was well attended and successful, with thanks to the Beavers and Cubs; Cllr Rogers to write a website article. JR

FINANCE: Payments for dog waste/litter collection (£213.85), risk assessment, the Clerk's quarterly fee and expenses (£1,221.13), and the internal audit fee. Bank Balance £12,138.08.

CAPITAL ITEMS: Declined the Sports Club's request to apply weed killer biannually, keeping to the existing three-year programme; a £234 quotation from Manor Estates for the current round was accepted. SG. The zip wire had been ordered for April installation, with a site meeting to agree its location. SG. No issues in the Playing Field Risk Inspection. In the play area, sinking entrance paviors (medium risk) were to be corrected by K Moore; a missing bench slat (low risk) would be repaired within the month. SG/GON

ROADS: The Clerk to raise knocked Bar Lane directional signs and blocked drains (already reported under ref 274256), a missing 30mph sign outside the Red Lion, and a reversed 50mph sign on the B4009 near Pettigroves. SG. Cllrs Harper, Walker and O'Neill agreed to assess the playing-field-to-school path together. GH/GON/IW

CORRESPONDENCE: A BCC Draft Parking and Enforcement Policy consultation was noted.

REPORTS: Cllr O'Neill reported the Council's bid for a Vehicle Activated Sign on the Thame Road had succeeded, for installation next financial year; the proposed Travelling Showmen site in Longwick had been dropped from WDC's list following Local Area Forum discussions. The Neighbourhood Action Group reported a fire engine access incident on Dorrells Road/Walnut Tree Road highlighting ongoing school-parking problems, and two reported crimes (a service-station non-payment and a Meadle greenhouse theft).

10/ REPAINTING THE PARISH SEATS

Following consideration of quotations, agreed to instruct K Moore. SG

11/ INSURANCE COVER REVIEW

Agreed the level of cover was adequate.

12/ PLAYING FIELD GRASS CUTTING CONTRACT 2010/2011

Accepted Manor Estates' quotation of £2,791.23. SG

13/ INTERNAL AUDITOR APPOINTMENT

Mr G Weir appointed as internal auditor for the 2009/2010 return.

14/ GYPSY, TRAVELLER AND TRAVELLING SHOWMEN SITES

Following a resident's request for continued vigilance, Cllr Rogers agreed to write a newsletter article on WDC's decision to remove the proposed Longwick site. JR

15/ PARISH FOOTPATH SURVEYS

Agreed to complete the Rights of Way department's survey forms. SG

16/ BCC WINTER MAINTENANCE CONSULTATION

Agreed councillors would submit individual comments for the Clerk to collate into a response.

17/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

New applications, all with no objections: 10/05147/FUL (2 Jubilee Cottages — rear extension, garage demolition); 10/05288/CTRE (Horsenden Manor — extensive tree works); 10/05335/FUL (Meadowsweet Barn, Stockwell Lane — front porch). Approved: 09/07444/FUL (Hatchmead Farm).

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 20th April 2010 at 8.00pm, preceded by the Annual Parish Meeting at 7.30pm. The meeting closed at 9.10pm.
