PRESENT: Cllr Brian Richards (Chair for the meeting), Cllrs Ian Walker, Jane Rogers and Richard Myers, Clerk Frank Post.

Apologies were received from Cllrs McPherson, van Apeldoorn, Whitworth and County Cllr Bendyshe-Brown; none received from District Cllr Harriss. The Minutes of 15th May 2018 were approved. Cllr Rogers declared an interest regarding Owlswick Green and the former Shoulder of Mutton.

Matters arising: bank statements requested from the Clerk on 27th April remained outstanding. FP. An alternative VAS project quote had been received from Elancity. FP/RM. VAS electricity costs awaited agreed locations. Noticeboard planning approval had been submitted 5th May. FP. The CIL account paperwork still required one signatory. FP/VMc

5/ OWLSWICK GREEN

Discussed in depth the proposal for the Parish Council to accept ownership of the green as a community asset. Cllr Walker raised concerns about parishioner involvement in grass cutting, though Cllr Rogers clarified residents would outsource this at their own cost; Cllr Myers noted any legal costs should fall to the developer. Agreed to contact the developers to discuss further. FP

6/ CIL WORKING GROUP REPORT

Confirmed £70,000 had been received into the Parish Council account; the Clerk to contact WDC's Gerard Coles on further monies. A shortlist of projects would be reviewed after the meeting with BCC's Sian Thomas. FP/VMc

7/ COUNTY COUNCILLOR REPORT

In Cllr Bendyshe-Brown's absence, the Clerk reported: the Village Hall sign was in place; BCC would continue cutting vegetation on the Village Hall-to-school pathway; TfB would liaise with Croudace on Bar Lane flooding; and both District and County councillors were pressing for an alternative site entrance to the Ilmer Road issue.

8/ PLAYING FIELD HEDGING (BELLWAY DEVELOPMENT)

A meeting with Bellway's area manager was being arranged (Cllrs McPherson and Myers), with Village Hall Committee input agreed as appropriate. FP/VMc. Cllr Richards raised concerns about the absence of ecological/bird surveys and unclear boundary ownership proof. Corrective action was agreed on the residual impact of UK Power Network activity on the playing field. FP

9/ PAYE OUTSOURCING

Agreed to outsource PAYE to PB Financial Solutions per Cllr McPherson's circulated proposal. FP

FINANCE: Payments including the Clerk's salary, grass cutting, admin stationery/postage, home allowance, risk assessment, milestone signwriting (£220), hall hire and playing field electricity. Total £848.46.

11/ RISK ASSESSMENTS

Playing field and children's area assessments received, with no medium or high risks identified. FP

12/ WELCOME PACKS

Agreed to continue with Local Authority Publishing. JR/SW

14/ PLANNING

Objected strongly: 18/06303/FUL (change of use of land to a private gypsy site); the Clerk to draft a response. No objection, but suggesting hedgerow replacement of the fence: 18/06169/FUL (conversion of a 7-bed dwelling to two semis). No objection: 18/06277/FUL (pub conversion to a 6-bed dwelling plus 5 new houses); 18/06164/FUL (pub conversion to café, 5-bed dwelling plus 5 new houses, café viability queried); 18/06315/FUL (two-storey rear extension); 18/06568/CLP (new windows).

16/ ENFORCEMENT

An update was given on enforcement issues regarding Bar Lane and Footpath 34.

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 17th July 2018 at 7.30pm. The meeting closed at 9.10pm.
