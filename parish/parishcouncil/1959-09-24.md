LONGWICK-CUM-ILMER PARISH COUNCIL meeting Held 7-30 pm 24th Sept Schoolroom Longwick

[Margin: Present] Councillors Harper, Walker, Mrs Huntley Girling Tappin Williams

[Margin: Mins] Min of last meeting were read and confirmed

[Margin: Monks Risboro Charities] It was unanimously decided that Councillors Girling and Tappin should represent the Council on this Charity and the clerk was instructed accordingly

[Margin: P Risboro Charities] The question of Councillor Mrs Huntley continuing to represent the Council on this Charity was discussed and left in abeyance.

[Margin: Footways] The clerk was instructed to remind the Div Surveyor that the pavement between Bar Lane and Walkers Yard was still subject to splashes from passing cars after rain. The need for a footpath between the Red Lion and Duke of Wellington should also be stressed again

Meeting closed at 8.20 pm W J Harper 6/11/59
