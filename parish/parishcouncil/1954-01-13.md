meeting of Longwick Cum Ilmer Parish Council Held in School Room Longwick on Jan 13th 1954 To Read & Confirm minutes of Last meeting matters arising, & Objection of Transport Executive To Footpath 12A & To Any Other Business Councillors Present W. Harper Chairman, D Claydon F Williams & the Clerk. apologies Where Received From L Claridge & O Tapping.

[Margin: minutes] The minutes of the Last meeting Where Read & Confirmed

[Margin: matters arising] Pro C F Williams Sec D Claydon this matter Re Water Supply Ilmer be left until the next meeting owing to the small number of Councillors being Present Carried

[Margin: Correspondence] It Was agreed that the whole of the Correspondence Re Objections be held Over until the next meeting to be held on Jan 20th 1954 - at 7-30 Pm

[Margin: Accounts] Pro C F Williams Sec D Claydon The Following account be Paid, Clerks Salary Due Dec 24 1953 £2. 10. 0 Carried W J Harper Jan 20/54
