1/ PRESENT

Cllr G Harper (Chairman)

APOLOGIES FOR ABSENCE

Apologies were received and accepted from Cllr I Walker

2/ MINUTES

The Minutes of the previous meetings of the Parish Council, having been circulated were approved by the Meeting and signed by the Chairman.

3/ DECLARATIONS OF INTEREST

Members were asked to declare any personal interest and the nature of that interest which they may have in any of the items under consideration at this meeting.

Cllrs Mrs P Priestley declared an interest in agenda item 15 - Planning Applications

4/ MATTERS ARISING NOT OTHERWISE ON THE AGENDA

a) Extending the existing skateboard facilities

The Clerk informed the members that WDC were unable to help on this occasion and that it would be more beneficial for the Parish Council to obtain quotations direct from the supplier. The Clerk has requested three quotations for an additional concrete half pipe.

b) Walnut Tree Lane footpath extension

Cllr G Harper has spoken to the resident as agreed in the last meeting and was informed that there was a delay due to drainage being installed. Cllr Harper was assured that the work would be completed before the winter.

Receipts

The income and expenditure for August was examined by the Council. All present agreed that the necessary cheque be signed.

6/CAPITAL ITEMS AND COMMUNITY FACILITIES

A. Playing Field

Cllr G Harper suggested that the Parish Council should start the preparation for the Estimates meeting in November by obtaining quotations for new pieces of play equipment that might be considered for the coming financial year. All present agreed. The Clerk is requested to obtain 3 quotations for a Zip Wire and also a replacement Climbing Frame for the children's play area. SG

B. Children's Play Area

The part to repair the elephant springer is now quite rare, but Safe and Sound will be able to get hold of it. However, it will take a couple weeks to source and receive.

8/ CORRESPONDENCE:

To be discussed

A request for the use of the Playing Field next year to erect a small marquee for a wedding reception being held in the Village Hall. All present agreed providing that the resident contacts the Sports Club to liaise concerning cricket fixtures.

Items of general interest on table.

9/ REPORTS

School Governors

Nothing to report

Local Community Partnership

Nothing to report. - The next meeting is on September 23rd at 7.00pm in Longwick Village Hall.

WDALC

Nothing to report. - The next meeting is on September 3rd

Neighbourhood Action Group

PCSO Lisa Roberts sent her apologies for not being able to attend the Parish meeting due to staff shortages because of the holiday period. The police have been running a successful Speedwatch campaign in the Longwick –cum-Ilmer Parish. Eight motorists have been caught speeding.

PCSO Roberts sent a short crime report stating that there had been 7 crimes in the parish since the first of July; Six concerning non payment at the petrol station and one domestic situation.

PC M Logan has joined the Neighbourhood team replacing PC A Ralph.

The top three priorities of the Outer Risborough N.A.G are speeding, parking and anti-social behaviour.

Newsletter

Cllr Mrs V McPherson has been contacting various organisations and long term residents for articles for the next newsletter. The deadline for receiving theses articles is the 17th September 2009.

Cllr Mrs V McPherson informed the members that the existing printer the newsletter had been using was retiring in August and that she had recommended a replacement printer. Cllr Mrs V McPherson had obtained a quotation from the new printer of £295.00 for 600 copies. All present agreed to accept this quotation. VM

10/ A REPORT FROM THE WEBSITE WORKING GROUP

Cllrs Mrs J Rogers and Mrs V McPherson confirmed that the website is now up and running. The Clerk is requested to obtain the number of hits on the website.

Cllr C Harper requested that the appreciation of the Parish Council for the hard work of councillors Mrs J Rogers and Mrs V McPherson is recorded.

11/ CONFIRMATION OF THE CLERKS DECLARATION OF EARNINGS FOR 2008/2009 IN ORDER THAT THE COUNCIL DOES NOT FACE A POTENTIAL SURCHARGE BY HMRC IN THE EVENT THAT THE EARNINGS WERE NOT DECLARED

To be deferred until the next meeting.

12/ ADOPTION OF A DEED OF DEDICATION TO PROTECT THE RECREATIONAL GROUND WITHIN THE VILLAGE

All present agreed not to adopt a deed of dedication at the present time, but that it should be reviewed in the future if the present circumstances changed.

A RESPONSE TO THE WYCOMBE DISTRICT COUNCIL IMAGINE THE FUTURE 5 - THE CONSULTATION ON DELIVERY AND SITE ALLOCATIONS DEVELOPMENT PLAN DOCUMENT.

The Clerk is requested to formulate a response objecting to the proposed development north of Longwick and the Park Mill Farm development. The members are unhappy with any development that would blur the separation of Longwick from Princes Risborough. SG
