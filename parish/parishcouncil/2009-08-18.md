PRESENT: Cllr G Harper (Chairman), Cllrs Mrs J Rogers, Mrs V McPherson, Mrs D Brock, Mrs P Priestley and G O'Neill, Mrs S Griffiths (Clerk).

Apologies were received and accepted from Cllr I Walker. The Minutes of the previous meeting were approved and signed. Cllr Mrs P Priestley declared an interest in Planning Applications.

Matters arising: WDC could not assist with the skate park half-pipe extension, so the Clerk sought three quotations direct from suppliers. The Walnut Tree Lane footpath extension was delayed by drainage works but assured to complete before winter.

FINANCE: Grass cutting payment of £380.81. Bank Balance £12,739.96. The Clerk confirmed the accounts and bank reconciliation were satisfactory.

CAPITAL ITEMS: Agreed to begin preparing for the November Estimates meeting by obtaining quotations for a Zip Wire and replacement Climbing Frame. SG. The rare replacement part for the elephant springer was being sourced by Safe and Sound.

ROADS: The Clerk to raise: a fir tree dumped on Bridlepath 14; a damaged road sign on Lower Icknield Way; a leaning black-and-white post outside Restawhile, Owlswick; and two similar posts on Ilmer Lane near the brook, on a stretch that is dangerous when icy. SG

CORRESPONDENCE: Agreed to allow a small marquee on the Playing Field next year for a wedding reception, subject to liaison with the Sports Club over cricket fixtures.

REPORTS: The Neighbourhood Action Group reported a successful Speedwatch campaign (8 motorists caught) and 7 crimes since 1st July, mostly petrol non-payment; PC M Logan had joined the Neighbourhood team. Cllr McPherson obtained a new newsletter printer's quotation of £295 for 600 copies, accepted. VM

10/ WEBSITE WORKING GROUP

The website was confirmed live; the Clerk to obtain hit numbers. The Council recorded its appreciation for Cllrs Rogers and McPherson's work on it.

11/ CLERK'S DECLARATION OF EARNINGS 2008/2009

Deferred to the next meeting.

12/ DEED OF DEDICATION FOR THE RECREATION GROUND

Agreed not to adopt a deed of dedication at present, to be reviewed if circumstances change.

13/ RESPONSE TO THE WDC IMAGINE THE FUTURE 5 CONSULTATION

The Clerk was asked to formulate an objection to the proposed development north of Longwick and at Park Mill Farm, as members were unhappy with anything blurring the separation of Longwick from Princes Risborough. SG

14/ PARISH RECORDS ARCHIVING

Cllrs Harper and Rogers confirmed a substantial run of parish records — including Declarations of Acceptance of Office (1934–1984), Annual Parish Meeting Minutes (1934–1998), Minute Books (1934–1990), correspondence, the Parish of Ilmer Minute Book (1894–1934) and early Ilmer parish bank books — had been archived at County Hall, along with a DVD donated by Alan Bairstowe. The Council thanked Alan Bairstowe by letter and thanked Cllrs Harper and Rogers for their work sorting the records. The Clerk to check whether the Playing Field is owned by or held in trust by the Parish Council. SG

15/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

New applications: 09/06533/FUL (Horsenden Manor — storage building) — no objections. 09/06518/FUL (Land off Stockwell Lane, Little Meadle — new access/gate) — recommended refusal for lack of visibility on exit, with conditions suggested if approved regardless. 09/06547/LBC and 09/06546/FUL (The Corn Barn, Longwick Mill — side extension) — response deferred pending a site meeting. Approved: 09/06174/FUL (Total Service Station) and 09/06046/FUL (Land Adjacent Hazel Trees).

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 15th September 2009. The meeting closed at 9.25pm.
