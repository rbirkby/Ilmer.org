Meeting of the Longwick-cum-Ilmer Parish Council, held in the Schoolroom, Longwick, on Monday February 1st 1937 at 7 pm. To read and confirm Minutes of last meeting. To appoint a representative to serve on the Princes Risborough Sub-Area Committee for Air Raid Precautions. Nomination of Parish Constables. Payment of accounts. Present:- Councillors Wm A. Walker (Chairman), H. S. Pope. W. J. Harper, F. Williams and Mr Robert Bailey (Clerk).

[Margin: Minutes] Minutes of last meeting read and confirmed.

[Margin: Apology for absence] An apology for absence was received from Councillor H. Rogers.

[Margin: Electricity Charges Meadle] The Aylesbury Borough Electrical Engineer wrote with reference to Electricity charges for supply of current to the Bungalows of Messrs E. T. Rutland and A. Keen at Meadle, that he had gone thoroughly into the matter of carrying the supply to the two premises and found that the erection of an extension of approximately 200 yards will be necessitated before a satisfactory supply can be given. As some capital expenditure would be involved in order to carry out the work, he was obliged to ask the consumers at the two premises each to pay the sum of £5 cash and each to guarantee to consume or pay for a minimum of £5 worth of electricity per annum for a period of five years. He was writing to Messrs Keen and Rutland informing them of the position as outlined above. The Clerk reported he had received no further communication from Messrs Keen and Rutland on the matter. It was therefore agreed that any further action by the Council, should stand over for the time being.

[Margin: Land for Council Houses] The Chairman reported that the Wycombe R.D. Council had decided to proceed with the purchase of land for the erection of additional Council Houses at Longwick. He proposed to recommend to the Council to consider the

erection of four houses of the usual type together with two of a smaller type for aged persons.

[Margin: Stiles Ilmer] As no information was before the meeting as to whether the Stiles referred to at the last meeting had been erected at Ilmer the matter was left over for enquiries.

[Margin: Parish Meeting re Coronation] The Clerk was authorised to have 25 Posters printed, arranging for a Parish Meeting to be held in the Village Hall, Longwick on Monday February 22nd 1937 at 7.30 pm to consider what steps shall be taken to celebrate the Coronation of Their Majesties King George VI. and Queen Elizabeth.

[Margin: Representative on Air Raid Precautions Sub-Committee] Proposed by Councillor Harper seconded by Councillor Pope that Councillor Wm A. Walker be appointed as representative to serve on the Princes Risborough Sub-Area Committee for Air Raid Precautions. Carried

[Margin: Parish Constables] Proposed by Councillor Pope seconded by Councillor Harper that the following Persons be nominated to act as Parish Constables for the ensuing year:- Messrs Owen Goodchild, Fred Williams, Harry Goodchild, William Alfred Richardson, Charles Tucker and Ernest Thomas Rutland. Carried

[Margin: Payment of accounts] Proposed by Councillor Williams seconded by Councillor Pope, that Cheques be drawn in payment of the following accounts:- Commissioners of Inland Revenue. Sch. A. Tax on Parish Cottages 1936/7 14/3d. Robert Bailey, One Quarters Salary as Clerk to 25/12/36 £1/5/- Carried Mar 22nd 1937 Wm A Walker
