PRESENT: Cllr Mrs Valerie McPherson BEM (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Jane Rogers, Sally Whitworth, Rolf van Apeldoorn and Brian Richards, Clerk Susanne Griffiths, Clerical Assistant Jayne Mylchreest, 6 members of the public.

No apologies were received. The Minutes of 21st June 2016 were approved and signed. Cllr Rogers declared an interest in agenda item 17 (Malbaret).

Matters arising: the Yew Tree Cottage resident having objected to siting the noticeboards nearby, agreed instead to site them on the playing field opposite the Scout Hut entrance, subject to a planning-permission check. JM

Cllr Bendyshe-Brown (BCC) reported on the Wellington House development; the Thame Road VAS now working from the Thame direction; a meeting on two further VAS locations; completed footpath/pavement repairs near the school and at Briants; and an awaited response on re-siting the Village Hall directional sign.

FINANCE: Payments including salaries, pension, grass cutting, risk assessment, Neighbourhood Plan professional fees and mileage (Thomas Regeneration, £608.70), dog waste collection, the WDC annual play area inspection, and annual website hosting/Plan updates (£900). Bank Balance £26,729.24.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No issues in the June Risk Inspection Reports; a low-risk skateboard ramp crack was noted, with three repair quotations to be obtained. JM. Agreed to retain the old play area gate as a spare, deferring any repair decision to the estimates meeting.

CORRESPONDENCE: Cllr Rogers highlighted a proposed Aylesbury Vale Local Plan site (Site 6) near the parish boundary and urged member responses; the South Oxfordshire Design Guide and South & Vale Local Plan 2032 consultations were noted for comment. Resident emails on the Rose Farm application were noted, with residents to be informed of the Council's Neighbourhood Plan intentions. JM. A BMKALC unitary authority survey was noted for individual councillor response. A dog-fouling complaint linked to the play area gate was resolved, the gate's closing mechanism having been repaired. JM

9/ LOCAL AREA FORUM BUDGET PROJECT

Noted the 31st August TfB submission deadline.

10/ ANNUAL PLAY INSPECTION REPORT

WDC's annual inspection found no medium or high risk issues.

11/ VILLAGE HALL PROMOTIONAL EVENING (9TH SEPTEMBER)

Agreed to participate with a Parish Council display. JM

12/ PARISH EMERGENCY HELP CO-ORDINATOR

Cllr van Apeldoorn appointed as replacement co-ordinator.

13/ NEW NEIGHBOURHOOD PLAN

Following the Boxer Road/Barn Road appeal being allowed, Cllr Richards proposed restarting the Neighbourhood Plan; resolved to proceed immediately, re-engage Louise Thomas of Thomas Design and Regeneration and Consultation Ltd, retain Shoosmiths for legal advice, and circulate an explanatory notice (reproduced as an appendix) to every household and the Bucks Herald. JM

14/ MEETING WITH WDC HEAD OF PLANNING

Resolved to meet Penelope Tollitt on Wednesday 27th July 2016 to discuss the Neighbourhood Plan.

15/ WELLINGTON HOUSE DEVELOPMENT UPDATE

Cllr Bendyshe-Brown reported ongoing discussions with the developer on providing a dedicated route through the archway to the car park, which looked hopeful of resolving the right-of-way dispute.

16/ TESCO GRANT PLAYGROUND EQUIPMENT UPDATE

Work orders had been issued for a multi-play unit, a frog sit-in springer replacing the elephant springer, and new surfacing, with an estimated August 2016 start. JM

17/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

Objected: 16/06723/FUL (Land adjoining Saddleback Barn, Chadwell Hill Farm — round chapel), as inappropriate development in open countryside (Cllr Rogers declared an interest and took no part); 16/06732/OUT (Crownridge, Longwick Road — 4 dwellings), as outside the village curtilage. No objections: 16/06623/FUL (Malbaret, Bar Lane, Owlswick); 16/06941/FUL (Upper Manor Farm, Ilmer — Cllr Rogers declared an interest and took no part). 16/06787/TPO5 (5 Woodbine Close): to abide by the Arboriculturalist's decision. 16/06673/OUT (Land south of Rose Farm, Thame Road — 65 dwellings): an extension was requested pending the WDC Neighbourhood Plan meeting.

Approved: 16/06084/FUL (Chiltern, Chestnut Way). Refused: 16/06643/MIN (5 Lower Icknield Way — non-material amendment); 15/07209/OUT (OS Parcel 4446, Longwick Road — 9 dwellings). Appeal dismissed: 15/07508/FUL (Old Oaks, Thame Road).

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 20th September 2016 at 7.30pm. The meeting closed at 9.00pm.
