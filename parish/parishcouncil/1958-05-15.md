Longwick Cum Ilmer Parish Council meeting Held Schoolroom May 15th 1958 7-40 Pm

Present Councillors Harper (Chairman) Claydon, Tapping, Girling, Williams, Miss Walker & the Clerk

[Margin: Minutes] The minutes of the Last meetg where Read & Confirmed

[Margin: Appointment of New Clerk & Retirement of Present Clerk] It Was Proposed By Councillor F Walker Seconded By Councillor D Claydon That the Clerks Resignation be accepted & a vote of Thanks be Recorded to Him For the satisfactory Way he as Carried out his Duties It Was Proposed Councillor Claydon Seconded Councillor Tapping That Mr Chivers Forge Cottage Longwick be accepted as the new Clerk to the Council as & From June 24 1958 at a Salary of £12-0-0 Per annum Carried

[Margin: School Managers] Councillor Claydon Seconded Councillor Tapping That Councillors W J Harper & F Williams be appointed School Managers For 1958/1961 Carried

[Margin: Payment of accounts] Councillor Walker Seconded Councillor Tapping The Following accounts be Paid audit Stamps 10/- Hon Secretary County of Buckingham association of Parish Councils £1-7-6 Clerks Salary & Postage £3-1-5 Due June 24th 1958

[Margin: Electric Wire across No 21 Footpath] Proposed Councillor Claydon Sec Councillor Walker the clerk Write the County Surveyor Re Electric Wire on No 21 Footpath on Mr Hazellton's Meadle Farm Meadle

The Clerk was Instructed to see Mr Pearce Re puts entrax Gutter From Bar Lane to Mr Withes Woodyard.

[Margin: Election of Officers] Pro Councillor Walker Seconded Councillor Tapping That Mr Harper & Mr Claydon Carry on With the offices of Chairman & Vice Chairman Respectively Carried

W J Harper Oct 2/58
