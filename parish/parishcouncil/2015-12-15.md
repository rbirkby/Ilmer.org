9\. CORRESPONDENCE

A letter has been received from Lloyds Bank regarding the Financial Services Compensation Scheme. With effect from 1st January 2016 the Scheme limit will change from £85000 to £75000. Noted.

An email has been received from Wycombe District Council regarding the Wycombe District Local Plans Princes Risborough Town Plan Regulation 18 Consultation. The Parish Council is invited to submit any comments on what it thinks the new Princes Risborough Town Plan should contain, as part of the statutory consultation stage (Regulation 18 Consultation). WDC would like the Longwick-cum-Ilmer Parish Council to inform WDC of any strategic issues that the Parish Council believe WDC should be considering in preparing the New Local Plan and more generally any issues that the Princes Risborough Town Plan should be addressing. All present agreed for The Clerk to submit the following comments:- a woodland boundary to be created at the perimeter around the whole development for the benefit of wildlife and the view to residents; there are concerns that the suggested route for the ring road will cause an increase in traffic on the B4009 diverting traffic through Longwick village and surrounding minor lanes, the preferred option for the Parish Council is for a new road through the development to reduce the impact of passing traffic.

An email has been received from Bucks County Council regarding the draft Local Transport Plan. The Plan is a blueprint for travel in Buckinghamshire in the future, covering all types of transport and looking ahead to 2036. It sets out how transport can play a vital part in realising the County Council's vision to make Buckinghamshire a great place to live and work. It will be supported by more detailed documents, which provide further policy and guidance. Some have already been produced and some are yet to be developed. BCC urge everyone to have a say, and to share this through the networks. There is more information at https://democracy.buckscc.gov.uk/mgConsultationDisplay.aspx?ID=1117 where there is a link to the consultation survey. Noted.

An email has been received from Tesco Local Community Grants. A new grants programme is being delivered to fund projects on green and open spaces. The types of project they can fund are parks, pocket parks and urban green spaces; green corridors – river and canals, cycle ways; formal and informal play areas and many more. Projects will still need to benefit the community and be free to access. All present agreed for the Clerk to apply for funding to update the Playground Equipment in the playing field.

10\. REPORT FROM THE STEERING GROUP ON THE NEIGHBOURHOOD PLAN

Cllr Brian Richard raised the issue of apparent flooding in the village. Cllr Val McPherson confirmed that she was liaising with the residents to draw some information together and would also contact Thames Water.

A draft of the Longwick-cum-Ilmer Neighbourhood Plan has been submitted for examination. Cllr Brian Richards proposed that the Parish Council requests that the Steering Group meet to recommend the courses of action to inform the residents following the Examiners Report.

11\. TRAFFIC CONGESTION IN THE LOCALITY OF THE PLAYING FIELD

Cllr Ian Walker informed the members that parents of school children are parking in the Village Hall car park causing an inconvenience to local residents and he is concerned that there will be an accident.

Cllr Jane Rogers proposed to ask the police to visit The Green at the relevant times due to the amount of traffic causing a nuisance. Seconded by Cllr Sally Whitworth. All present agreed. The Clerk is requested to contact the police.

Cllr Graham Walters proposed to contact the Highways Traffic Management team at Bucks County Council to request they also make a site visit and suggest a long term solution. Seconded by Cllr Val McPherson. All present agreed. The Clerk is requested to contact Bucks County Cllr B Bendyshe-Brown.
