PRESENT: Cllr Mrs Valerie McPherson BEM (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Jane Rogers, Rolf van Apeldoorn, Graham Walters, Sally Whitworth and Brian Richards, Clerk Mrs Susanne Griffiths, Clerical Assistant Mrs Jayne Mylchreest. No members of the public were present.

No apologies were received. The Minutes of 17th November 2015 were approved and signed. No interests were declared.

Matters arising: the Christmas tree and lights were in place with thanks to the resident volunteer; the Remembrance Sunday arrangements had gone well.

FINANCE: Routine year-end payments including grass cutting, salaries, risk assessment, dog waste/litter collection and Christmas tree costs. Bank Balance £23,750.62.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No issues in the Risk Inspection Report. Tree planting to proceed in the New Year weather permitting.

ROADS: The Clerk to continue chasing BCC on outstanding lay-by, signage and hedge issues raised in November. SG

CORRESPONDENCE: Noted a BALC newsletter and a WDC consultation on the Local Plan timetable. Play equipment costings were presented following the resident's fundraising offer; agreed to proceed with obtaining formal quotations for the New Year. SG

11/ NEIGHBOURHOOD PLAN STEERING GROUP

The Steering Group reported continuing progress toward the Submission Version, with flooding evidence and the Landscape Visual Impact Assessment still to be finalised.

12/ BUDGET AND PRECEPT 2016/2017

Agreed the budget and precept for 2016/2017 as circulated.

13/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

No new applications to consider.

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 19th January 2016 at 7.45pm. The meeting closed at 9.15pm.
