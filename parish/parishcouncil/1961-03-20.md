LONGWICK-CUM-ILMER PARISH COUNCIL 228 Meeting Held School room Longwick Monday 20th March 1961 7.30 pm

[Margin: Present] Councillors Harper, Claydon. Brock Tappin Gomme

[Margin: Mins] Minutes of last meeting were read and confirmed

[Margin: Street names] The clerk was instructed to contact WRDC regarding street name signs

[Margin: Footpaths] It was proposed by Councillor Claydon and seconded by C Gomme that the clerk should contact the Div Surveyor (Mr Nobbs) regarding the completion of the footpath in Chestnut Way Longwick

[Margin: Finance] It was proposed by Councillor Brock and seconded by Councillor Claydon that the following accounts should be paid Clerks Salary Jan Feb March 1961 £3-0-0 Postage 3/11 Audit Stamp 5/- Parish Councils Ass £2-8-0 Shaw Sons (Stationery) 6/3. Carried

[Margin: School Managers] It was proposed by Councillor Gomme and seconded by Councillor Claydon that the School managers for Longwick C/E Primary School for the next term of office should be Councillor W Harper, and Mr J Chivers Carried W. J Harper 29/5/61
