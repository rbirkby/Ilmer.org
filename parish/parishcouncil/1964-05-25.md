LONGWICK. CUM-ILMER PARISH COUNCIL MEETING HELD VILLAGE HALL MONDAY 25TH MAY 1964 at 7.30 p.m.

[Margin: Present.] Cllr Leuw, Gomme, Harper, Tapping. Mrs Wootton. [Margin: Absent.] Apologies for absence for Cllr Walker & Mrs Brock. [Margin: Mins] Minutes of the last meeting were read and confirmed.

[Margin: Election of Officers] Cllr Leuw, seconded Cllr Wootton that Cllr Harper be re-elected Chairman Cllr Gomme seconded Cllr Tapping that Cllr Brock be re-elected Vice Chairman Carried.

[Margin: Main Drainage] A letter was read from the Engineer & Surveyor stating that a design for Main Drainage scheme for Longwick was being commenced. and it was hoped that a start could be expected in the autumn of 1965.

[Margin: Ilmer Road.] The chairman informed the meeting that £500 had been allocated for work on Ilmer Road.

[Margin: Mill Stream Bridge] Cllr Gomme asked if a letter could be sent to Mr Frankland as to the narrowness of the bridge over the Mill stream also that the parapet was daily slipping away into the stream, a reminder for the 40 p.m.h. speed limit signs was asked for as well.

[Margin: Finance] Proposed Cllr Leuw seconded Cllr Gomme that the annual subscription to the Bucks Assc. of Parish Councils be paid £3 . 14 . 2. Carried

W J Harper 17/8/64
