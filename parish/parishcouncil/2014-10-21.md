PRESENT: Cllr Mrs Valerie McPherson (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Graham Walters, Mrs Jane Rogers, Rolf van Apeldoorn, Brian Richards and Sally Whitworth, Mrs Caroline Page (Acting Clerk), 1 Member of Public.

No apologies were received. The Minutes of 16th September 2014 were approved and signed.

Cllr Bendyshe-Brown sent apologies. Network Rail had painted over the railway bridge graffiti. GW. The Chairman reported that Risborough Rangers Juniors training on the Playing Field on Saturday mornings involved far more children than envisaged, churning up the pitch used by Longwick players on Saturday afternoons, with parents' cars filling the car park; all present agreed to write asking them to limit numbers to 15 and use the field for 2 hours maximum.

FINANCE: Routine payments (clerk cover fee, Risk Assessments, grass cutting £442.88, a play area gate post repair £310.80, printing for the questionnaire and newsletter, Clerk's October salary, and reimbursed stamp/envelope costs to several councillors for the questionnaire mailing) were examined and signed off. Bank Balance £19,957.13 after the WDC precept.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No medium/high risk issues. The Clerk to chase the outstanding hedge-trimming quotation and ask the contractor to inspect two leaning trees on footpath 4.

ROADS: The Clerk to report to BCC Transport for Bucks that hedges are obstructing road signs on the B4009 in Meadle where Stockwell Lane joins Lower Icknield Way (per a Chiltern Society footpath report).

CORRESPONDENCE: Councillors agreed to respond individually to BCC's consultation on County Council priorities for 2015/16. A Polling District and Polling Places Review consultation, an Annual Chilterns Forum invitation, and a WDC Standards training invitation were noted. Agreed, following a resident's request, that both first names and surnames will be read at the Remembrance Service. The Local Area Forum's spending-priority workshop received the Council's ranked list: (1) traffic management/speeding, (2) rural public transport, (3) support for older/isolated people, (4) sport and leisure, (5) youth activities, (6) sustainable lifestyles; Cllr Rogers to attend the workshop with these priorities.

10/ FEEDBACK FROM THE 7TH OCTOBER PUBLIC CONSULTATION MEETING

Cllr Graham Walters gave an update; he would circulate a draft findings report by 26th October ahead of WDC's 28th October deadline for comments. Cllrs McPherson, Walters, Whitworth, van Apeldoorn and Walker agreed to meet on 26th October to agree the Council's response.

11/ DEVOLVED SERVICES UPDATE

The meeting scheduled for 15th October had been cancelled.

12/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

New application: 14/07401/FUL, 1 Central Cottage, Thame Road, Longwick (single storey rear extension following garage demolition) — consultation period had already expired.

Approved: 14/06953/FUL (Crownridge, Longwick Road — two storey side extensions etc). Refused: 14/06993/PA (Owlswick Barn — change of use to dwelling) and 14/06998/PA (Poplars Farm, Meadle Village Road — change of use to dwelling), both prior notification applications under Class MB.

Cllr Graham Walters advised that the Ilmer solar farm application would go to WDC committee on 29th October with an officer recommendation for approval; Cllr Sally Whitworth agreed to speak against it for up to 3 minutes, with Cllr Walters drafting her speech.

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 18th November 2014 at 8.00pm. The meeting closed at 9.40pm.
