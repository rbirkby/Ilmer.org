meeting of Longwick Parish Council Held at the School Room Longwick april 29th 1953 To Read & Confirm minutes of Last meeting, To Read Correspondence, To Elect the Chairman & Vice Chairman For 1953. Any other Business Present Councillors Harper Chairman, D Claydon, O Tapping, E Girling, L.W. Claridge, Miss F M Walker F Williams & The Clerk

[Margin: minutes] The minutes of the Last meeting where Read & Confirmed

[Margin: Correspondence] Correspondence Was Read Re Claiming of Footpath at Horsendon. Pro Councillor Williams Sec Councillor Girling That we Claim the said Footpath the matter Being left in Chairmans Hands to be dealt With

[Margin: Reports] a Report Was Given By. The Chairman & Councillor Claydon Re Parish Council association meeting Held at High Wycombe & aylesbury.

[Margin: Election of Chairman & V. Chairman] It Was Proposed Councillor Claridge & Sec Councillor Girling That Councillor Harper be Re-elected as Chairman Carried Pro Councillor Williams Sec Councillor Claridge That Councillor Claydon be Re-elected Vice Chairman Carried

[Margin: Any other Business] It Was Decided to leave the question Re Handcuffs For Parish Constables For Further Enquiries

Pro Councillor Williams Sec Councillor Claydon that the Clerk Pursues the matter of the Water Supply at Ilmer With the Wycombe R.D.C. asking For a Deputation to be met on this matter Carried

Pro Councillor Claydon Sec Councillor Claridge that The Clerk Complain very Strongly to the Thames Valley Traction Company, Re no 80 Bus Service & the inconvenience of Long Distance Pass Travelling by this Service From aylesbury Pointing out that some Passengers For Longwick Had been Left at aylesbury Carried W J Harper July 28/53
