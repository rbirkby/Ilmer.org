Meeting of the Longwick-cum-Ilmer Parish Council held in the Schoolroom, Longwick on Monday Aug 8th 1938 at 8 pm. To read and confirm Minutes of last meeting. Replies to correspondence. Consideration of Sale of Parish Property. Accounts for payment. To consider the issuing of a Precept upon the Rating Authority for General Expenses of the Council. Trimming of Hedges at Ilmer. Present:- Councillors Wm A. Walker (Chairman) Herbert S Pope. Albert Jas Goodchild, Wm J. Harper, F. Williams, E. J. Girling, Herbert Wm Lewis and Mr Robert Bailey (Clerk).

[Margin: Minutes] Minutes of last meeting read and confirmed.

[Margin: Foot paths Longwick] The County Surveyor wrote with reference to the Council's application for foot paths in Longwick, stating the South-Western Divisional Highways Committee of the County Council consider that neither of these foot paths are urgently required, as there is little development on the sections of roads in question, and there are more pressing cases elsewhere for which, at present, funds are not available. In the circumstances, the Committee decided to recommend that no action be taken in the matter at the present time.

[Margin: 30 miles per hour Speed Limit] He also wrote with reference to the Councils application for a 30 m.p.h. speed limit between the Summerleys Road junction with the Chinnor Road, through Longwick to a point near Anderdons Farm, stating that the Highways Committee of the County Council after consultation with the Chief Constable, had decided not to recommend the Council to make an Order imposing such new restrictions.

[Margin: Filling in of Pond at Ilmer] A further communication was received from the County Surveyor, with reference to the filling in of the

pond opposite Ilmer Church, stating that the South-Western Divisional Highways Committee had decided to fill in a portion of the pond, leaving the remainder in form of a large ditch to take the road surface water, with an overflow pipe to the ditch on the opposite side of the road. It was considered inadvisable to fill in the whole of the pond, in view of the fact that it is situated at a low point in the road and the adjoining land is low-lying and subject to flooding. The Divisional Committee recommend that the whole cost of the proposed work be borne by the County Council, and he would be glad to hear that the Parish Council agree with what is proposed to be done. It was agreed that the Clerk communicate with the County Surveyor, stating that the Parish Council agree with what is proposed to be done.

[Margin: Sale of Parish Property] The question of the Sale of the Parish Property at Ilmer was considered, Mrs S. Matthews wrote asking that her offer of £25 for the purchase of the property might be submitted to the Minister for his consideration. Mr G. H. Donald, of Dragon Farm, Long Crendon, wrote submitting a revised offer of £52. for the purchase of the property, and an offer of £50 was received from Walter Rose of Haddenham. Proposed by Councillor H. S. Pope seconded by Councillor F. Williams, that the offers, together with the correspondence received and other matters relating thereto, be sent to the Minister of Health for his consideration and instructions. Carried

[Margin: Payment of Accounts] Proposed by Councillor F. Williams seconded by Councillor H. W. Lewis that Cheques be drawn in payment of the following accounts:- Messrs G. T. de Fraine & Co Ltd. Advert re Sale of Parish Property 5/-d. Robert Bailey

refund of Cash paid for Rates on Parish Cottages, occupied by Mrs S. Matthews, half year ending Sept 30th 1938 16/6. Audit Stamp 1937/38 5/- Total Cheque to Bailey £1/1/6. Carried

[Margin: No Precept to be issued for Financial Year ending 30/3/39.] The question of issuing a Precept upon the Rating Authority for General Expenses of the Council was considered, and as the Council had a balance in hand of £25/3/2 at the end of March 1938, Councillor Pope proposed and Councillor Girling seconded that no Precept be issued for the Financial year ending March 31st 1939. Carried

[Margin: Trimming of Hedges Ilmer] Proposed by Councillor Harper seconded by Councillor Williams that the Clerk communicate with Messrs Douglas Goodchild, and Robert Mater, asking them to have their hedges trimmed back on the road leading to the Parish Cottages. Carried

[Margin: Foot-paths. Signposts] As the County Council were now erecting the Public Foot-path signposts, it was agreed that the Councillors should make an inspection, with a view to verifying the positions with the foot paths Map. Oct 24th 1938 Wm A Walker
