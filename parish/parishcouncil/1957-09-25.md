Longwick Cum Ilmer Parish Council meeting Held Schoolroom Longwick Sept 25th 1957

Present Councillors W Harper Chairman, D Claydon L Claridge, F M Walker O Tapping & the Clerk.

[Margin: Minutes] The minutes of the Last meetg where Read & Confirmed

[Margin: Civil Defence circular] It Was agreed the Civil Defence Poster be Displayed

[Margin: Payment of Accounts] Proposed Councillor Claridge Sec Councillor Tapping the Following accounts be Paid Clerks Salary £6-0-0 Workmans Limited Liability act 5/0 municipal Insurance Coy 10/0 Carried

[Margin: Footpath No 73 & Stile no 3] It Was agreed to leave the matter of Stile on Footpath no 3 untill after the Chairman had viewed same

meeting Closed 8.30pm W. J. Harper
