Longwick Cum Ilmer Parish Council Meeting Held in School Room Longwick Aug 10th 1955 Present Councillor Harper Chairman, Councillor Claydon, Girling, Williams, & Miss Walker & the Clerk.

To Read & Confirm minutes of Last meetg matters arresing, the abolition of Parish Constables, Chairmans Report B,a,P,C meetg held at Aylesbury any other Business Before the meetg opened the Chairman Called the Council to stand in Silence in Respect For Alderman Walker

[Margin: Minutes] The Minutes of the Last meety where Read & Confirmd.

[Margin: Parish Constables] Pro Councillor Walker & Sec Councillor Girling That we Object very strongly against the Chief Constables appeal to abolish Parish Constables, & that we support Taplows Objection Re same. Re Mr William Representing us at the Bucks Standy Joint Committee The Clerk was instructed to inquire int any expenses the would be incurred as we Could get Legal advice From the solicitor through the Bucks association of Parish Councils Carried.

[Margin: B. A. P. C] The Chairman gave a good & Detailed Report of Bucks association of Parish Councills meetg held at Aylesbury

[Margin: Any other business] Pro Councillor Williams Sec Councillor Girling the Clerk Write the Surveyor asking For a meeting With a Deputation From the Council to Discuss Ways & means to improve the approaches to the Sportsman Garage Crossroads, also the Duke of Wellingtons Corner, & the obstruction by a Holly Tree in the Bar Lane of the View to the main Road

[Margin: Payment of Accounts] P Councillor Girling Sec E Claydon the Followy accounts be Paid municipal mutual Insurance 10/- Employers minute liability act 5/0 W J Harper 19/10/55
