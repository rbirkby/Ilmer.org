PRESENT: Cllr Val McPherson BEM (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Sally Whitworth, Jane Rogers, Rolf van Apeldoorn, Richard Myers and Brian Richards, Clerk Susanne Griffiths, Deputy Clerk Jayne Mylchreest. No members of the public.

No apologies were received. The Minutes of 16th May 2017 were approved and signed. Cllr Rogers declared a non-pecuniary interest in item 8 (former Shoulder of Mutton).

Matters arising: approved a £108 playing field fence post repair. JM. English Heritage had been contacted regarding the two parish milestones; Cllr Whitworth to contact local tradesmen. SW

Cllr Bendyshe-Brown reported on a Thames Water road closure, Bridleways 14 (Owlswick) and 21A (Meadle), the Wellington House development, the Scout Hut-to-school footpath, Speed Watch, flood management at the Bar Lane development, and an Askett planning application.

FINANCE: Payments including salaries, pension, risk assessment, grass cutting, meeting hall hire, travel expenses for developer meetings, PAYE, Village Fete printing, BALC Good Councillor Guides, councillor induction training, and playing field electricity. Receipts included £5,047.43 from the BALC Transparency Fund and £13,873.15 WDC precept VAT repayment. Bank Balance £34,239.46.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No issues in the May Risk Inspection Reports.

CORRESPONDENCE: Cllr Rogers having declared an interest, agreed to invite the developer behind a proposal to purchase the former Shoulder of Mutton, Owlswick as a post-rehabilitation "half-way house" for spinal injury patients from Stoke Mandeville, to an informal meeting. JM. Agreed to express interest in a WDC town/parish charter, subject to the Secretary of State's reorganisation decision. JM. Declined to host a Chilterns Conservation Board "Chalk, Cherries and Chairs" workshop. JM. Agreed playing field access for a pre-school end-of-term party (21st July), subject to standard documentation. JM

9/ NEIGHBOURHOOD PLAN REPORT

Cllrs Richards and Myers had discussed a Sequential Flooding Report with WDC's Planning Officer; WDC confirmed the newsletter's Rose Farm information was correct, apologised for Local Plan-related delays, and confirmed no pre-examination report would be required; the plan remained with solicitors.

10–11/ DEVELOPER MEETINGS

Cllr McPherson reported meeting Croudace Homes with Cllr Richards; agreed to arrange a full-Council meeting. JM. The Chairman and Vice Chairman's meeting with Bellway Homes (covering street lighting, nest boxes, affordable housing, start dates and drainage) had been followed up at a full Council meeting on 13th June.

12/ REMEMBRANCE SERVICE

Cllr van Apeldoorn to arrange the 11th November service, with expenditure up to £100 approved and Cllr Richards to invite local schoolchildren. RvA/BR

13/ 2018 ANNUAL PARISH MEETING FORMAT

Agreed a separate meeting on Monday 23rd April 2018, doors 7pm for 7.30pm. JM

14/ CAROL SERVICE

Agreed Monday 18th December 2017 at 5pm. JM

15/ OUTDOOR ADULT FITNESS EQUIPMENT

Cllr Myers to investigate grants for eight pieces of equipment. JM

16/ PARISH LOGO

Agreed a further newsletter appeal for resident design ideas. VM

17/ PLAYING FIELD REGULATIONS REVIEW

Agreed no policy changes, but to publicise the application process on the website. JM

18/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

To abide by the Arboriculturalist's decision: 17/06486/CTREE (Cozens Holdings, Owlswick). Objected strongly: 17/06263/FUL (Plots 4 & 5, OS Parcel 8955, Askett — retrospective siting of 3 mobile homes/caravan), as contrary to the emerging Local Plan's unsuitability finding for this land, seriously harmful to open countryside character, unsympathetic to the local landscape, damaging to the Lower Icknield Way's public enjoyment, harmful to AONB views from the Chilterns, and noting the development had already taken place unlawfully. Approved: 17/05899/FUL (Lyndale, Meadle); 17/05621/FUL and 17/05622/LBC (Meadle Cottage). Noted: WDC TPO Order No. 29/2016 (OS Parcel 2075, Thame Road).

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 18th July 2017 at 7.30pm. The meeting closed at 9.30pm.
