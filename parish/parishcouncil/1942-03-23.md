Meeting of the Longwick-cum-Ilmer Parish Council held in the Schoolroom, Longwick, on Monday March 23rd 1942 at 7.15. pm. To read and confirm Minutes of last meeting. Payment of accounts. Present:- Councillors Wm A. Walker (Chairman) E. J. Girling F. Williams, W. J. Harper. A. J. Goodchild and Robert Bailey (Clerk)

[Margin: Minutes] Minutes of last meeting read and confirmed.

[Margin: Payment of accounts] Proposed by Councillor W. J. Harper seconded by Councillor F. Williams that the following accounts be passed for payment:- Robert Bailey. One Quarters Salary as Clerk to 25/3/42 £1/5/- Postages (One year) 8/8. Total Cheque to Bailey £1/13/8. Correspondent, Longwick C. of E. School. Use of Schoolroom for meetings 10/-d. Carried

[Margin: Date of Annual Meeting of the Parish Council] It was agreed that the Annual Meeting of the Parish Council be held at the Schoolroom, Longwick, on Monday April 27th 1942 at 8 pm.

[Margin: Financial Statement. Year ending 31/3/41] The Financial Statement of Accounts for year ending March 31st 1941 as certified by the District Auditor was laid before the meeting. H. S. Pope. Apr 27th 1942

[Stamp: DISTRICT AUDIT]
