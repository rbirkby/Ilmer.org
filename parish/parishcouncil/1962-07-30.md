LONGWICK-CUM-ILMER PARISH COUNCIL Meeting held School room Longwick Monday 30th July 1962

[Margin: Present] Cll Harper, Walker, Gomme, Leuw, Tappin, Mrs Brock, Miss Wootton

[Margin: Mins] Minutes of the last meeting were read and confirmed

[Margin: Speed Limit] The clerk read a letter from the Bucks County Council regarding the application for a speed limit through Longwick. The matter is under consideration by the Bucks C.C.

[Margin: Clerk to Council.] It was proposed by Cll Walker and seconded by Cll Gomme that Mr A Austin of Lower Icknield Way, Longwick be appointed as Clerk to the Parish Council, Carried.

[Margin: Finance] It was agreed that the following accounts be paid. Clerks salary April, May, June, July 1962 £4 . 0 . 0 Postage 3/6 Audit Stamps for 1960-1961 £1 . 0 . 0. Total £5 - 3 - 6

Meeting closed 8.20 pm W. J. Harper 30/10/62
