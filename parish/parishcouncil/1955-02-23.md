Longwick Cum Ilmer Parish Council meeting Held in Central Cottages Longwick Feb 23rd 1955 Present, Councillors Harper, Chairman., Claydon, Claridge Tapping, Walker, Williams & The Clerk. To Read & Confirm minutes of Last meeting, matters Arising Issuing a Precept. Payment of Accounts, Any Other Business

[Margin: minutes] The minutes Where Read & Confirmed

[Margin: Ilmer Water Supply.] It Was agreed. The Clerk acknowledge Correspondence of the R D C. & wait Further Developments, re use the amount of the Grant Proposed For Same.. Carried

[Margin: Princes Risborough Charities] Pro Councillor Claridge Sec D. Claydon That Miss F M Walker be Reappointed Trustee. Carried

[Margin: Monks Risborough Charities] Pro Councillor Claridge Sec D Claydon That Mr Girling be Reappointed Trustee. Carried

[Margin: Precept] Pro F Williams Sec L Claridge That we Precept For £20. For the half year's Ending September 1955. Carried

[Margin: Payment of Accounts] Pro O Tapping Sec Miss F M Walker That the Following accounts be Paid Ten shillings For audit Stamp. Carried

[Margin: Election of Parish Constables] Pro D Claydon Sec O Tapping That The Follany Parish Constables be Elected En Bloc F Williams, O Goodchild, H Goodchild, O Tapping & E T Rutland Carried W J Harper 23/3/55
