meeting of Longwick Cum Ilmer Parish Council Held July 28th at the School Room Longwick To Read & Confirm minutes of Last meeting To Read Correspondence , Payment of Accounts Any other Business.

[Margin: minutes] The minutes of the Last meeting Was Read & Confirmed

[Margin: Correspondence] It Was Decided that the matter Re Water Supply at Ilmer be brought up at the next meeting Proposed Councillor Claydon Sec Councillor Walker that A Ancliffe the Clerk be appointed School Manager

[Margin: Accounts] Pro Councillor F Williams Sec Councillor Claydon The Following accounts be Paid affiliation To County association of Parish Councils £1-10-0 Clerks Salary To June 24th 2-10-0 Clerks Bond 3/0 Employers Liability act 5/0.

[Margin: Any other Business] The Chairman Gave a Report on the meeting of the association of Parish Councils Stating that He had asked For a Telephone Kiosk to be Installed at the Sportsman Garage Crossroads The Clerk Was Instructed to write Miss E Kingham Vine Cottage Longwick a Letter of appreciation & thanks For Voluntary to Keep the War memorial Clean. &, in order. W J Harper Oct 1st 1953
