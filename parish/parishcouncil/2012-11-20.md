10\. THE SPEED LIMIT ON THAME ROAD.

Cllr G O'Neil reported on his meeting with the Local Area Technician. It was agreed to place slow markings on the road and to install more speed warning signs on the Thame Road.

11\. THE MEETING DATES FOR 2012

All present agreed to continue meeting on the third Tuesday of each month for the monthly Parish Council meeting.

12\. THE UPDATE OF CONTACT DETAILS FOR THE PARISH EMERGENCY PLAN

All present agreed to be on the emergency management and activation team and to supply Cllr G Walters with their contact details and those of any other residents that would be willing to be part of the emergency plan by the 11th December 2012.
