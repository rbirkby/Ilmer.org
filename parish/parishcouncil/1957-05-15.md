Longwick Cum Ilmer Annual Parish Council meeting Held in the School Room Longwick may 15th 1957

Present Councillor W Harper Chairman, D Claydon F Williams, Miss F M Walker & the Clerk. Apologize was Received From O Tapping not bing able to attend owing to an injury

[Margin: Minutes] The minutes of the Last meetg where Read & Confirmed

[Margin: Correspondance] Pro Councillor Walker Sec Councillor Claydon That we Enter the Best Kept Village Competition & the Chairman Get in touch with the W Institute on this matter Carried

[Margin: Any Other Business] Pro Councillor Williams Sec Councillor Walker The Clerks Salary be Raised £2-0-0 making his salary £12-0-0 Per annum Carried It Was agreed the Clerk get in Touch With the Surveyor Re the Stile on Footpath no 17.

[Margin: Accounts] Pro Councillor Walker Sec Councillor Williams The Following accounts be Paid audit Stamp 10/0 affiliation Fee to County of Buckingham association of Parish Councils £1-7-6 Carried

[Margin: Election of Chairman & Vice Chairman] Pro Councillor Williams Sec Councillor Claydon Councillor Harper Be Relected Chairman Carried Pro Councillor Walker Sec Councillor Williams Councillor Claydon be Relected Vice Chairman Carried

W J Harper Sept 25/57
