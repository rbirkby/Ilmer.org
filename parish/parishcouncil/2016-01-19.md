PRESENT: Cllr Mrs Valerie McPherson BEM (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Jane Rogers, Rolf van Apeldoorn, Graham Walters, Sally Whitworth and Brian Richards, Clerk Mrs Susanne Griffiths, Clerical Assistant Mrs Jayne Mylchreest, 2 members of the public.

No apologies were received. The Minutes of 15th December 2015 were approved and signed. No interests were declared.

Cllr Bendyshe-Brown (BCC), unable to attend, reported by note: lay-by soil removal was a priority following pre-Christmas sampling; the Bar Lane flooding was being pushed for early resolution (landowners are responsible for clearing their own ditches); the B4009 lay-by caravan had been reported to WDC's Waste Team; and Vehicle Activated Signs for Thame Road and Chestnut Way would be considered in the next BCC financial year. Cllr Clive Harriss (WDC) reported the Thame Road lay-by recycling bins had been removed.

Matters arising: Sgt James Benfield had confirmed police would target inconsiderate school-time parking on The Green; the Village Hall car park congestion remained a civil matter between the car park's owner and the school. WDC was investigating a Planning Enforcement complaint regarding a field under multiple ownership, and BCC was investigating a related fire. A revised tree-works quotation from the WDC Tree Survey was approved. A resident's hedge cutting notice was partly complied with. A Working Group (Cllr Whitworth) was set up to progress the Tesco Bags of Help playground grant.

FINANCE: Routine payments including grass cutting, salaries, pension, risk assessment, a laminated parish map, WDALC subscription and map cabinet keys. Bank Balance £21,057.43.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No issues in the December Risk Inspection Report; Cllr Whitworth to arrange hedge cutting for the playing field.

The Sports Club's request to switch grass cutting from rotary to cylinder mowing was declined for the current financial year, with new quotations to be sought for 2017; a Sports Club new-football-team notice was agreed for the newsletter.

ROADS: Overgrown Bar Lane vegetation reducing visibility onto Thame Road was reported to BCC. The Clerk to raise outstanding Thame Road lay-by soil, unclear Stockwell Lane directional signage from Great Kimble, a leaning sign at the Stockwell Lane/B4009 triangle, a request for extra slow-turning signage on the Stockwell Lane bend into Meadle, and overgrown vegetation on the B4009 footway near the railway bridge. SG/JM

CORRESPONDENCE: A new "Longwick Village News & Gossip" Facebook page was noted.

10/ NEIGHBOURHOOD PLAN STEERING GROUP

The Plan remained with the Examiner; nothing further to report.

12/ NEWSLETTER PRODUCTION AND ADVERTISING

Agreed a £300 quotation for another local authority to produce the newsletter, and to accept paid advertising subject to Council approval, with rates to be set at the February meeting following research into comparable local costs.

14/ ILMER GREEN AND POND

BCC confirmed the land is registered village green no. 12, with the Parish Council registered as owner since 1974 under the Commons Registration Act 1965. Pollarding of trees by the pond, being in a conservation area, requires a WDC application. SG

15/ INTERNAL AUDITOR APPOINTMENT

Appointed Mr Don Timms for 2015/2016. SG

16/ QUEEN'S 90TH BIRTHDAY CELEBRATIONS

Agreed to invite LOGS to join a village litter pick on 5th March; the Big Lunch on 7th June would be represented by Cllrs Whitworth and Rogers; no beacon would be lit for lack of a suitable site.

17/ BUDGET AND PRECEPT 2016/2017

Agreed the budget and a precept of £22,890.00.

18/ SECOND DEFIBRILLATOR LOCATION

Agreed to site a donated defibrillator outside the Red Lion, subject to the owner's permission. RvA

19/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

15/08455/OUT (OS Parcels 6232 & 7428, Thame Road — up to 43 dwellings): response deferred to the February meeting. 16/05051/CTREE (Ilmer Meadow, Ilmer Lane, Ilmer — felling/pruning including dead Elm): to abide by the Arboriculturalist's decision. Approved: 15/07824/FUL (Three Cottages, Stockwell Lane); 15/08033/CLP (Sans Paddock, Owlswick); 15/08144/FUL (5 Meadow Drive). A Tree Preservation Order was made for a Beech at Ilmer Meadow, Ilmer (TPO/21/2015); TPOs were declined for Beech at The Old Vicarage, Ilmer and trees at October Cottage, Ilmer. Refused: 15/05579/CLP (Twin Oaks Park — additional caravans).

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 16th February 2016 at 7.30pm. The meeting closed at 9.40pm.
