Meeting of Longwick Cum Ilmer Parish Council Held in the School Room Longwick June 26th 1952 at 8-0 To Read & Confirm minutes of Last meeting Correspondence Received Payments of Accounts any other Business Present - Councillors J. Harper Chairman, D Claydon E J Girling, Miss Walker, F Williams

[Margin: minutes] The minutes of the last meeting where Read & Confirmed

[Margin: Correspondence] The Reply to our application For the 30 MPH limit was Read. & noted. also the Reply Re the Ilmer Townray Road Ditch was Passed on to Councillor Claridge

[Margin: Telephone Pole] After Discussing the Reply From Oxford The Clerk was instructed to send this matter to the County Council Highways Committee Pro Councillor F Williams Sec. D Claydon

[Margin: Water Hydrants Owlswick] Pro Councillor Miss Walker Sec Councillor Girling The Clerk Write the County Fire Bridge Head quarters Pressing For a Fire Hydrant to be installed also serve Manor Farm & Old acres.

[Margin: Payment of Accounts] Pro Councillor Girling Sec F Williams The Following accounts be Paid Parish Council Election Expences £21 - 15 - 9 Clerk's Salary Due June 24th £2 . 0 . 0 Chairman Closed the meeting 8-40 pm W. J Harper
