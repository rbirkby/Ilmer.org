meeting of Longwick Cum Ilmer Parish Council Held in School Room Longwick on Jan 20th 1954 To Read & Confirm minutes of Last meeting, matters arising & Objection of Transport Executive To Footpath. 12 A also Benskins Brewery Ltd objection To Footpath 18. any other Business Councillors Present, Councillor Harper Chairman, D Claydon E J Girling, O Tapping, F M Walker, F Williams

[Margin: minutes] The minutes of Last meeting Where Read & Confirmed

[Margin: matters arising Footpaths] It was agreed Pro Councillor Walker Sec Councillor Williams That we do not object to the objection of the Transport Executive on Footpath no 12 A. That we Report no Objection submitted by Benskins Brewery, & send in Draft map Statement With Witness Forms Carried

[Margin: Ilmer Water Supply] It Was agreed The Chairman Contact Ilmer & Report at next meeting

[Margin: Any Other Business] Pro Councillor Claydon Sec Miss F Walker that a Complaint be Sent to Princes Risborough P.C. Re shortage of Staff & service at Princes Risborough Post office W J Harper March 30/54
