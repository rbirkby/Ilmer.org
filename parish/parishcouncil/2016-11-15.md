PRESENT: Cllr Mrs Valerie McPherson BEM (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Sally Whitworth, Jane Rogers, Rolf van Apeldoorn and Brian Richards, Clerk Susanne Griffiths, Deputy Clerk Jayne Mylchreest, 2 members of the public.

No apologies were received. The Minutes of 18th October 2016 were approved and signed. Cllrs Rogers and Walker declared an interest in item 10 as Village Hall Trustees.

Cllr Bendyshe-Brown (BCC) reported on the winter maintenance programme (clearing Footpath 4 from the playing field, the Thame Road/Ilmer Road footway, and roundabout hedging), the Wellington House development, VAS funding efforts, and ditch clearance at Horsenden to address flooding.

FINANCE: Payments including salaries, pension, grass cutting/maintenance, risk assessment, public-meeting leaflet printing, dog waste collection and newsletter printing. Receipts included the final £3,000 Tesco Bags of Help grant payment. Bank Balance £23,556.63.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No issues in the October Risk Inspection Reports.

CORRESPONDENCE: Agreed to welcome a street-trading application for the A4129 Thame Road lay-by, subject to litter and fly-tipping responsibilities. JM. No objection was raised to BT's proposal to remove the village's sole, low-usage phone box. Agreed the Council could not comment on a possible Multi Use Games Area at the playing field (linked to the Rose Farm application) ahead of the 23rd November Neighbourhood Plan public meeting; to be revisited in December. JM. A BCC invitation to a 12th December local government reorganisation event was noted.

9/ COUNCILLOR VACANCY BY CO-OPTION

Deferred to the next meeting to allow a second candidate to be interviewed. JM

10/ VILLAGE HALL ACCOUNTS

Received for members' consideration (Cllrs Rogers and Walker having declared an interest and taken no part).

11/ FUTURE OF LONGWICK SPORTS CLUB

Following the Club's Extraordinary General Meeting, members were informed it had voted to close, with assets (garage, football posts, floodlights, cricket nets) passed to four Trustees, who sought to hire the field's use to a responsible local group. Agreed two Parish Council members would meet two Village Hall Management Committee members to discuss the way forward. VM

12/ PLAYING FIELD USE POLICY

Adopted (5–1 abstention) a formal policy governing outside organisations' use of the playing field (reproduced as an appendix), covering risk assessment/insurance, vehicle weight limits, revocation rights, and hirer liability. JM

13/ PARISH COUNCIL LOGO

Agreed to use the heading "Longwick-cum-Ilmer Parish Council" with a walnut tree logo, a resident having offered to design it. JM

14/ NEIGHBOURHOOD PLAN UPDATE

A public meeting was arranged for 23rd November, with presentations from WDC's Penelope Tollitt, Cllr Richards, Louise Thomas (Thomas Design and Consultation Ltd) and Mark Matthews (Thames Water), followed by public questions and a parish vote on whether to continue with a new Neighbourhood Plan.

15/ PRINCES RISBOROUGH LOCAL PLAN UPDATE

A small buffer would be retained at the Crownridge site between Longwick and Princes Risborough.

16/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

No objections: 16/07622/LBC (Dock Farm, Meadle); 16/07742/FUL (The Cobblers, Meadle); 16/07898/FUL (36 Walnut Tree Lane). Approved: 16/06860/FUL (Land adjacent Home Farm — 3 dwellings); 16/07247/FUL (5 Lower Icknield Way); 16/07515/FUL (West Barn, Thame Road). No TPO made: 16/07610/CTR (Meadle Cottage).

17/ EXCLUSION OF PUBLIC AND PRESS

Agreed, under Section 12 of the 1960 Act, to discuss the preferred maintenance contractor in closed session.

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 20th December 2016 at 7.30pm. The meeting closed at 8.55pm.
