PRESENT: Cllr Mrs Valerie McPherson BEM (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Jane Rogers, Rolf van Apeldoorn and Brian Richards, Clerk Susanne Griffiths, Deputy Clerk Jayne Mylchreest, 3 members of the public. Held at the Princes Centre, Clifford Road, Princes Risborough.

Apologies were received from Cllr Whitworth. The Minutes of 19th July 2016 were approved and signed. No interests were declared.

Matters arising: the Tesco Grant playground equipment installation was expected to start in August, taking about a week with security fencing allowing the play area to potentially remain open. Cllr Richards reported Neighbourhood Plan costs incurred to date and around £1,000 identified in potential grant funding, with a decision on borrowing to fund the project to be an agenda item at the next meeting.

5/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

No objection: 16/06835/FUL (Ray Farmhouse, Thame Road — barn conversion); 16/07071/FUL (Holly Tree Barn, Owlswick Lane); 16/06994/FUL (Folly Cottage, Ilmer Lane, Ilmer).

Objected: 16/06673/OUT (Land south of Rose Farm, Thame Road — 65 dwellings), on the grounds that permission had recently been granted for another 160-house development with existing infrastructure already under pressure, that the proposed pedestrian/cycle link to Walnut Tree Lane was not a convincing substitute for safe local Thame Road conditions, that further eastward extension toward the railway line was a real risk, and that the far-reaching vista from Whiteleaf Cross would be impaired.

Recommended approval (3–2 following a defeated counter-proposal to object on curtilage grounds): 16/06860/FUL (Land adjacent Home Farm, Thame Road — 3 dwellings replacing a livery). Recommended approval (4–1 abstention): 16/06864/OUT (Land rear of Hazeldene Lodge, Thame Road — one dwelling).

Recommended refusal: 16/06375/FUL (Land north/rear of The Old Pigeons, Thame Road — 5-bed dwelling), as an overdevelopment overbearing to neighbouring properties, sitting outside the building line, and detrimental to wildlife if trees were removed.

Approved (noted): 16/06682/CTR (Bank Farm, Meadle — no TPO); 16/06893/MIN (Three Cottages, Stockwell Lane — non-material amendment).

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 20th September 2016 at 7.30pm. The meeting closed at 4.50pm.
