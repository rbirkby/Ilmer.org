Meeting of the Longwick-cum-Ilmer Parish Council held in the Schoolroom, Longwick on Tuesday March 29th 1949. at 7.45 pm. To read and confirm Minutes of last meeting. To appoint a Trustee to Monks Risborough Parochial Charities. Consideration of dangerous ditch, Ilmer. Consideration of replacement of Road Sign at Bar Lane. Payment of accounts. Present:- Councillors W. J. Harper (Chairman) A. J. Goodchild. F. Williams E. J. Girling D. Claydon, D. M. Walker and the Clerk. [inserted: H. W Lewis]

[Margin: Minutes] The Minutes of the last meeting were read and confirmed.

[Margin: appointment of E. J. Girling as a Trustee to M. R. Parochial Charities] Proposed by Councillor D. M Walker seconded by Councillor H. W Lewis, that Mr. E. J. Girling be re-appointed a Trustee to Monks Risborough Parochial Charities (his previous term of office having expired).

[Margin: Dangerous ditch at Ilmer] The Clerk was asked to communicate with the Divisional Highways Surveyor, drawing his attention to a dangerous ditch at Ilmer, and to ask [inserted: that] steps be taken to pipe the section of the ditch from a point near the Village Hall, to connect up with the existing drain near the Cottages, a distance of about 15 yds, also to ask if he would arrange

[Margin: Direction Sign. Bar Lane] for a Direction Sign to be erected at the east end of Bar Lane, Longwick indicating the direction of Longwick, Monks Risborough. Owlswick & Ford.

Proposed by Councillor Lewis seconded by Councillor Claydon that Cheques be drawn in payment of the following accounts:- Correspondent Longwick C of E. School for use of Room for meetings (One year) 10/-d. Robert Bailey, One Quarters Salary as Clerk to 25/3/49 £1/5/- Postages One year 9/9d Total Cheque to Robert Bailey £1/14/9d Carried W. J Harper May 30 /49
