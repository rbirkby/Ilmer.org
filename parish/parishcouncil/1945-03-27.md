Meeting of the Longwick-cum-Ilmer Parish Council held in the Schoolroom, Longwick on Tuesday March 27th 1945 at 7.45 pm. To read and confirm Minutes of last meeting. To appoint a Trustee to Monks Risborough Charities. Payment of accounts. To fix date and time for Annual Meeting of the Parish Council. Present:- Councillors Wm A. Walker (Chairman) H. S. Pope E. J. Girling. F. Williams. W. J. Harper. A. J. Goodchild, and the Clerk.

[Margin: Absence of H. W Lewis] Councillor H. W Lewis was unable to attend owing to illness.

[Margin: Minutes] The Minutes of the last meeting were read and confirmed.

[Margin: Appointment of E. J. Girling as a Trustee to Monks Risboro' Charities] Proposed by Councillor F. Williams, seconded by Councillor H. S. Pope that Mr E. J. Girling be re-appointed a Trustee to Monks Risborough Charities, his previous term of office having expired. Carried.

[Margin: Payment of accounts] Proposed by Councillor H. S. Pope seconded by Councillor E. J. Girling that the following accounts be passed for payment:- Robert Bailey. One Quarters Salary as Clerk to 25/3/45 £1/5/- Postages (One year) 6/11d Total Cheque to Bailey £1/11/11. Correspondent Longwick C of E. School. for use of a room for meetings. 10/-d. Carried.

[Margin: Date of Annual Meeting of Parish Council] It was agreed that the Annual Meeting of the Parish Council be held at the Schoolroom, Longwick on Tuesday April 24th 1945 at 8 pm. Wm A Walker
