8/ CORRESPONDENCE

Correspondence: items of general interest on the table.

Correspondence to be discussed:

- Response from Bucks CC re: Consultation on the review of the speed limit sign. The Parish Council was disappointed with the response and notes the comments.
- Message in a bottle. Cllr V McPherson informed the members of the purpose of the scheme. It basically consists of a plastic bottle containing a leaflet on which you can write your emergency contact details and medical history. The bottle is then placed in the fridge. It is a piece of partnership working, and the fire & ambulance service are well aware of it. It is simply a time saving, and life saving idea. The Neighbourhood Watch Scheme has a supply of the bottles and would be pleased for any resident to contact them concerning the scheme.

9/ A REPORT FROM THE WEBSITE WORKING GROUP

Cllrs Mrs V McPherson and Mrs J Rogers gave a report on the progress to date of establishing a website for the Parish Council. They advised that up to three additional pages might be required at a cost of £15.00 per page. All present agreed to the additional cost up to a limit of £45.00. The Clerk is requested to contact the local organisations that will be listed on the website to seek their agreement. Cllr Mrs J Rogers will supply the names.

10/ DOG FOULING PROBLEM WITHIN THE PARISH

Cllr Mrs V McPherson has contacted the Dog Warden who has agreed to visit the parish on a more regular basis. The concerns of a resident about the problem of dog fouling on the pavements in Longwick has been passed to the Environmental Health Team at W.D.C. under reference number CT20090216_0000000025. Cllr Mrs V McPherson agreed to include another article in the newsletter to highlight the issue.

11/ THE PLANS FOR A TRAFFIC ISLAND BEING INSTALLED ON THE THAME ROAD

The Parish Council were concerned to learn of plans for a new traffic island to be installed on the Thame Road by reading an article in the Princes Risborough Area Newsletter published by the B.C.C. The Clerk is requested to write to the B.C.C. advising that the Parish Council feel very strongly about being included in discussions and plans for any improvements in the Parish and requesting an update on the timescales for the project and information concerning the road closure mentioned in the Princes Risborough Area Newsletter.

12/ APPOINTMENT OF THE INTERNAL AUDITOR

All present agreed that Mr Geoff Weir is appointed as the internal auditor for the financial year 2008/2009.

14/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

Progress on applications already considered:

- 08/07710/CLE: Maplefield, Owlswick Lane, Owlswick. Certificate of existing lawfulness for the continued occupation of dwelling in breach of condition. Permitted.
- 09/05221/CTREE: Appletrees, Meadle Village Road, Meadle. Reduce crowns by 3-4m to Silver Birch Trees. Application withdrawn.
- 09/05046/FUL: The Old Forge, Thame Road, Longwick. Construction of first floor extension and detached double garage. Permission Refused.

New Applications to be considered:

- 08/07786/FUL: Horsenden Manor, Horsenden Lane, Princes Risborough. Amendment to original application. Erection of a detached four bay garage with separate room above. The Parish Council has no objections.
- 09/05401/MDS: Maplefield, Owlswick Lane, Owlswick. Application to discharge a S106 agreement relating to the use of a building as a single family dwelling. The Parish Council has no objections.
- 09/05170/FUL: Land at Corner of Lower Icknield Way and Mill Lane, Monks Risborough. Construction of 4 x timber stables and tack room, feed box and hay store. The Parish Council recommend that the application is refused for the following reasons: the construction of 4 x timber stables and tack room, feed box and hay store is an over development of the site and is considered to be too many stables for the amount of land available. The Parish Council is concerned that the stables will be used for commercial use which is effectively seeking the change of use of the land. The additional stables erode the openness of the countryside and are detrimental to the open character and appearance of this part of the countryside. If the application is likely to be approved, the Parish Council would like the application to be referred to the Development Control Committee to be determined and a restriction preventing commercial use and lighting to be attached to the permission.
- 08/06150/FUL: Apple Tree Cottage, Thame Road, Longwick. Amendment to original application for the demolition of existing dwelling and erection of 2 link-detached dwellings with car ports attached, creation of new access and parking area. The Parish Council has no observations on this application.
