PRESENT: Cllr Mrs Valerie McPherson BEM (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Sally Whitworth, Jane Rogers, Rolf van Apeldoorn and Brian Richards, Clerk Susanne Griffiths, Deputy Clerk Jayne Mylchreest, 2 members of the public.

No apologies were received. The Minutes of 17th January 2017 were approved and signed. No interests were declared. Cllr van Apeldoorn asked for a Neighbourhood Plan update; none was available. Cllr Bendyshe-Brown was unable to attend.

FINANCE: Payments limited to salaries, pension and risk assessment. Bank Balance £18,861.54.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No issues in the January Risk Inspection Reports; Cllr Whitworth to arrange hedge trimming. SW

CORRESPONDENCE: Agreed playing field access for the Village Hall's annual fete (28th–29th April) and a school "Race for Life" (11th June), subject to standard documentation. JM. A letter of congratulation was agreed for Longwick School's regional road-safety award. JM. A RARA petition on WDC's Local Plan housing numbers was noted, but the Council felt unable to assist further, having no office. A resident's Neighbourhood Plan query was held pending WDC advice following the Government's Housing White Paper. VM. Cllr van Apeldoorn agreed to attend a WDC Modernising Local Government session on unitary reorganisation. SG/RVA

9/ TERMS OF THE SPORTS CLUB ASSET TRANSFER

Agreed to transfer the assets in their entirety without conditions, with the Clerk to liaise with the Trustees on a legal document. SG

10/ VILLAGE FETE STALL

Agreed the Parish Council would have a stall at the 29th April fete, arranged by Cllr van Apeldoorn. RVA

11/ PRINCES RISBOROUGH CHARITY TRUSTEE

Appointed Cllr Whitworth as representative for the Princes Risborough and Monks Risborough Charities. JM/SW

12/ PLAY EQUIPMENT FUNDRAISING

Nothing to report since the last meeting.

13/ INSURANCE POLICY REVIEW

Deferred pending the Sports Club asset transfer decision. SG

14/ WEBSITE DIRECTORY UPDATE

Agreed to remove the defunct Cricket, Sports and Football Clubs and add/update entries for the pre-school, Longwick Choir, the school, the WI, the playing field contact, and the Ilmer, Owlswick and Bledlow churches.

15/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

To abide by the Arboriculturalist's decision: 17/05227/CTREE (St Peter's Church, Ilmer). No objections: 17/05144/FUL (Ray Farmhouse, Thame Road — alternative scheme). Approved: 16/08104/FUL (Old Orchard, Thame Road); 16/07997/FUL (8 Lower Icknield Way).

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 21st March 2017 at 7.30pm. The meeting closed at 8.55pm.
