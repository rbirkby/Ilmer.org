Longwick Cum Ilmer Parish Council meeting Held in School Room Longwick Sept 29 1954 Present Councillors W. Harper Chairman D. Claydon E. Girling, F M Walker, F Williams, & the Clerk To Read & Confirm minutes of Last meeting matters arising, Correspondence, Payment of Accounts Any Other Business

The minutes of the Last meeting Where Read & Confirmed

[Margin: Ilmer Water Supply] The Chairman Volunteers To Contact the People Concerned at Ilmer. after Receiving the Decision arrived at by the R. D. C.

[Margin: Mrs Hamilton Jenkins] It Was Agreed That Councillor Girling interview Mrs Hamilton Jenkins at Meadle. Re Correspondence Re Pond..

It Was agreed That the Follany accounts be Paid [Margin: Payment of Accounts] It Was Proposed D Claydon Sec F. Williams That the Following accounts be Paid Clerks Salary £2 . 10 - 0 Shaw & Sons 13s . 2 . 0 municipal mutual Insurance Coy 10/0. W J Harper Nov 10 / 54
