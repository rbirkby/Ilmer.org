LONGWICK-CUM-ILMER PARISH COUNCIL Meeting held in Schoolroom Longwick 7-30 pm Thursday 22nd March 62

[Margin: Present] Cllr Harper, Walker, Mrs Wootton Mrs Brock, Tappin Apology for absence received from Cllr Gomme.

[Margin: Mins] The minutes of the last meeting were read and confirmed

[Margin: Cllr Claridge] The chairman, Cllr Harper requested the Cllr members to stand for one minute as a mark of respect for the late Cllr Claridge

[Margin: Election of Councillor] It was proposed by Cllr Mrs Brock and seconded by Cllr Walker that Mr D. S. Leuw of Ilmer be elected to replace the late Cllr Claridge Carried

[Margin: Assoc of Parish Councils] The clerk read a letter requesting the annual dinner of the Assoc of Parish Councils. Unfortunately no members of the Council were in a position to attend and the clerk was so instructed

[Margin: Planning Permission] The Council discussed the lack of information supplied by the WRDC to Parish Councils re

planning permission etc within the Parish.

[Margin: Accounts] Clerks salary Jan Feb March 62 £3.0.0 Ptge 1/9 Meeting closed 8.25 pm W J Harper 28/5/62
