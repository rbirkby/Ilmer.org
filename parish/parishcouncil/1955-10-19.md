Longwick Cum Ilmer Parish Council meeting Held in School Room Longwick Oct 19th 1955 Prensnt Councillor Harper Chairman, Councillor Walker, Williams & the Clerk

To Read & Confirm minutes of Last meetg, matters arresing Correspondence, Chairmans Report on meeting With Surveyor To Elect a Trustee For the Following Princes Risborough Charities Poor Elotments, Lesitices, minches, Chibnalls & Smith's, any Other Business

[Margin: Minutes] The minutes where Read & Confirmed

[Margin: Trustee] Pro Councillor Harper Se Councillor Williams That Councillor F m Walker be appointed Trustee For the said Charities Carried

[Margin: Chairman's Report] The Chairman Gave a Detailed Report on meeting With Surveyer, hoping the Results of the meeting will be an Improvement. to matters Discussed Carried

[Margin: Tresaurer] It Was agreed that Mr Symons Bank manager Lloyds Bank be appointed to take over Tresaurer. the Post being Vacant owing to the Resingnation of Mr Bare

[Margin: Footpath] Pro Councillor Walker Sec Councillor William that the Council Have no Objections to the Diversion of Footpath at Brook Farm Meadle

[Margin: Any Oth Business Rays Farm] It Was agreed the Chairman Get in touch With the Surveyor. over the state of the gutter on the Road Side outside Rays Farm

[Margin: Payment of Accounts] Proposed Councillor Williams Seconded Councillor Walker That the Following accounts Be Paid Clerks Salary Due September 24th £2 . 10 . 0 Carried

W J Harper 15/2/56
