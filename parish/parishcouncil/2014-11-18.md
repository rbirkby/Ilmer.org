PRESENT: Cllr Mrs Valerie McPherson (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Graham Walters, Mrs Jane Rogers, Rolf van Apeldoorn, Brian Richards and Sally Whitworth, Mrs Susanne Griffiths (Clerk), County Cllr Bill Bendyshe-Brown.

No apologies were received. The Minutes of 21st October 2014 were approved and signed.

Cllr Bendyshe-Brown reported he had toured the parish with the Local Area Technician on flood-vulnerable areas and winter works; funding for the Devolved Services trial had been agreed from 1st April 2015. On the school footpath, both BCC Rights of Way and Transport for Bucks had declined to adopt it as public highway given its poor surface; consistent with prior legal advice, all present agreed to keep pressing the County Council to keep the path open rather than accept liability themselves, and asked Cllr Bendyshe-Brown to explore funding options. BBB

Matters arising: a letter had been sent to Risborough Rangers on limiting numbers and hours, with no response yet. Dog fouling signs were being placed around the village. RvA

FINANCE: Routine payments (grass cutting £442.88, WDALC subscription £10.00, postage £132.96, Clerk's November salary £433.37, Risk Assessment £15.18, Cllr Whitworth's travel expenses £11.45) were examined and signed off. Bank Balance £18,912.24.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No medium/high risk issues. A car had damaged a Playing Field fence post; Cllr Ian Walker to obtain quotations for immediate repair and for full fence replacement next year. IW

ROADS: The Clerk to report a bent sign near Bridleway 14 and the Bell Crescent road sign/supports needing replacement. SG

CORRESPONDENCE: Agreed the Council would be interested in managing two WDC-maintained green spaces for a fee, but not taking ownership. SG. A resident's dog-fouling complaint was noted as outside the Council's jurisdiction, with advice to report to the WDC dog warden. A School Travel Plan Team letter on poor Longwick pavements prompted the Clerk to investigate Local Area Forum funding. SG/BBB. George Harper's offer to provide the Carol Service Christmas tree was accepted with thanks. IW. Agreed to apply for a small Woodland Trust tree pack (30 saplings) to plant a small copse in the Playing Field, with the Scouts asked to help plant them (6 votes to 1 abstention). SG/RvA

10/ THE FUTURE OF LONGWICK CONSULTATION

Cllr Graham Walters reported on progress; councillors agreed to meet informally to agree wording if a response to the second draft consultation report was needed before the December meeting.

11/ MOBILE VEHICLE ACTIVATED SIGN

Having investigated installation and maintenance costs, Cllr Val McPherson proposed the Council not take on ownership of a mobile VAS due to prohibitive cost; carried.

12/ 2015 MEETING DATES

Agreed for a 7.45pm start (except the Annual Parish Meeting at 7.30pm/Council Meeting at 8.00pm on 21st April, and the Annual Meeting of the Parish Council on 19th May): 20 Jan, 17 Feb, 17 Mar, 21 Apr, 19 May, 16 Jun, 21 Jul, no meeting in August, 15 Sep, 20 Oct, 17 Nov, 15 Dec.

13/ DEVOLVED SERVICES UPDATE

Cllrs Sally Whitworth and the Clerk agreed to attend a BCC update meeting on the Parish Devolution proposal on 4th December. SW/SG

14/ COMMUNICATIONS QUESTIONNAIRE RESPONSES

142 replies had been received to date; the Clerk to report response numbers by area at the next meeting. SG

15/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

New applications: 14/07841/LBC, Woodbine Cottage, Chestnut Way (listed building consent for a single storey extension — to abide by the Conservation Officer's decision); 14/07729/FUL, Hunters Lodge, Bar Lane, Owlswick (conservatory to rear) — no objections.

Refused: 14/06582/FUL, the Bumpers Farm, Ilmer Lane, Ilmer solar farm application.

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 16th December 2014 at 8.00pm. The meeting closed at 9.30pm.
