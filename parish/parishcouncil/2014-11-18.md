4/ A REPORT FROM CLLR BENDYSHE-BROWN (BCC) ON MATTERS CONCERNING LONGWICK-CUM-ILMER PARISH COUNCIL

Cllr Bill Bendyshe-Brown reported that he had visited the parish with the Local Area Technician to identify areas vulnerable to flooding, take preventative action and agree work to be carried out over the winter months by the TFB gang. He was pleased to advise that the funding for the Devolved Services trial had been agreed with effect from 1st April 2015.

Cllr Bill Bendyshe-Brown advised that the Rights of Way team have considered the footpath leading to the school and will not accept responsibility as the surface is in a poor condition and would not wish (or would be able to) take on such a maintenance liability. They have consulted colleagues in Transport for Bucks and they too have stated they would be unwilling to adopt it as public highway. The Parish Council consulted a solicitor last year concerning the footpath and was advised against accepting liability for the footpath but he suggested applying pressure on the County Council to keep the path open as he did not consider that it is the Parish Council's responsibility to maintain a school path. All present agreed that every effort must be made to keep the path open for the residents and parents of the school children to use and requested that Cllr Bill Bendyshe-Brown obtain quotes and possible avenues for funding for the maintenance of paths. BBB

9/ CORRESPONDENCE

Potential Devolution of Green Space management within Longwick-cum-Ilmer Parish. All present agreed that the Parish Council would be interested in managing the two areas currently maintained by W.D.C. for a fee but would not consider transferring the land to the Parish Council's ownership at the present time. The Clerk is requested to advise W.D.C accordingly. SG

E-mail from resident re dog fouling. An e-mail from a resident has been received regarding dog owners whose pets had fouled outside the resident's house. They do not feel that it is their duty or have the time to clear the mess and wish to bring the matter to the Parish Council's attention. All present sympathised with the resident but it is outside the Parish Council's remit as it has no jurisdiction in this area. It is suggested that the resident obtains the names of the dog owners and the dates of the incidents and reports the information to the dog warden at W.D.C. who will be able to prosecute the owners. SG

Letter concerning the poor condition of pavements in Longwick Village. A letter has been received from the School Travel Plan Team requesting that the Parish Council apply for funding from the Local Area Forum for repairs to the footpaths in Longwick. The Councillors are aware of the poor condition of the paths and have brought it to the attention of the County Councillor. The Clerk agreed to investigate if funding for the repair of the paths is available through the Local Area Forum. SG/BBB

Offer to collect and install the Christmas tree for the Carol Service. George Harper has offered to collect and install a Christmas tree for the Carol Service. All present agreed and thank George for his kind offer. Cllr I Walker agreed to liaise with George. IW

E-mail from Lynne Maddocks, Community Engagement Officer, Aylesbury Vale District Council. AVDC has informed the Parish Council that The Woodland Trust has 3000 free tree packs to give away to schools, community and youth groups. The free tree packs are available for planting on a publicly accessible site. The packs available are; Small (30 saplings); Medium (105 saplings); and Large (420 saplings). Packs will be awarded on a first come first serve basis. All applicants need to do is find a suitable site and supply the volunteer planters and tree protection. Cllr Val McPherson proposed that the Parish Council apply for a small pack to plant in the Playing Field as a small copse. Cllr Rolf van Apeldoorn agreed to ask the Scouts if they would volunteer to plant the saplings. A vote was taken and the motion carried with 6 votes for and 1 abstention. The Clerk is requested to apply on behalf of the Parish Council. SG/RvA
