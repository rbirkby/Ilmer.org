The Annual Meeting of the Longwick-cum-Ilmer Parish Council was held in the Schoolroom, Longwick on Monday April 24th 1939 at 8 pm. To appoint a Chairman and Vice-Chairman for the ensuing Year. To read and confirm Minutes of last meeting. To appoint two representatives to serve on the Valuation Committee of the Wycombe R.D. Council for the ensuing Year. To consider a letter from Wycombe R.D. Council re provision of Trenches. Present:- Councillors Wm A. Walker (Chairman), H. S. Pope, A. J. Goodchild, W. J. Harper, F. Williams, E. J. Girling, H. W. Lewis and Mr Robert Bailey (Clerk).

[Margin: Chairman] Proposed by Councillor Harper seconded by Councillor Williams that Councillor Wm A. Walker be re-appointed Chairman for the ensuing year. Carried unan

[Margin: Vice-Chairman] Proposed by Councillor Williams seconded by Councillor Lewis that Councillor H. S. Pope, be re-appointed Vice-Chairman for the ensuing year. Carried

[Margin: Minutes] Minutes of last meeting read and confirmed.

[Margin: Representatives on Rating Valuation Committee] Proposed by Councillor Girling seconded by Councillor Pope that Councillors A. J. Goodchild and F. Williams be re-appointed to serve as Representatives for the Parish on the Rating and Valuation Committee of the Wycombe R.D. Council for the ensuing year. Carried

[Margin: Provision of Trenches] A letter was read from the Clerk of the Wycombe R.D. Council relating to the provision of trenches, in the event of an emergency arising. The Parish Council were invited to co-operate in the selection of suitable sites, the formation of squads of volunteers to undertake the

digging of the trenches, a survey of the tools available locally, and the nomination of a person to be in control of trench digging, as and when an emergency arose. After consideration of the letter, it was agreed that Councillor Girling should make a survey of suitable sites in the Meadle area, Councillor Harper, to survey Ilmer, and Councillors Goodchild and Pope to survey Longwick and report to a later meeting. Proposed by Councillor Girling, seconded by Councillor Harper, that Councillor H. S. Pope be nominated to be in control of trench digging operations. Carried

It was felt that the present time was not opportune for the formation of squads of volunteers, but that should an emergency arise, no difficulty would be experienced in getting the necessary labour for the digging of the trenches, also that sufficient tools would be available.

[Margin: Dividends on 2 1/2 % Consolidated Stock to be paid direct to Lloyds Bank Ltd] A form of request for payment of Dividends on the 2 1/2 % Consolidated Stock, direct to Lloyds Bank Ltd was duly executed. The Dividends to be credited to the Parish Council's account. Wm A Walker Feb 26 1940
