Longwick Cum Ilmer Parish Council meeting held in School Room Longwick June 22nd 1955

Present Councillors Harper Chairman, Councillors Williams, Claridge, Claydon, & the Clerk. To Read & Confirm minutes of Last meetg matters arising, Payment of Accounts, Any Other Business

[Margin: Minutes] The minutes of Last meetg Where Read & Confirmed

[Margin: School Business] Councillor Williams Reported on the School Business after Discussion this Was left to the Education Authorities

[Margin: Payment of Accounts] Pro Councillor Williams Seconded Councillor Claydon that the Following accounts be Paid WRDC Election of Councillors £3-17-8. Hon Secretary of Buckingham association of Parish Councils £1-10-0, Clerks Salary £2-10-0, Audit Stamp 10/0. Cheverton & Laidler £2-5-11 Carried

W J Harper Aug 10/55
