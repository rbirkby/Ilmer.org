6/ CAPITAL ITEMS AND COMMUNITY FACILITIES

A. Playing Field. Nothing to report.

B. Children's play area. Nothing to report.

C. Community facilities. Cllr I Walker informed the members of an Art class to be held at the Scout Building on Mondays between 3pm and 6pm, and that there was no parking facility specifically for the Scout Building. Concerns were expressed that attendees would use the Village Hall car park, which is currently used by parents of the school at the same time, causing congestion. All present agreed that the matter should be referred to the Village Hall Committee by Cllrs J Rogers and I Walker.

7/ ROADS, VERGES AND RIGHTS OF WAY

Further to the request for the grass cutting schedule for Longwick from B.C.C., it was noted there is no mention of grass cutting between the Railway Bridge and Summerleys Road on the B4009, and residents in this area feel quite strongly about their omission from regular grass cutting. The Local Area Technician advised that this area is part of a Rural grass cutting schedule and is only cut 3 times a season. All present agreed that the Clerk send a letter to the Highways Dept requesting inclusion in the normal schedule, copying County Cllr P Rogerson for his support. The Clerk is requested to obtain a progress report on the replacement sign post with only one sign, which should have three, previously reported under reference number 341490.

External Auditors Certificate and Opinion on the audit of the annual return year ended 31st March 2012. Noted.

10. PLANS FOR A VILLAGE GREEN

Cllr I Walker proposed that the Parish Council lease the land owned by the District Council at the junction of Bell Crescent/Chestnut Way/Thame Road in Longwick to provide a village green for the residents. He further proposed that the site be scarified and drilled with seed as per the playing field, to provide a green comprising an open grassed area with a low post and chain boundary fence inside an open ditch surround with existing trees, accessed by a sleeper bridge off Chestnut Way, incorporating park benches for seating as appropriate. After discussion, Cllr G O'Neill proposed that the Clerk approach WDC with this proposal to ascertain if they would lease the land and, if so, the cost of the lease and estimated solicitor's fees in preparing it. All present agreed.
