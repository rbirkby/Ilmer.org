Longwick Cum Ilmer Parish Council meeting Held Schoolroom Longwick March 20th 1958

Present Councillors W. J. Harper Chairman, Claydon, Claridge Tapping, Williams, F M Walker & the Clerk.

[Margin: Minutes] The minutes of the Last meetg Where Read & Confirmed

[Margin: Matters Arresing] The Clerk Reported the matter of the Stile on Footpath no 3 Was already in the Hands of the R D C & Would be Done as soon as Possible.

[Margin: Road Safty Circular] it was agreed to accept Posters From the Road Safty Committee & Display in the Village

[Margin: Issuing a Precept] Pro Councillor F M Walker Sec Councillor Williams That we issue Precept For the sum of £20-0-0 For the Year ending march 31st 1959

[Margin: Payment of Accounts] Pro Councillor F M Walker Sec Councillor O Tapping That the Following accounts be Paid £6-0-0 Clerks Salary 3/0 Clerks Bond 8/4 Postage 10/0 Rent of School Room

[Margin: Any Other Business] It was agreed the Clerk get in touch With Road Foreman Re the state of the Footpath Which is being Fouled by Mr Williams Cattle

[Margin: Retirement of Clerk] The Clerk Gave the meetg notice of Giving up the Clerkship on June the 24th 1958

meeting Closed 8.30pm W J Harper May 15/58
