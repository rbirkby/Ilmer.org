PRESENT: Cllr Mrs Valerie McPherson BEM (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Jane Rogers, Rolf van Apeldoorn, Graham Walters, Sally Whitworth and Brian Richards, Deputy Clerk Jayne Mylchreest, 6 members of the public.

No apologies were received. The Minutes of 16th February 2016 were approved and signed. No interests were declared.

4/ ACTION FOLLOWING THE NEIGHBOURHOOD PLAN EXAMINER'S REPORT

The Examiner's Report required alterations to meet the Basic Conditions; WDC's view was that, if adopted, the Plan would no longer achieve sustainable development. Four options were considered: (1) accept the Examiner's recommendations and proceed to referendum — not recommended, as WDC considered the Plan would no longer meet its aims; (2) reject the Examiner's Report — considered legally difficult without strong grounds; (3) rewrite, re-consult and resubmit for examination — rejected as costly and of questionable merit given the emerging district-wide Local Plan; (4) withdraw the Plan — recommended, meaning planning applications would continue to be assessed against the 2004 Local Plan, 2008 Core Strategy and National Planning Policy Framework, with Neighbourhood Plan policies incorporated into the new Local Plan as far as possible.

Cllr Richards proposed adopting option 4 (withdrawal); seconded by Cllr Whitworth; carried. The Clerk to write to WDC. JM. Agreed to hold an Open Forum public meeting before the 15th March Parish Council meeting to explain the decision, with Cllr Walters to invite WDC officers and to write an explanatory newsletter article. GW

5/ RESPONSE TO PLANNING APPLICATION 15/08455/OUT

Address: OS Parcels 6232 & 7428, Thame Road, Longwick. Proposal: outline application (access only) for up to 43 dwellings, public open space and landscaping. The Council objected on the grounds of: (1) inadequate non-car access without a co-ordinated peak-time bus service and cycle routes to Princes Risborough station; (2) the site falling outside the settlement boundary with no current planned approach to develop beyond it; (3) the risk of Longwick being overrun with uncoordinated planning applications lacking infrastructure improvements such as new road junctions; (4) flooding and waste water capacity concerns; and (5) a request for developer-funded noise mitigation at source for the nearby pet hotel, to protect the existing business, should the application be approved.

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 15th March 2016 at 7.30pm. The meeting closed at 8.35pm.
