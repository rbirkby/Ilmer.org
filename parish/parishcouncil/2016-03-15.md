PRESENT: Cllr Mrs Valerie McPherson BEM (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Jane Rogers, Graham Walters, Sally Whitworth and Brian Richards, Clerk Mrs Susanne Griffiths, Clerical Assistant Jayne Mylchreest, 3 members of the public.

Apologies were received and accepted from Cllr Rolf van Apeldoorn. The Minutes of 24th February 2016 were approved and signed. No interests were declared.

Matters arising: WDC confirmed no Tree Preservation Order barrier to pollarding the trees at the pond adjacent to Ilmer Meadow, Ilmer Lane; agreed to proceed. JM. Cllr Bendyshe-Brown (BCC) was unable to attend.

FINANCE: Payments including salaries, pension, risk assessment, extraordinary-meeting hall hire, PAYE, the remainder of the Tree Survey work (£718.80), litter-picking equipment, bench/fence repairs, postage/mileage backdated to October 2014, Neighbourhood Plan Update printing, and hall hire for the Neighbourhood Plan public forum. Bank Balance £17,305.64.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No medium or high risk issues in the February Playing Field or play area Risk Inspection Reports.

ROADS: The Clerk to report an advertising board tied to a Longwick roundabout sign, a large pothole at "Restawhile", Owlswick, fly tipped tyres in the B4009 Chinnor Road lay-by, a fallen road-name sign on Stockwell Lane, and a hanging directional sign near The Three Horseshoes, Stockwell Lane. JM

CORRESPONDENCE: An invitation to the WDC Chairman's Annual Reception was noted. LOGS thanked the Council for supporting the "Clean for the Queen" litter pick, with a further pick arranged for 22nd March. A resident's query on B4009 signage/grass cutting was referred to BCC/WDC. Agreed no objection to the Chinnor Neighbourhood Plan pre-submission document. SG

10/ NEIGHBOURHOOD PLAN STEERING GROUP

Following the Council's public meeting of 14th March explaining the Plan's withdrawal (agreed at the 24th February extraordinary meeting), members thanked the Steering Group for its work; Cllr Walters agreed to complete the Groundwork report and Cllr McPherson to thank the co-opted members. The Working Group was formally dissolved.

12/ GRANT POLICY AND APPLICATION FORM

Adopted, to be reviewed annually at the November estimates meeting.

13/ FUTURE AUDITOR APPOINTMENTS (SAAA)

Agreed to remain an opted-in authority under the Smaller Authorities Audit Appointments scheme.

14/ RESPONSE TO THE RISBOROUGH TOWN DRAFT PLAN CONSULTATION

Agreed to support the Draft Plan's protection of the gap between Longwick-cum-Ilmer and Princes Risborough, its new road route, and its proposed bus service extension into the parish. SG

15/ TREE SURVEY REVIEW

Cllrs Walker and van Apeldoorn to site-visit and report on progress against the Tree Survey ahead of the April meeting. IW/RvA

16/ FINANCIAL REGULATIONS REVIEW

Adopted the revised Regulations reflecting the £25,000 contract-tendering threshold.

17/ FLOWER CONTAINERS AT VILLAGE ENTRANCES

Agreed not to proceed, the quotation being cost-prohibitive; to be reconsidered at the November estimates meeting. JM

18/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

New: 16/05611/FUL (OS Parcel 8955, Askett Village Lane — two chicken sheds, a chattel and a workshop, retrospective); Cllr Whitworth to discuss with WDC and draft a response for approval. Approved: 16/05175/CTR and 16/05151/CTREE (Ilmer — no TPO made). Noted: the 14/06965/OUT Land off Barn Road appeal inquiry would sit 12–15 and 19–20 April at WDC offices; Cllr Walters to prepare evidence and Cllr McPherson to speak for the Parish Council. VM/GW/SG

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 19th April 2016 at 7.30pm, preceded by the Annual Parish Meeting at 7.00pm. The meeting closed at 9.45pm.
