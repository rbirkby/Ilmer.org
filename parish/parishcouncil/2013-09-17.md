12/ ON THE ACTION TO BE TAKEN REGARDING THE NOTICE BOARD FOR THE STREAM & FOOTPATH BY SCOUT HUT

Concerns were expressed by the councillors about the risk of litigation should a member of the public fall and injure themselves on the path and whether the erection of a sign would be sufficient to eliminate this potential risk. The Parish Council has the right to relinquish the licence giving 6 months notice to the BCC but then would be unable to influence the future use of the path. Councillors were keen to keep the path open for the community if possible, providing that the Parish Council is not liable for any claims. All present agreed to consult a solicitor for advice in this matter. The Clerk is requested to arrange an appointment. SG
