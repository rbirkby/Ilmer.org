PRESENT: Cllr Val McPherson BEM (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Sally Whitworth, Jane Rogers, Rolf van Apeldoorn and Brian Richards, Clerk Susanne Griffiths, Deputy Clerk Jayne Mylchreest. No members of the public. Preceded by the Annual Parish Meeting at 7.00pm.

No apologies were received. The Minutes of 21st March 2017 were approved and signed. No interests were declared.

Matters arising: EON confirmed the transferred playing field electricity account was on "deemed" out-of-contract pricing, with minimal actual usage (floodlights unused for three years); the Clerk to seek a better rate once the Sports Club asset transfer agreement, then being drafted by the solicitor, was signed. JM/SG

Cllr Bendyshe-Brown reported at length: the Dorrells Road school footpath had been resurfaced and its ditch cleared; Thame Road and Chestnut Way had been resurfaced, with Ilmer Road prepared for resurfacing; Owlswick Road and Stockwell Lane (Meadle) were due for repair; Bell Crescent's pavement was resurfaced, with the Chestnut Way–Walnut Tree Lane school walkway prioritised next; Watery Lane's ditches were cleared, with Bar Lane's flooding ditches to be prioritised ahead of the proposed development there; two further permanent VAS installations were planned along with a Community Speedwatch scheme (volunteers sought); the Wellington House footway dispute had been resolved after BCC Rights of Way intervention, with the developer reinstating encroached land and the footway to become a permanent right of way; and BCC was beginning work with the Council on anticipated HS2 HGV traffic through the village.

FINANCE: Payments including salaries, pension, risk assessment, grass cutting, £75 grants each to St Dunstan's (Owlswick), St Peter's (Ilmer) and Bledlow PCCs, a £50 Tea at Three grant, insurance renewal (£670.61), BALC subscription, newsletter printing, Village Hall hire and dog waste collection. Receipts included £3,102.14 BCC Devolved Services funding. Bank Balance £18,626.76.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No issues in the March Risk Inspection Reports. Agreed to remove a fallen post and relocate the goal post frame. SG

CORRESPONDENCE: The South Oxfordshire Local Plan Second Preferred Options consultation was noted. A resident's request for the Centenary Cottages noise survey report was referred to WDC. JM

9/ NEIGHBOURHOOD PLAN REPORT

The Steering Group would meet on 4th May to discuss WDC's comments on the Housing White Paper's implications, reporting back at the next meeting.

10/ COUNCILLOR VACANCY BY CO-OPTION

Following informal interviews of three candidates, agreed to co-opt Candidate B to fill the vacancy.

11/ PARISH LOGO

Agreed to invite resident suggestions via the newsletter. VM

12/ OPEN SPACE AT THAME ROAD/BAR LANE (NEIGHBOURHOOD PLAN)

Agreed it was too early to adopt the space in principle, pending a developer meeting on terms.

13/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

No objections, noting the Thame Road construction-access agreement: 17/05825/FUL (Bumpers Farm, Ilmer — battery storage compound for National Grid balancing services). No objections: 17/05899/FUL (1 Lyndale, Stockwell Lane, Meadle). Approved: 17/05144/FUL (Ray Farmhouse); no TPO made for 17/05227/CTR (St Peter's Church, Ilmer). Refused: 16/08471/FUL (Owlswick Barn).

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 16th May 2017 (Annual Parish Council Meeting) at 7.30pm. The meeting closed at 9.20pm.
