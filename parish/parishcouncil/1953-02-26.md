Meeting of Longwick Parish Council Held at the School Room Longwick Feb 26th 1953 To Read & Confirm minutes of Last meeting, Correspondence, To appoint Parish Constables For 1953 Payment of accounts. - Any other Business Present Councillors Harper, C, F Williams, Girling, O Tapping & D Claydon & Clerk.,

[Margin: minutes] the minutes of Last meeting where Read & Confirmed

[Margin: Correspondence] Correspondence Was Read, The Chairman Volunteers To Bring the Corres- Re 30 mile an Hour Limit in front of the next Parish Councillor association meeting

[Margin: Parish Constables] Pro Councillor Girling Sec Councillor Claydon and Carried That Messrs. O Goodchild, F Williams, O Tapping E T Rutland & H Goodchild be appointed Parish Constables For the year 1953

[Margin: accounts] Pro Councillor Claydon Sec Councillor F Williams that the Following accounts be Paid. ( 1 ) Correspondent to Longwick C of E Schools 10/- Rent For use of School Room. ( 2 ) Secretary of Village Hall. Committee 10/- For use of Village Hall For a Spl Parish meeting ) 3th Clerk of Council 6/5 Postage & Stationary Carried

[Margin: Any other Business] Pro Councillor Girling Sec Councillor Claydon. That the Clause Re Clerks Salary in minute on Page 171 appertaining to the appointment of Clerk be Deletied owing to misinterpretation of the Rule. The Clerk should not have Been a Paid Servent For the Year 1952 Carried Pro Councillor Williams Sec Councillor Girling That The Clerks Salary be 10£ Per Year From april 1st 1953 Payable in equal instalments of 2-10-0 on the usual quarter Days Carried W J Harper
