meeting of Longwick Parish Council Held at School Room Longwick Jan 29 / 1953 To Read & Confirm minutes of Last meeting matters arising Any other Business Present Councillors Harper Chairman., D Claydon, Owen Tapping, Miss Walker, F Williams & the Clerk.

[Margin: minutes] The minutes of the Last meeting where Read & Confirmed

[Margin: matters arising] a Report Was Given By. Councillors Harper & Miss Walker Re Parish Councillors association meeting Held in November a Report Was also Given By Councillors Harper & Claydon Re Parish Councillors association meeting Held at High Wycombe

[Margin: any other Business] It Was agreed the Clerk write the R-D-C Re the Laying of main Water to Ilmer. also To the County Surveyor. Re the state Condition of the Road. in Front of Mr F Winkers Farm. It Was agreed the Clerks Salary should be left over until the meeting :

[Margin: next meeting] It Was agreed the next meeting should be Held With the Parish meeting of February 26th at 7 30 pm W. J Harper Feb 26/53
