PRESENT: Cllr Mrs Valerie McPherson BEM (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Jane Rogers, Rolf van Apeldoorn, Graham Walters, Sally Whitworth and Brian Richards, Clerk Mrs Susanne Griffiths, Clerical Assistant Mrs Jayne Mylchreest.

No apologies were received. The Minutes of 15th September 2015 were approved and signed. Cllrs Jane Rogers and Ian Walker declared an interest in agenda item 17.

Robert Curtis (WDC Electoral Services & Land Charges Manager) explained the Neighbourhood Plan referendum process and answered questions; detailed guidance notes to follow. Cllr Bendyshe-Brown was unable to attend.

Matters arising: the tree-felling contractor confirmed attendance on 30th October. John Bercow MP had escalated the Right to Buy/Rural Exception Scheme query to the Secretary of State, who welcomed the local input. WDC confirmed no funding was currently available for playground replacement. A newsletter printing quote from Princes Risborough Town Council would be ready for the November estimates meeting. JM

FINANCE: Routine payments including grass cutting, salaries, risk assessment, PAYE, play area swing seats/bushes (£346.80), dog waste/litter collection, £7,140.03 to Thomas Design & Regeneration for Neighbourhood Plan consultation preparation, and the newsletter print run. Bank Balance £25,934.40 after the WDC precept.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No issues in either Risk Inspection Report. Agreed to Cllr Walker buying and overseeing planting of 10 trees (£50). IW

ROADS: A proposal to ask BCC to remove the Thame Road lay-by recycling containers was defeated 4–3. The Clerk to ask Cllr Bendyshe-Brown about lay-by CCTV to deter fly tipping, and to report overgrown lay-by hedges, a reversed weight-restriction sign on Bar Lane, and a hedge cutting notice for Water Spring House, Meadle. SG. Cllr Rogers to pursue the remaining overgrown hedge on Owlswick Lane and had reported an abandoned wheelchair on Green Lane; most fly tipping had been cleared bar dumped soil.

CORRESPONDENCE: A Bucks Playing Fields Association AGM invite and a BCC Town and Parish event (including Emerging Devolution Proposals) were noted. The Buckinghamshire Local Access Forum sought a volunteer for rights-of-way research; the Clerk to raise the Ilmer-to-Kingsey footpath with the Chiltern Society. SG. A resident's offer to erect the Christmas tree/lights was accepted with thanks. SG. For the Remembrance and Christmas services, agreed a Bugler from RAF Halton (£20, £50 reserve) and mince pies from the village shop (carried 4–2–1). RvA/BR. A resident's play-equipment suggestions, with an offer to fundraise, were to be costed for the December estimates meeting. SG

11/ LOCAL GOVERNMENT PENSION EMPLOYER'S DISCRETIONS POLICY

Agreed to accept the policy as circulated. SG

12/ NEIGHBOURHOOD PLAN STEERING GROUP

The 17th September Steering Group meeting agreed changes for the Submission Version arising from the consultation responses (24 decisions in total), including strengthening the affordable housing policy and commissioning a community-led Landscape Visual Impact Assessment from Whiteleaf Cross.

13/ RISBOROUGH AREA COMMUNITY FORUM REPRESENTATIVE

Agreed the Council could not provide a representative. SG

14/ ANNUAL REVIEW OF INTERNAL AUDIT EFFECTIVENESS

Agreed the Internal Auditor had met expected standards.

15/ DEVOLVED SERVICES FROM WDC 2016/2020

Agreed to accept the circulated contract. SG

16/ PRINCES RISBOROUGH LOCAL PLAN UPDATE

Cllr van Apeldoorn reported; details available via Princes Risborough Town Council's website.

17/ VILLAGE HALL CAR PARK SIGNAGE ON THE PLAYGROUND FENCE

Cllr McPherson expressed disappointment that signs had been installed on the playground fence without the Council's permission; Cllrs Rogers and Walker (having declared an interest) agreed to raise this with the Village Hall Committee.

18/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

Objected (both citing the site's location outside the settlement/Green Belt buffer, the valued existing livery use, and design/traffic concerns): 15/07389/FUL and 15/07604/FUL, both Land Adjacent to Home Farm, Thame Road. No objections: 15/07508/FUL (Old Oaks); 15/07658/FUL and 15/07659/LBC (Meadle Cottage); 15/07916/FUL (4 Bell Crescent). No comments: 15/07736/CLE. Deferred pending a site visit: 15/07817/OUT (OS Parcel 2075, Thame Road) and 15/07846/FUL (1 Williams Way). Tree works at 36 Bell Crescent to abide by the Arboriculturalist's decision. Approved: 15/06678/CLP.

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 17th November 2015 at 7.45pm. The meeting closed at 9.53pm.
