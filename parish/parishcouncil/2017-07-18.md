PRESENT: Cllr Brian Richards (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Sally Whitworth, Jane Rogers, Rolf van Apeldoorn and Richard Myers, Clerk Susanne Griffiths, Deputy Clerk Jayne Mylchreest. No members of the public.

Apologies were received and accepted from Cllr McPherson. The Minutes of 20th June 2017 were approved and signed. No interests were declared. Three of the four Longwick Sports Club Trustees had signed the asset transfer agreement.

Cllr Bendyshe-Brown reported on the Wellington House development, completed B4009 road works, ongoing VAS funding, Speed Watch, the Bumpers Farm development, a Travellers' site planning application, Ilmer Road resurfacing and Owlswick Road.

6/ PRINCES RISBOROUGH LOCAL PLAN STEERING GROUP REPORT

Cllr M Walsh gave an overview of the draft Local Plan and answered councillors' questions.

FINANCE: Payments including salaries, pension, risk assessment, grass cutting, dog waste collection, a meeting travel claim, hall hire and playing field electricity. Bank Balance £32,646.33.

CAPITAL ITEMS: A further £260 repair was needed after the previous skate ramp crack repair failed; the Clerk to query the contractor. JM. Agreed to dig out and permanently fill the concrete posts remaining from the removed cricket nets/football posts. SG

CORRESPONDENCE: WDC Standards Committee co-option vacancies were noted. A resident's flooding/traffic/lighting objection to a nearby development was answered in detail: flood-plain land is not automatically undevelopable but requires mitigation; the WDC "What's ahead for Princes Risborough" employment site is not part of the Neighbourhood Plan; a Longwick/Princes Risborough buffer was expected, though an appeal was pending against a refused scheme between the roundabout and railway bridge. The new parish website was due live 24th July, for councillor review. ALL. The East West Rail consultation (Bicester–Bedford, including a Princes Risborough–Milton Keynes spur) was noted. ALL

10/ PARISH MILESTONES

Cllr Myers to investigate a sign writer from WDC's conservation officer's contractor list. RM

11/ OUTDOOR ADULT FITNESS EQUIPMENT

Agreed to apply for funding. JM

12/ CROUDACE HOMES MEETING

Following the Chairman/Vice Chairman's meeting, issues (street lighting, nest boxes, affordable housing, start dates, drainage) were considered further at a full Council meeting on 11th July.

13/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

Recommended refusal: 16/06375/FUL (Land north/rear of The Old Pigeons, Thame Road — 5-bed dwelling), repeating overdevelopment, building-line and wildlife concerns.

Commented on (17/06562/REM, OS Parcels 6232 & 7428, Thame Road — 43 dwellings' reserved matters): requested an on-site construction compound and delivery-time restrictions around school hours; raised flooding/surface-water concerns; objected to street lighting, consistent with residents' prior vote against village lighting; and flagged the Bar Lane access as dangerous without mitigating measures such as a pavement.

Commented on (17/06691/REM, Boxer Road/Barn Road — 160 dwellings' reserved matters): the same construction-compound, delivery-time, flooding and lighting objections, plus concern that the single 5.3m vehicular access was too narrow for construction traffic and would disrupt Barn Road residents.

Objected: 17/06648/FUL (Land adjoining Saddleback Barn — round chapel), as inappropriate, car-dependent development in open countryside with poor pedestrian links. No objections: 17/06874/FUL (11 Walkers Road); 17/06662/FUL (Sharnley, Thame Road). Approved: 17/06486/CTR (Cozens Holdings — no TPO); 17/05930/FUL (Brook Cottage, Horsenden Lane); 17/05720/CLP (5 Orchard Close). Refused: 17/06160/PN (Hillview Farm — agricultural conversion); 17/06162/CLP (11 Walkers Road).

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 15th August 2017 at 7.30pm. The meeting closed at 9.10pm.
