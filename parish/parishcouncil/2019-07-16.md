PRESENT: Cllr Val McPherson BEM (Chair), Cllrs Brian Richards, Ian Walker, Jane Rogers, Richard Myers and Rolf van Apeldoorn, County Cllr Bill Bendyshe-Brown, Clerk Frank Post, 2 members of the public.

District Cllr Harriss did not attend. The Minutes of 18th June 2019 were approved. Cllr Rogers declared an interest in Village Hall matters as a Trustee.

Matters arising: Cllr van Apeldoorn had identified a stonemason to clean the War Memorial's lettering before completing the full clean. RvA

Cllr Bendyshe-Brown reported: 12,000 attendance at Armed Forces Day; one arrest following the Briants break-in; an impending meeting with senior police on local PC numbers; the Ilmer Road closure and Network Rail discussions; the non-functioning top-of-village VAS; the need for further Bar Lane traffic data, with agreement that future traffic calming should consider the whole parish; and the overgrown Blenheim Close hedge.

6/ INTERNAL AUDIT AND AGAR

The internal audit report and observations were reviewed and responses agreed; a bank reconciliation variance of over £700 was delaying AGAR submission. FP/BR/VMc

7/ DRAINAGE PROJECT

Following a meeting with a drainage consultant (Cllrs Richards and Myers, and the Clerk), agreed by majority not to progress drainage at this stage. FP

8/ MUGA AND OUTDOOR FITNESS EQUIPMENT

With drainage not proceeding, agreed to purchase both from Kompan: MUGA £64.8k, Outdoor Fitness £25.4k (ex VAT), funded by CIL. FP

9/ EASY ACCESS GATES

Agreed to purchase from Jacksons Fencing in tranches of 6–7 units at circa £245 each, with installation at circa £280 per gate. FP/BR

10/ WELCOME PACKS

Printing complete and copies commended; agreed to make brochures available at the Bellway and Croudace sales offices. ALL

11/ DEVOLVED SERVICES

Agreed to extend the PRTC agreement to include Ilmer at the same cutting frequency as the Longwick Playing Field/verges. FP

12/ ALTERNATIVE GRASS CUTTING SOURCING

Cllrs van Apeldoorn and Walker to identify alternative providers for review at contract end. RvA/IW

13/ PRE-SCHOOL FUNDING

Agreed the £3.8k plus VAT grant (CIL funding). FP

14/ LONGWICK SCHOOL FUNDING

Agreed £20k CIL funding for Phase 1 & 2 of the school's proposals, based on competitive quotes. FP

15/ VILLAGE HALL FUNDING

Agreed circa £60k for changing room/shower conversion, subject to three competitive quotes; car parking issues remained unresolved. FP

16/ VAS INSTALLATION CREDIT NOTES

Still outstanding with one provider. FP

17/ WDC GREEN SPACE DEVOLUTION

Agreed to extend contract coverage to Bell Crescent and Walkers Road, subject to WDC budget. FP

18/ BLEDLOW REFUSE SITE

Agreed to support Cllr Bendyshe-Brown's crowdfunding initiative for a commercial alternative to the site's closure. FP/VMc

19/ DISCRETIONARY EXPENDITURE THRESHOLD

Agreed to uplift from £600 to £1,000 and amend standing orders accordingly. FP

20/ NEIGHBOURHOOD WATCH GRANT

Deferred pending a proposal. VMc/FP

21/ EMAIL HUB

Delayed to September pending the provider's new platform. FP

PLANNING: No objection: 19/06633/FUL (Old School House, Thame Road — care home extension). FP

FINANCE: Payments including the Clerk's salary/allowance/expenses, Q1 PAYE tax, risk assessment and Devolved Services. Total £1,578.82.

24/ COUNCILLOR VACANCY

Following interviews of two applicants, agreed to appoint Alex Barter. FP

25/ CORRESPONDENCE RESPONSES

Noted outstanding responses on Blacksmith Road (following Croudace's departure) and overhanging school trees onto the Village Hall. FP/VMc. Cllr Richards clarified the Playing Fields were clearly Parish Council-owned, despite correspondence describing them as a "Village Asset".

26/ COUNCILLOR REPORTS

Cllr van Apeldoorn briefed the Council on a Chesham unitary authority update meeting.

27/ COMPLAINT

Agreed to refer a complaint to WDC's Monitoring Officer. FP

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 17th September 2019 at 7.00pm. The meeting closed at 10.05pm.
