LONGWICK-CUM-ILMER P.C. meeting held 7 30 pm 6th Nov 1959 Schoolroom Longwick

[Margin: Present] Councillors Harper Claydon. Tappin

[Margin: Mins] Minutes of last meeting were read and confirmed

[Margin: Charities] It was Proposed by C Claydon seconded by C Tappin that Mrs D Walker of Rose Farm Longwick should represent the Council on P Risboro Charities in view of the resignation of Mrs Huntley due to leaving the Parish

[Margin: Litter Bins] At the request of Wycombe R.D.C 6 sites in the Parish were selected, where litter bins would serve a useful purpose. The clerk was instructed to inform the Eng & Surveyor of WRDC accordingly

[Margin: Finance] It was proposed by C Tappin seconded by C Claydon that the accounts as listed should be paid, Clerks salary 4 months ending 24th Oct 59 £4-0-0 Postage 4/7 Insurance 15/- Total £4-19-7 Carried

[Margin: School] The Chairman informed the Council that a site had been selected for the proposed new school.

Meeting closed 8-15 pm W J Harper March 22/60
