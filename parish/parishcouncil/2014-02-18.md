PRESENT: Cllr Mrs Valerie McPherson (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Mrs Jane Rogers, James Butler, Brian Richards and Graham Walters, Mrs S Griffiths (Clerk), County Cllr Bill Bendyshe-Brown.

No apologies were received. The Minutes of the meeting on 21st January 2014 were approved and signed.

Cllr B Bendyshe-Brown reported that BCC had set its precept with a 1.5% rise, with roads and pothole filling a high priority; HS2 Ltd has identified the A4010 as a transport corridor for construction traffic, with the Hybrid Bill petitioning process likely to start later this year.

Cllr Graham Walters confirmed the Emergency Team list had been updated following the previous review; the Clerk was asked to post it on the Notice Board and website. SG

FINANCE: Routine payments (Risk Assessment £15.03, Clerk's February salary £396.93) were examined and signed off. Bank Balance £12,511.87.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No issues were highlighted in the January Risk Inspection Reports for the Playing Field or Children's play area. The Clerk was asked to check on hedge-cutting contractor availability, with Cllr Ian Walker to seek alternative quotations. SG/IW

ROADS, VERGES AND RIGHTS OF WAY: The Clerk was asked to raise several items with the local authorities — two fallen road signs at the Ilmer junction on the A4129, the Owlswick signpost needing straightening, dirty Chestnut Way signs, broken chevrons on the Longwick roundabout, and recurring fly tipping by the bottle bank on the A4129 (the Clerk to ask WDC about a camera to catch offenders). SG

CORRESPONDENCE: A letter from BCC Rights of Way introduced the Parish Paths Clearance fund, which lets parishes manage the timing of strimming/mowing on rights of way themselves rather than waiting for the County programme; all present agreed to apply for this funding. SG. A related letter from the Chiltern Society offered to provide some path clearance in return for a donation; agreed in principle pending further detail on the size of donation. An invitation to a meeting on Solar Park proposals for Ilmer was noted; Cllr Graham Walters agreed to seek planning department advice on whether it would be appropriate to meet the prospective developers. GW

10/ UPDATING THE PARISH COUNCIL WEBSITE

Cllr James Butler presented suggestions for updating the website with the current provider and asked councillors to send him their preferences so he can obtain a quotation. JB

11/ TO REVIEW THE IDENTIFICATION OF PARISH ASSETS

Cllr Graham Walters confirmed the War Memorial sits on land owned by Briants, and that the Ilmer Green half of the pond in Parish Council ownership is not registered. Cllr Brian Richards agreed to research the Parish records to obtain evidence of ownership with a view to registering these assets. BR

12/ THE ACTION TO BE TAKEN CONCERNING DOG FOULING ON THE PLAYING FIELD

Cllr Val McPherson reported a complaint concerning dog fouling on the Playing Field. All present agreed the Parish Council was unable to police the field and it relied on dog owners taking responsibility.

13/ THE ACTION TO BE TAKEN CONCERNING ADVERTISEMENT BOARDS IN THE PARISH

Following the October 2013 devolved services agreement covering removal of fly posters and approval of charity advertising signage, Cllr Jane Rogers proposed publicising these services on the website, notice board and in the newsletter. JR

14/ THE PREFERRED COMPANY FOR THE RENEWAL OF THE PARISH COUNCIL INSURANCE

Following confirmation from the broker that Aviva offers the best value, all present agreed to renew the policy for a further 3 years to benefit from the long term agreement discount. SG

15/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

Two applications were deferred pending further information or a site visit: 14/05276/FUL, Sheredon, Thame Road, Longwick (erection of a two storey 4-bed detached dwelling — alternative scheme to the rear-most dwelling permitted under 12/07012/REM & 12/06226/OUT; the Clerk to request an extension while awaiting a reply to the Council's query), and 14/05370/FUL, 13 Bell Crescent, Longwick (raising the roof of an existing garage with parapet and canopy; Cllrs Graham Walters, Val McPherson and Ian Walker agreed to visit the property before the next meeting). SG

To Receive Notice of Planning Applications Refused: 13/07972/FUL — Refused. Address: OS Parcel 9547 Owlswick Buckinghamshire. Proposal: Demolition of existing barns and erection of a single storey 5 bed dwellinghouse with detached car port and new vehicular access.

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 18th March 2014 at 8.00pm. The meeting closed at 10.00pm.
