PRESENT: Cllr G Harper (Chairman), Cllrs I Walker, Mrs J Rogers, Mrs V McPherson, Mrs D Brock, Mrs P Priestley and G O'Neill, Mrs S Griffiths (Clerk), 2 members of the public.

No apologies were received. The Minutes of the previous meeting were approved and signed. Cllr Mrs P Priestley declared an interest in Planning Applications.

Matters arising: the promised Walnut Tree Lane footpath extension had still not started; Cllr Harper to press the landowner for a firm date. GH. The Clerk to meet the Area Co-ordinator for more detail on a Local Community/Prioritisation Plan for Princes Risborough. SG

FINANCE: Payments including a memorial bench for Mr M White (£617.55, offset by a £537 donation), grass cutting, risk inspection and hedge cutting fees totalled £1,156.06. Bank Balance £13,120.76.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No issues in the Playing Field inspection. The RoSPA inspection found no high/medium risks in the play area; a work order was issued to replace the grip on the elephant play equipment's handle. SG

ROADS: Cllr Harper to strim around the War Memorial. GH. The Clerk to raise: vegetation obscuring visibility at the Thame Road/Walkers Road junction; an overgrown footpath section between the Total Garage and Red Lion; overgrown Playing Field exits on footpath 4 towards Bledlow; and a weight-restriction sign on the B4009 needing to be moved to warn lorries earlier. SG

CORRESPONDENCE: A thank-you letter was received from Mrs White for the memorial bench.

REPORTS: Cllr G O'Neill's suggestion that school children lay a Remembrance Day wreath at the War Memorial was welcomed; he would approach the headmistress, and Cllr Rogers would inform the usual wreath-layer. GON/JR. Cllr McPherson reported on the Neighbourhood Action Group and agreed to seek clarification on the process for repeat speeding offenders caught by Community Speedwatch. VM

10/ WEBSITE WORKING GROUP

Cllrs McPherson and Rogers presented six masthead options; option 5 (pub and store, no village hall) was agreed, and the "Last Page Updated"/"Live News" pages were agreed unnecessary. VM/JR

11/ EXTENDING THE SKATEBOARD FACILITIES

Following a request from two members of the public for a half-pipe extension, agreed to arrange a site meeting between Cllrs Priestley and Harper, WDC and the requesters. GH/PP

12/ DEED OF DEDICATION FOR THE RECREATION GROUND

The Clerk to investigate the benefits/disadvantages of adopting a deed of dedication to protect the recreation ground, and report back. SG

13/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

New applications, all with no objections: 09/06178/FUL (The Cart Shed, Rose Farm — conversion to a dwelling); 09/06174/FUL (Total Service Station — ATM and bollards); 09/06341/FUL (8 Dorrells Road — garage conversion). An alleged unlawful use of a building known as the Stables on Mill Lane was investigated by the Enforcement Officer and found not to have occurred. Approved: 09/05787/FUL (The Old Forge, Thame Road).

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 18th August 2009. The meeting closed at 10.10pm.
