Meeting of the Longwick-cum-Ilmer Parish Council held in the Schoolroom, Longwick on Monday February 15th 1943 at 7 pm. To read and confirm Minutes of last meeting. To nominate Parish Constables. To issue a Precept upon the Rating Authority for General Expenses of the Council. Payment of accounts. To fix date, place and time of Annual Parish Meeting to be followed by a meeting of the Parish Council. To consider repairs needed to the Bar Lane Road, Longwick. Present:- Councillors Wm A. Walker (Chairman) W. J. Harper, H. S. Pope, H. W Lewis, A. J. Goodchild and the Clerk.

[Margin: Apology for absence] Apology for absence received from Councillor F. Williams.

[Margin: Minutes] Minutes of last meeting read and confirmed.

[Margin: Mr Owen Tapping to serve as a Trustee to Monks Risborough Charities] Arising out of the Minutes of last meeting, the Clerk reported that Mr Chas Tucker was unwilling to serve as a Trustee to the Monks Risborough Charities, and in consequence Mr Owen Tapping had agreed to serve as a Trustee, and his appointment had been duly notified to the Charity Commission.

[Margin: Nomination of Parish Constables] Proposed by Councillor Lewis seconded by Councillor Pope, that the following persons be nominated to serve as Parish Constables:- Messrs Owen Goodchild, Fred Williams. Harry Goodchild, Owen Tapping and Ernest Thomas Rutland. Carried

[Margin: Precept for £10] Proposed by Councillor Pope seconded by Councillor Goodchild that a Precept for £10 for General Expenses be issued upon the Rating Authority, to become payable on June 30th 1943. Carried

[Margin: Payment of Accounts] Proposed by Councillor Lewis seconded by Councillor Harper that Cheques be drawn in payment of the following accounts:- Robert Bailey. One Quarters Salary as Clerk to 25/12/42 £1/5/- "Logonia" Premium on Clerks Bond 3/-d. Carried.

[Margin: Date of Annual Parish Meeting] It was agreed that the Annual Parish Meeting be held at the Schoolroom, Longwick, on Monday March 22nd 1943 at 7.30. pm to be followed by a meeting of the Parish Council.

[Margin: Repairs to Bar Lane Road Longwick] Consideration was given to repairs needed to the Bar Lane Road, Longwick, and the Chairman agreed to take up the matter with the authorities concerned. H S Pope 22/3/1943.
