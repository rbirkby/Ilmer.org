Meeting of the Longwick-cum-Ilmer Parish Council held in the Schoolroom, Longwick, on Monday March 24th 1941 at 6.45 pm. To read and confirm Minutes of last meeting. Payment of accounts. Present:- Councillors Wm A. Walker (Chairman) H. S. Pope. A. J. Goodchild. F. Williams, E. J. Girling W. J. Harper, and the Clerk.

[Margin: Minutes] Minutes of last meeting read and confirmed.

[Margin: Payment of Accounts] Proposed by Councillor Harper seconded by Councillor Williams, that the following accounts be passed for payment:- Robert Bailey, One Quarters Salary as Clerk to 25/3/41 £1/5/- Postages (One year) 7/1d. The Correspondent Longwick C of E School, for use of Schoolroom for meetings (One year) 10/-d. Messrs Shaw & Sons Ltd, Financial Statements, Notices etc. £1/0/1. Carried

[Margin: Date of Annual Meeting of Parish Council] It was agreed that the Annual Meeting of the Parish Council be held at the Schoolroom, Longwick on Monday April 21st 1941 at 7.30. p.m. Wm A Walker
