PRESENT: Cllr Mrs Valerie McPherson (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Graham Walters, Mrs Jane Rogers, Rolf van Apeldoorn and Sally Whitworth, Mrs Caroline Page (Acting Clerk), County Cllr Bill Bendyshe-Brown, 2 Members of Public.

Apologies were received and accepted from Cllr B Richards. The Minutes of 15th July 2014 were approved and signed.

Cllr B Bendyshe-Brown reported on roads and footpaths. The Chairman raised complaints that the Chestnut Way footway is too narrow and uneven; he suggested requesting LAF delegated funding to widen it. The Clerk was asked to arrange cutting back of undergrowth obscuring the Summerleys Road 30mph signs. It was agreed to investigate adding public liability insurance to the Council's policy so it could consider taking responsibility for the Dorrells Road/Scout Hut footpath to the Village Hall car park, and Cllr Bendyshe-Brown will arrange a meeting to try to resolve the issue.

FINANCE: Routine payments (Risk Assessment £15.58, grass cutting £442.88, WDC annual play inspection £76.80, external audit fee £120.00, Cllr Whitworth's training expenses £11.25, hall hire £20.00, NI contribution £5.60) were examined and signed off. Bank Balance £13,917.69.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No medium/high risk issues in the Risk Inspections.

15/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

14/06965/OUT: OS Parcel 9166 Boxer Road and OS Parcel 6576 Walnut Tree Lane, Longwick — outline application for up to 175 dwellings with access from Boxer Road/Barn Road and pedestrian/cycle access to Walnut Tree Lane. The Parish Council lodged a detailed objection covering: (1) a roughly six-fold increase in traffic through the already-problematic Chestnut Way junctions, plus years of construction traffic; (2) unsustainable development given poor bus, cycling and pedestrian provision and heavy reliance on car use; (3) water issues, noting the site's own Flood Risk Assessment accepted high flood risk and proposed discharge to a watercourse outside the site, which the Council considered unacceptable given Thames Water's identified capacity shortfall; (4) a fundamental change to the character of a small, linear village — 175 dwellings would be a roughly 40% increase on its 366 addresses; (5) inadequately assessed biodiversity, with part of the site already functioning as an informal nature reserve; (6) prematurity, since the wider question of whether Longwick should absorb housing at all had not been settled; and (7) a summary that the application failed the tests for sustainable development. Should WDC nonetheless be minded to approve, the Council asked that developer contributions be secured for: an alternative access; Chestnut Way junction improvements; a shared cycleway/footway along Lower Icknield Way and Summerleys Road; footway widening along the A4129 to Princes Risborough; surface improvements to Bridleways 5 and 6 and the Phoenix Trail; peak-time bus services; education provision; play area provision and 30-year maintenance (preferably as an extension to the existing Recreation Ground rather than a separate facility); open space/landscaping including a walk-through from Barn Road to the Recreation Ground; and disposal of all surface water to SUDs rather than the nearby watercourse.

14/07220/DMI: Armour Farm, Stockwell Lane, Little Meadle — prior approval for partial demolition of an outbuilding. No objections.

Approved: 14/06523/FUL (Hatchmead Farm); 14/06581/FUL (Rose Farm); 14/06818/FUL (Kingswood, Chestnut Way).
