PRESENT: Cllr Val McPherson BEM (Chairman), Cllrs Brian Richards, Ian Walker, Sally Whitworth, Jane Rogers, Rolf van Apeldoorn and Richard Myers, Clerk Susanne Griffiths, Deputy Clerk Jayne Mylchreest. No members of the public.

No apologies were received. The Minutes of 19th September 2017 were approved and signed. No interests were declared.

Matters arising: an inventory had been sent to Risborough Rangers Junior Football Club and the Sports Club Trustees to establish ownership of items in the garage, with the Trustees asked to remove remaining rubbish including the roller. JM. Cllr Whitworth agreed to contact a graphic designer to produce the preferred parish logo. SW

Cllr Bendyshe-Brown reported by note that the Thame-direction VAS was now working and TfB had agreed to cut back Thame Road/Lower Icknield footway overgrowth. Cllr Walker raised concerns about excessive pelican crossing waiting times, for the Clerk to query. SG

FINANCE: Payments including salaries, pension, risk assessment, grass cutting, cricket net removal/digger hire/grass seed, two newsletter print runs, dog waste collection and playing field electricity. Receipts included a £13,700 WDC precept payment. Bank Balance £34,396.07.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No issues in the September Risk Inspection Reports.

CORRESPONDENCE: An outdoor fitness equipment supplier's offer was deferred pending the Neighbourhood Plan decision. WDC's New Local Plan approval and final consultation (16th October–27th November) was noted.

9–10/ REMEMBRANCE AND CAROL SERVICES

Cllr van Apeldoorn confirmed the Remembrance Day order of service; the Carol Service was confirmed for Monday 18th December 2017 at 7pm.

11/ LOCAL PLAN UPDATE

Cllr van Apeldoorn reported the New Local Plan's approval and final consultation period, and would attend WDC's 6th November Planning Committee on the Gladmans application; agreed he would raise the Longwick/Princes Risborough buffer zone with the Cabinet Member for Planning. RvA

12/ NEIGHBOURHOOD PLAN UPDATE

WDC was considering comments received; an Inspector was being sought, with a newsletter to follow once recommendations were known, ahead of setting a referendum date.

13/ INTERNAL AUDIT EFFECTIVENESS REVIEW

Deferred to the next meeting pending circulated information. SG

14/ 2018 MEETING DATES

Agreed (5–2) not to hold an August meeting. Agreed (6–1 abstention) the Annual Parish Meeting for Monday 23rd April 2018, with the Rt Hon. John Bercow MP invited as guest speaker. SG/VM. The full 2018 calendar was agreed, including the Annual Meeting of the Parish Council on 15th May. JM

15/ PARISH MILESTONES

Approved a £220 quotation for sign-writing the lettering, the preferred contractor no longer being available. JM

16/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

No objection, but with concerns: 17/05825/FUL (Bumpers Farm, Ilmer — battery storage compound), noting construction traffic was using the unsuitable Ilmer Road rather than the agreed Thame Road access, requesting enforcement, and criticising inadequate site screening. Approved: 17/07126/FUL (8 Bell Crescent); 17/06956/FUL (7 Lower Icknield Way); 17/06848/FUL (Boundary House, The Green); 16/06375/FUL (Land north/rear of The Old Pigeons — permitted on appeal/resubmission); 17/06999/FUL (Mistlethrush Barn, Rose Farm); 17/06972/FUL (18 Walkers Road).

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 21st November 2017 at 7.30pm. The meeting closed at 8.50pm.
