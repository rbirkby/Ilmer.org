Longwick Cum Ilmer Parish Council meeting Held in Schoolroom Longwick 20-3-57

Present Councillors W Harper Chairman, D Claydon E Girling, O Tapping F Williams, F M Walker & The Clerk Apologize From Councillor L Claridge For not bing able to attend.

[Margin: Minutes] The minutes of Last meetg where Read & Confirmed

[Margin: Precept] Pro Councillor F Walker Sec Councillor F Williams That we Precept For approximately a 1d Rate i.e. 20£ to Cover the Councils Payments For the Year 1957

Councillor Harper Gave an interesting Report on The Executive meetg.

[Margin: Accounts] Proposed Councillor Williams Sec Councillor Tapping The Following accounts be Paid Clerks Salary £5 . 0 0 Clerks Bond 3/0 Postage 5/11, Shaw & Sons 18/10 School Correspondent C of E School For Rent 10/0

It was agreed the Clerk see the Road Foreman Re the state of the Drain at Meadle & also the Footbridge on Footpath 22.

W J Harper May 15 / 1957
