8\. CORRESPONDENCE

Public footpath 3, parish of Longwick-cum-Ilmer. Further to the Parish Council's recent report regarding this footpath, the Rights of Way department sent an email informing the Parish Council of the action taken. An inspection of the site found an electric fence with the third strand of wire not insulated and at such a height, making it near impossible for people to safely step over. They have written to the landowner requesting a gap is created in the electric fence and, if necessary, hook and handles to be installed.

An invitation to a Focus Group to consider the future design of the local Health Watch from the B.C.C. Noted.

9\. REPORTS

School Governors. Nothing to report.

NW Chilterns Local Area Forum. Cllr G Walters informed the members that the County Council agreed that the Princes Risborough Day Care building could be taken over by a Social Enterprise company who will run services from the building for the whole community, leasing it for a very low rent for up to two years so that the company has time to raise funds to purchase it.

WDALC. Cllr I Walker reported that Bucks County Council is part of 51M, a group of local authorities affected by the HS2 proposals. Local authorities investigating HS2 are being investigated via a complaint from an MP that they are not using their money wisely in a time of austerity, with the bigger councils directly affected being called NIMBYs by some politicians. There has been a lack of information and detail, no clear statement on environmental impact, and no business case once the £5 billion taken from existing services to support HS2 is considered. Councils have put £1.1 million into a fighting fund (£400,000 spent to date) against a fund of £800 million supporting the case; BCC will put in £1/2 million over three years if needed. 51M is encouraging people to write to their MPs and hopes to form an all-party group to raise awareness. A training session on the Localism Bill with Charles Meakings and Ian Manktelow is planned for the 15th December meeting, after the AGM, open to all Parish Councillors.

N.A.G. Cllr V McPherson advised that the NAG priorities will remain as last year except that Anti-Social Behaviour is to be replaced by Car Theft; Speeding Traffic, Burglary and Inconsiderate Parking being retained.

Newsletter. Cllr V McPherson apologised for the delay in the delivery of the latest newsletter, which was due to a delay in the printing stage.
