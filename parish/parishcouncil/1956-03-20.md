Longwick Cum Ilmer Parish Council meeting Held in School Room Longwick march 20th 1956

Present Councillor W J Harper Chairman, Councillors D. Claydon, E J Girling, O Tapping, W F Walker F Williams & the Clerk.

To Read & Confirm minutes of past meetg, matters arresing Payment of accounts any Other Business

[Margin: Minutes] The minutes of the Last meetg where Read & Confirmd

[Margin: Bridge Repairs on Footpath No 22] Proposed Councillor Claydon Sec Councillor Girling That The Clerk write the County Surveyor Re the Bad State of Repairs of the Footbridge on Footpath no 22 Carried

[Margin: Accounts] Pro Councillor Walker Sec Councillor Girling That The Following accounts be Paid Rent of C of E Schoolroom 10/0, Clerks Salary £5-0-0 Clerks Bond 3/0, Postage 9/0 Carried

W J Harper 16/5/56
