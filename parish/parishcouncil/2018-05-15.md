PRESENT: Cllr Val McPherson BEM (Chair), Cllrs Brian Richards, Ian Walker, Jane Rogers, Sally Whitworth, Richard Myers and Rolf van Apeldoorn, County Cllr Bill Bendyshe-Brown, Clerk Frank Post, 1 member of the public.

Apologies were received from District Cllr Clive Harriss. Cllr McPherson was re-elected Chair (proposed Rogers, seconded Myers) and signed the declaration of acceptance. Cllr Richards was elected Vice Chairman (proposed McPherson, seconded van Apeldoorn). The Minutes of 17th April 2018 were approved. No interests were declared.

Matters arising: agreed to store the fete's plastic chairs in the garage (RaV); to investigate grass cutting provision for Ilmer Green and the Green at the Red Lion (FP); a colour logo was agreed by majority vote for the new noticeboard (FP); Cllr Rogers to attend the July Parish Liaison meeting (JR); and to make representations to WDC about the Longwick Green/Playing Fields hedge in the context of the Bellway development (FP).

Cllr Bendyshe-Brown reported on BCC's support for the southern Oxford/Cambridge Expressway route option; the housing implications of the ongoing unitary authority debate; Armistice Day 2018 planning; the appointment of a Local Plan Inspector following RARA's submission; a new Village Hall directional sign awaiting installation; Owlswick Road resurfacing; Meadle potholes; the police station's planned relocation to the Fire Station; VAS electricity costs and confirmation that fixed VAS installations do not require TfB approval; a scheduled Bar Lane flooding meeting with the Flood Management Team and Highways; and priority winter maintenance for the footpath to Briants and the Chestnut Way footway. Cllr Rogers raised CIL options for discussion with BCC/TfB's Sian Thomas. FP/BBB

8/ COMMITTEE AND OUTSIDE BODY NOMINATIONS

Agreed: Cllr Rogers (NW Chilterns Area Forum; Allotment Charities; RARA engagement); Cllr Walker (WDALC); Cllrs Whitworth and Rogers (Monks Risborough Charities); Cllr Whitworth (Princes Risborough Charities); all councillors to participate in planning matters.
