Meeting of the Longwick-cum-Ilmer Parish Council held in the Schoolroom, Longwick on Monday March 27th 1939 at 7.15 pm. To read and confirm Minutes of last meeting. Payment of accounts. Present:- Councillors Wm A. Walker (Chairman), Herbert S. Pope, Albert Jas Goodchild, Wm Joseph Harper, Fred Williams, Edward John Girling, Herbert Wm Lewis, and Mr Robert Bailey (Clerk)

[Margin: Minutes] Minutes of last meeting read and confirmed.

[Margin: Payment of accounts] Proposed by Councillor Williams seconded by Councillor Lewis, that Cheques be drawn in payment of the following accounts:- Robert Bailey, One Quarters Salary as Clerk to 25/3/39. £1/5/- Postages (One year) 9/11d. Total Cheque to Bailey £1/14/11. Correspondent Longwick C. E. School for use of room for meetings year ending March 31st 1939. 10/-d. Carried.

[Margin: Date of Annual Parish Meeting] It was agreed that the Annual Meeting of the Parish Council be fixed for Monday April 24th 1939. at The School Longwick, at 8 pm. Wm A Walker [Stamp: DISTRICT AUDIT]
