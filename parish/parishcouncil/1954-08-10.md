Longwick Cum Ilmer Parish Council meeting Held in the Schoolroom Longwick Aug 10 1954 To Read & Confirm minutes of Last meeting matters arising, Correspondence Payment of Accounts Any Other Business, Present Councillors Harper Chairman, Claydon Girling, Tapping, Miss F M Walker F Williams & The Clerk. The minutes of the Last meeting Where Read & Confirmed

[Margin: matters arising] Proposed Councillor F Williams Sec E Girling That we Take out an Insurance with The municipal mutual Insurance Coy To Cover The Council's Legal Liability The Premium af 10/- For an Indemnity of £25000 Carried

[Margin: Ilmer Water Supply] Proposed Councillor Williams Sec Miss F Walker That we send a Deputation to meet the Public Works Committee of the R D C at High Wycombe on September 21st. The Deputation to Consist of Councillor Harper Councillor Claridge & Mr Serini of Ilmer Carried

[Margin: Precept] Proposed F Williams Sec D Claydon That we Put in a Precept For Ten Pounds To Cover expenses. For the Half year ending March 31st 1955 Carried

[Margin: Payment of Accounts] Proposed Councillor Tapping Sec Councillor Girling That The Following account be Paid Clerks Salary of £2 . 10 . 0 Due June 24th 1954 Carried

[Margin: Any Other Business] Proposed Councillor Girling Sec Councillor Claydon That the Clerk Write the Divisional Surveyor Drawing his attention to The Bad state of the Road Through Meadle Village Carried W J Harper Sept 29 / 54
