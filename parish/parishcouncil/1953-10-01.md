meeting of Longwick Parish Council Held Oct 1st 1953 at the School House Longwick. Present Councillor Harper Chairman D Claydon, E Girling L Claridge, O Tapping, Miss F M Walker & F Williams & the Clerk To Read & Confirm minutes of Last meeting matters arising Payment of accounts Any other Business.

[Margin: minutes] The minutes of the Last meeting Where Read & Confirmed

[Margin: matters arising Water Supply Ilmer.] Pro Councillor Girling, Sec Miss F M Walker. that we Pass the information on to the People Concerned. Carried

[Margin: Precept.] Pro F Williams Sec Miss F M Walker that we ask. For a Precept For 10-0-0 Carried

[Margin: Payment of accounts] Pro D Claydon Sec E Girling That The Follany account Be Paid Clerks Salary to Sept 24th 2-10-0 Carried

[Margin: Any other Business] [Margin: War memorial] Re Paint & Pointing War memorial it Was Decided that matter to Be Brought up at the next meeting

[Margin: Obstruction of Footpath.] It Was agreed the Clerk see Mrs Brooks Re the Cutting Back of a Tree Obstructing the Footpath W. J Harper Jan 13 / 54
