Notice of Appeals in Progress:

- 15/06161/OUT: Boxer Road/Barn Road (OS Parcel 9166 Boxer Road & OS Parcel 6576 Walnut Tree Lane). Outline application for residential development of up to 160 dwellings (ref APP/KO425/W/15/30/8514). Longwick-cum-Ilmer Parish Council objects to this application for the following reasons: (1) It does not conform to the visions, objectives or site allocations of the Draft Neighbourhood Plan, which proposes development spread across a number of smaller sites rather than one larger one. (2) Increase in traffic and risk to road safety: traffic at the Chestnut Way/Boxer Road junction could increase roughly six-fold, and Boxer Road and Barn Road appear too narrow for the extra traffic and 4-5 years of construction traffic. (3) Not sustainable development: existing bus services do not serve peak hours, neither route to Princes Risborough offers a safe cycling/walking alternative, and almost 70% of the parish already travels to work by car. (4) Water issues: the Flood Risk Assessment accepts a high risk of surface and ground water flooding and proposes disposal to a watercourse outside the site boundary, which the Parish Council considers unacceptable, and Thames Water has identified inadequate waste water infrastructure. (5) Changing the character of the village: 175 additional dwellings would represent a roughly 40% increase on the village's 366 addresses, fundamentally changing its currently small, linear character. (6) Biodiversity: the submitted assessments are mostly desk-based, and part of the site already serves as an informal nature reserve that could instead be retained as a village asset. (7) Prematurity: determination should await the planning authority's assessment of whether Longwick is an appropriate location for housing need at all, let alone a scheme of this scale. (8) Summary: the application does not meet the tests for the presumption in favour of sustainable development. (9) If nonetheless minded to approve, the Parish Council requests conditions or a legal agreement securing contributions towards: an alternative access; junction improvements at each end of Chestnut Way; a shared cycleway/footway along Lower Icknield Way and Summerleys Road; footway improvements along Longwick Road (A4129); surface improvements to Bridleways 5 and 6 and the Phoenix Trail; peak-time bus services to the station and town; education provision costs; and thirty years of play area provision and maintenance, plus (j) proper disposal of surface water to SUDs rather than the nearby watercourse.
