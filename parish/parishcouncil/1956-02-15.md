Longwick Cum Ilmer Parish Council meeting Held in the Schoolroom Longwick February 15th 1956

Present Councillor W J Harper Chairman, Councillor Claridge, Williams, Walker, Tapping, & The Clerk

To Read & Confirm Minutes of Last meeting matters arising, Correspondence, To Issue a Precept & Other Business

[Margin: Minutes] Th minutes where Read & Confirmed

[Margin: Telephone Kiosk] Pro Councillor Williams Sec Councillor Tapping That we ask for Telephone Kiosk to be Placed at the Sportsman Crossroads or Meadle, Carried

[Margin: Precept] Proposed Councillor Walker Seconded Councillor Claridge That we issue a Precept For £20 . 0 . 0 For the year Ending March 31st 1957.

[Margin: Highways] It Was agreed the Clerk notify the Surveyor The Condition of Footpath between the Bar Lane & Anderdons Farm also the condition of the Road Where the Water mains Had Crossed to Ilmer & Reminder Re the Ilmer Direction signs at the Red Lion

[Margin: Parish meeting] agreed Parish meeting to be Held 7-40pm 22 march 1956

W J Harper 20/3/56
