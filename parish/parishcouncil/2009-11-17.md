PRESENT: Cllr G Harper (Chairman), Cllrs I Walker, Mrs J Rogers, Mrs V McPherson, Mrs D Brock and G O'Neill, Mrs S Griffiths (Clerk).

Apologies were received and accepted from Mrs P Priestley. The Minutes of the previous meeting were approved and signed. None declared an interest.

Matters arising: the shop's telegraph-pole lamp was still awaiting repair, with ownership disputed between the Council and BCC. Cllr Walker had met the Local Area Technician about the overgrown scout hut path, who agreed to cut back trees and clear the ditch next financial year. BCC's audit found the Thame-end pedestrian refuge site unsuitable, so it will not be installed. The newsletter had been added to the website. The Village Carol Service was arranged for 6pm on 15th December, with refreshments, tree and school liaison assigned to Cllrs Rogers, Harper and O'Neill respectively. JR/GON/GH

FINANCE: Payments for concrete supply (£690.00), risk assessment, website newsletter upload and October grounds maintenance. Bank Balance £15,772.04.

CAPITAL ITEMS: Skate park graffiti (medium risk) — Cllr Harper agreed to remove it. GH. Reports of an unsupervised large dog in the Playing Field could not be acted on by the dog warden without a witnessed incident. Cllr Mrs D Brock agreed to buy three trees ahead of the Playing Field Working Group's Saturday work. DB. A broken bench slat on the Golden Jubilee Seat would be inspected by Cllr O'Neill, with Briants to repair if needed. GON

ROADS: Following another accident at the Bar Lane junction, members were concerned the "Give Way" sign sits too close to give sufficient warning; Cllr McPherson to obtain a police report. VM. The Clerk to pursue a weight-restriction sign for Bar Lane. SG

CORRESPONDENCE: A Wheels to Work invitation and bus service change notice were noted. A BCC request to organise Rights of Way walks needed no action as the Council already does this. Response to BCC's Local Transport Plan 3 consultation pack deferred to next meeting. SG

REPORTS: The school's Remembrance Day wreath-laying was thanked, with hope it becomes annual; Cllr O'Neill summarised a recent Ofsted report. The Neighbourhood Action Group reported a multi-area speed enforcement operation (16 motorists dealt with) and 2 burglaries in October.

10/ WEBSITE WORKING GROUP

The newsletter had been loaded to the website; the Clerk to follow up on photo permissions and a "Live News" quotation. SG

11/ MEETING DATES FOR 2010

Agreed to continue meeting on the third Tuesday of the month.

12/ VEHICLE ACTIVATED SIGN BID

The Clerk to gather local accident data to support a funding bid for a VAS; all present agreed the Council would contribute £350 towards supply and installation if successful. SG

13/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

New applications: 09/07098/FUL (Horsenden Manor — storage building; a site visit was agreed before deciding); 09/07037/CTREE (Meadle Cottage — fell a Lime tree); 09/06903/TPO (1 Innkeepers Court — Ash tree work); 09/06858/FUL (Orchard Cottage — side extensions) — all no objection except the deferred Horsenden Manor application. Approved: 09/06708/FUL (Hill View), 09/06702/FUL (Poplars Farm), 09/06547/LBC (The Corn Barn).

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 15th December 2009. The meeting closed at 9.25pm.
