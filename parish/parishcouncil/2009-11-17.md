6/CAPITAL ITEMS AND COMMUNITY FACILITIES

A. Playing Field

Graffiti on the skate park was highlighted as a medium risk in the Risk Inspection report. Cllr G Harper agreed to remove the graffiti. GH

Cllrs I Walker and V McPherson have both received reports of a large dog that was not being properly supervised in the playing field. The dog warden has been contacted but is unable to do anything unless they witness an incident. All present agreed that although the situation is unsatisfactory, the Parish Council is unable to take any action to ban the dog from the park.

Cllr Mrs D Brock agreed to purchase three trees for the playing field in advance of the work to be undertaken by the Playing Field Working Group on Saturday. DB

B. Children's Play Area

The Risk Inspection report has highlighted a broken bench slat on the Golden Jubilee Seat. Cllr G O'Neill has agreed to inspect the damage and if necessary contact Briants to organise the repair. GON

7/ ROADS, VERGES AND RIGHTS OF WAY

The members were informed of another accident involving a car exiting Bar Lane. The members are concerned that the siting of the "Give Way" sign is too close to the junction to give sufficient notice to the cars that are travelling along Bar Lane. Cllr Mrs V McPherson agreed to contact the police to obtain a report on the accident. VM

The Clerk is requested to contact BCC in relation to the Parish Council's request for a "weight restriction" sign to be placed before the entry to Bar Lane to prevent their entry as once the vehicles have entered the lane it is difficult for them to turn around. SG

8/ CORRESPONDENCE:

To be discussed

- An invitation to the Wheels to Work initiative on 27th November. Noted.
- Notification of changes to the bus services in the Parish. The Clerk is requested to place a laminated copy of the relevant times on the notice board.
- A request from BCC for the Parish Council to organise walks along local Public Rights of Way. No further action required as Longwick-cum-Ilmer Parish Council already operates a system whereby the local Public Rights of Way are walked on a regular basis and maintenance issues reported monthly.
- A letter from Transport for Buckinghamshire advising they are currently developing the third Local Transport Plan for Buckinghamshire (LTP3). They are seeking engagement at an early stage in the project to ensure that the Plan tackles local issues with localised options. They have requested that the Parish Councils discuss the pack, suggest if the stated objectives can be improved; review the problems and issues listed; prioritise strategic options for the County, with reference to the needs of the local area and post the comments, on the website by the 31st December 2009.

To enable the Parish Council to give an informed response, all present agreed to defer this item until the next meeting. SG

Neighbourhood Action Group

Cllr Mrs V McPherson presented the following report from the Neighbourhood Policing Team

Speed enforcement operation have been carried out involving the local Neighbourhood Policing team, the Thames Valley safer roads partnership and road safety officers from Transport for Buckinghamshire in the following areas: Chinnor Road- Bledlow Ridge, Ellesborough Road- Butlers Cross, Thame Road- Longwick along with Lower Icknield Road- Great Kimble. 16 Motorists were dealt with for exceeding the 30 mph speed limit. These results support the continued use of such speed enforcement operations in the area over the coming months.

The criteria for permanent speed indication devices or traffic calming measures to be fitted in villages is based on a priority basis using injury collision statistics to ensure the funds are spent where they are most needed.

Within the month of October there has been 2 burglaries reported in the Parish.

The next bear surgery in Longwick is on Thursday 26th November between 0830-0930am at the Longwick Post Office.

The next Neighbourhood action group meeting is at 7.30pm on Monday 30th November at Bradenham Cricket Club.
