PRESENT: Cllr Brian Richards (Acting Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Mrs Jane Rogers, James Butler and Graham Walters, Mrs C Page (Clerk), County Cllr Bill Bendyshe-Brown.

Apologies were received and accepted from Cllr Mrs Valerie McPherson. The Minutes of the meeting on 18th February 2014 were approved and signed.

Cllr B Bendyshe-Brown reported on roads and footpaths, requesting local councils arrange meetings with farmers and landowners to encourage clearing of culverts and drains to reduce flooding; it was agreed to place a small article in the Parish magazine. Cllr J Rogers reported the problem was worsened by fly tipping, including items left from Transport for Bucks works, and asked for flood warning signs left in the Bar Lane ditch to be collected.

FINANCE: Routine payments (Risk Assessment £15.03, annual insurance premium £1,255.55, website updates £36.00, dog waste/litter collection £308.88, PAYE tax £109.40) were examined and signed off. Bank Balance £9,978.12.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No issues were highlighted in the February Risk Inspection Reports. The Clerk was asked to arrange weed killing on the Playing Field. Cllr Ian Walker raised residents bringing household dog waste to the playing field bins, causing overflow, and will draft a magazine article clarifying the bins are for use while on the field. The Clerk was asked to check whether the ditch along the north boundary could be cleared under devolved services.

ROADS, VERGES AND RIGHTS OF WAY: The Clerk was asked to report recent fly tipping by the recycling bins in the A4129 lay-by and two road signs still lying on the verge at the Ilmer junction. SG

CORRESPONDENCE: An invitation to a Community Rights workshop in Aylesbury was noted. English Heritage invited comment on their consultation report assessing the Longwick War Memorial for statutory listing; the councillors had no further information to add.

10/ UPDATING THE PARISH COUNCIL WEBSITE

Cllr J Butler circulated suggestions by email; agreed to defer while he obtains accurate costings.

11/ AN UPDATE ON THE PARISH ASSETS

Cllr B Richards had visited County Hall and found a reference to half of Ilmer pond being filled in during July/August 1938 — this is the land now known as The Green (common land). The Clerk was asked to write to the Rights of Way Officer about registering this as a village green. SG

12/ APPOINTMENT OF AN INTERNAL AUDITOR FOR THE FINANCIAL YEAR 2013/2014

All present agreed to appoint Mr Don Timms as Internal Auditor. SG

13/ A RESPONSE TO THE CONSULTATION ON THE LOCAL PLAN

Cllr G Walters proposed the Council's response: taking into account views expressed at the 18th February public meeting, the Parish Council has serious concerns about future development around Longwick because much of the surrounding land is susceptible to surface water flooding and drainage problems, there are road safety/traffic concerns from increased movements, a potential change of character from a quiet linear village with few directly adjoining neighbours, and loss of views of open countryside. Seconded by Cllr J Rogers; all present agreed. SG

14/ VACANCY FOR A COUNCILLOR

Only one application had been received; agreed to invite the applicant to the next meeting. SG

15/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

New applications: 14/05276/FUL, Sheredon, Thame Road, Longwick (two storey 4-bed detached dwelling, alternative scheme; the Parish Council unable to comment pending a reply to its query); 14/05370/FUL, 13 Bell Crescent, Longwick (raising garage roof with parapet and canopy; no objections).

Approved: 13/07517/FUL (Land Adjacent Lapetra, Thame Road — 3 bed house adjoining the Post Office with flat above); 13/08073/FUL (OS Parcel 0038, Stockwell Lane, Little Meadle — continued mixed agricultural/dog training use); 13/07773/FUL (Water Springs, Meadle Village Road — two storey infill extension, roof raising and velux windows). Refused: 13/08044/FUL (Vale Edge Farm, Owlswick Lane — roof raising with dormer window, alternative scheme).

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 15th April 2014 at 8.00pm, preceded by the Annual Parish Meeting at 7.30pm. The meeting closed at 10.00pm.
