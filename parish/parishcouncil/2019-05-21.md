PRESENT: Cllr Val McPherson BEM (Chair), Cllrs Brian Richards, Ian Walker, Jane Rogers, Richard Myers and Rolf van Apeldoorn, Clerk Frank Post, 17 members of the public.

No apologies were received. Cllr McPherson was re-elected Chair (proposed Myers, seconded Rogers) and signed the declaration of acceptance. Cllr Richards was re-elected Vice Chairman (proposed McPherson, seconded Rogers). The Minutes of 16th April 2019 were approved. No interests were declared.

Matters arising: agreed to consider Owlswick's VAS requirement at the next meeting. FP. Anti-social behaviour reported by a parishioner had been relayed to the police by the Chairman. Agreed to commission Spittles to repair the footpath outside the village hall. FP. The pre-school agreement had been signed.

PLANNING: Objected (on the Chairman's casting vote): 19/05840/FUL (Orchard View Farm, Little Meadle). No objection: 19/06033/FUL (Crownridge, Longwick); 19/05933/FUL (46 Walnut Tree, Longwick).

Cllr Bendyshe-Brown tabled no report.

10/ COMMITTEE AND OUTSIDE BODY NOMINATIONS

Agreed: Cllr Rogers (NW Chilterns Local Area Forum; RARA engagement); Cllr Walker (WDALC); Cllrs Myers and Rogers (Monks Risborough Charities); Princes Risborough Charities representation left pending; all councillors to participate in planning matters.

11/ COUNCILLOR VACANCY

Agreed to advertise the vacancy arising from Cllr Whitworth's resignation. FP

12/ MUGA PURCHASE

Confirmed selection of Kompan following quotes received; a £10k lottery funding submission had been tabled for outdoor fitness equipment. FP

13/ WELCOME PACKS

Cllr Rogers noted three pages still to fill; agreed to add Council functionality copy, useful numbers and a street map. VM/JR/RM

14/ PRE-SCHOOL PROPOSAL

Approved at a cost of £3,840 plus VAT. FP

15/ VILLAGE HALL PROPOSALS

Cllrs Rogers and Walker, as Village Hall Trustees, took no part. Agreed (proposed Richards, seconded van Apeldoorn) to consider car parking and internal refurbishment as separate funding projects, for further discussion next meeting. FP

16/ PLAYING FIELD DRAINAGE

Agreed to commission an expert review and specification for supplier quotations. FP. Parishioner concerns about car parking on the recreation ground and a proposed brook culvert were noted; Cllr Richards confirmed no such proposal had been approved, with the Clerk to respond accordingly. FP

17/ EASY ACCESS (KISSING) GATES

Agreed footpath kissing gates (Centrewire "Oxford" range, BCC-approved) were a suitable CIL funding item, for further deliberation next meeting; councillors to gauge demand and landowner approval. ALL

FINANCE: Payments including the Clerk's salary/allowance/expenses, an ICO certificate, village hall hire, and risk assessment. Total £631.10. Receipts noted: £3,102.13 (BCC) and £14,112.50 (WDC precept), totalling £17,214.63.

20/ STANDING ORDERS

Agreed to review uplifting the £600 discretionary procurement threshold and extending public participation time to 15 minutes, and to consider incorporating GDPR. FP

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 18th June 2019 at 7.00pm. The meeting closed at 9.40pm.
