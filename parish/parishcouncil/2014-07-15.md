PRESENT: Cllr Mrs Valerie McPherson (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Graham Walters, Mrs Jane Rogers and Brian Richards, Mrs S Griffiths (Clerk), County Cllr Bill Bendyshe-Brown, 1 Member of Public.

Apologies were received and accepted from Cllr Sally Whitworth. The Minutes of 17th June 2014 were approved and signed. Cllr B Bendyshe-Brown reported on roads and footpaths in the parish.

Matters arising: the gate post hinge repair and hedge-cutting quotation were progressing; the missing bin lid quotation (£122.44) was accepted; the Playing Field ditch had been cleared and BCC informed so they could clear the school-boundary section; the two fallen Thame Road signs had been collected by TFB with replacements ordered; footpath 4 beyond the railway bridge had been cut back. SG/IW

FINANCE: Routine payments (Risk Assessment £15.03, dog waste/litter collection £308.88, grass cutting £442.88, website hosting £18.00, Cllr Whitworth's planning training £15.00) were examined and signed off. Bank Balance £15,097.17 after a £1,079.02 VAT refund.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No medium/high risk issues in the June Risk Inspections.

ROADS: The Clerk to write to Shell Garage about a hedge encroaching on the footpath, contact the enforcement officer about a new gate on land in Meadle, and raise overgrowth obscuring warning signs on the B4009 at Meadle turn and 30mph signs on Summerleys Road. SG/JR

9/ CORRESPONDENCE

An invitation to meet with Gladmans Developments Ltd on their proposals to develop land off Barn Road: Cllr Brian Richards proposed entering dialogue with the developers; seconded by Cllr Val McPherson; carried 4 votes to 1. SG. A request to hold a fire walk in the Playing Field was referred to Cllrs Jane Rogers and Val McPherson to discuss with the applicant. JR/VM. A resident's report of the Playing Field gate being left open was resolved — Cllr Ian Walker confirmed the gate and padlock are secure. An invitation from Solar Planning to present on their proposed Bumpers Farm solar farm was accepted; Cllr Val McPherson to meet them ahead of the public meeting, arranged for 7.30pm on 11th August. SG

10/ UPDATING THE PARISH COUNCIL WEBSITE

The free makeover requires a 5-year commitment the Council could not make; councillors agreed instead to review and update the site's contact details themselves.

11/ COMMUNICATIONS QUESTIONNAIRE

Cllr Brian Richards presented a draft paper questionnaire for inclusion in the September/October newsletter. A Working Group (Richards, Walters, McPherson) was formed to finalise content and later collate responses. BR/VM/GW

12/ FILLING THE VACANCY FOR A COUNCILLOR BY CO-OPTION

Of three well-qualified candidates, Cllr Brian Richards proposed co-opting Rolf van Apeldoorn to fill the vacancy left by James Butler's resignation. Seconded by Cllr Val McPherson. All present agreed.

13/ PUBLIC MEETING ON THE PROPOSED SOLAR FARM AT BUMPERS FARM, ILMER LANE, ILMER

Cllr Graham Walters proposed holding a public meeting between Ilmer residents and a WDC planning officer, to be held on 18th August; Cllr Walters to draft the notice. Seconded by Cllr Brian Richards; all present agreed. SG/GW

14/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

14/06582/FUL: Land North Of Bumpers Farm, Ilmer Lane, Ilmer — construction of a ground-mounted solar farm with inverter enclosures, DNO cabin, security fencing/CCTV and access tracks. The Parish Council requested an extension to respond until after the 6th August site meeting and 18th August public meeting; Cllrs Richards, McPherson and Rogers to form the Council's response afterwards. BR/VM/JR

14/06581/FUL: Rose Farm, Thame Road, Longwick — amendment to red edge for change of use between agricultural and residential with a detached garage and shed. No objections.

Approved: 14/06140/FUL (Mistlethrush Barn); 14/06112/FUL (OS Parcel 9547, Owlswick). Refused (Tree Preservation): 14/06351/CTR (Water Springs, Meadle).

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 16th September 2014 at 8.00pm. The meeting closed at 10.15pm.
