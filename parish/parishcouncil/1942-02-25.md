Meeting of the Longwick-cum-Ilmer Parish Council held in the Schoolroom, Longwick on Wednesday February 25th 1942 at 7 pm. To read and confirm Minutes of last meeting. Nomination of Parish Constables. Payment of accounts. Present:- Councillors Wm A. Walker (Chairman) H. S. Pope. E. J. Girling. F Williams and Robert Bailey (Clerk).

[Margin: Minutes] Minutes of last meeting read and confirmed.

[Margin: Parish Constables] Proposed by Councillor Pope seconded by Councillor E. J. Girling that the out going Parish Constables be re-nominated namely:- Messrs Owen Goodchild, Fred Williams, Harry Goodchild, Charles Tucker and Ernest Thomas Rutland. Carried

[Margin: Payment of account] Proposed by Councillor Williams, seconded by Councillor Girling, that a Cheque be drawn in payment of the following account:- "Logonia" Premium on Clerks Bond 3/-d. Carried.

[Margin: Date of Annual Parish Meeting] It was agreed that the Annual Parish Meeting be held at the Schoolroom, Longwick, on Monday March 23rd 1942 at 7 pm to be followed by a meeting of the Parish Council. Wm A Walker
