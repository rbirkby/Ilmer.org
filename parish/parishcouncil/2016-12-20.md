PRESENT: Cllr Mrs Valerie McPherson BEM (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Sally Whitworth, Jane Rogers, Rolf van Apeldoorn and Brian Richards, Clerk Susanne Griffiths, Deputy Clerk Jayne Mylchreest, 6 members of the public. A formal petition against the Rose Farm development was handed to the Council.

Apologies were received and accepted from Cllrs Whitworth and Rogers (unwell). The Minutes of 15th November 2016 were approved and signed. No interests were declared. No matters arose outside the agenda.

Cllr Bendyshe-Brown (BCC) reported on the Wellington House development (advertising board removed), VAS funding efforts, the winter programme (Thame Road/Ilmer Road footway, Owlswick 30mph sign), the Scout Hut hedge, Network Rail bridge repairs at Walnut Tree Lane, and the Local Plan.

FINANCE: Payments including salaries, pension, grass cutting/maintenance, risk assessment, domain renewal, newsletter administration, PAYE, Village Hall hire, sign cleaning, recycling-facility travel and £649.66 to the Chiltern Society for path clearance. Bank Balance £21,282.00.

CAPITAL ITEMS: A medium-risk skate park surface issue was approved for repair (£480+VAT), to be carried out once frost cleared. JM. No issues in the November play area inspection.

CORRESPONDENCE: Tea at Three thanked the Council for a £50 grant. A further BT phone-box removal consultation was noted. A draft BCC/developer agreement dedicating a public path at Wellington House was welcomed. JM. Agreed playing field access for tree works on 28th–29th December, gates to be locked afterward by the contractor. SG/VM. A residents' petition (Walnut Tree Lane, Orchard Close, Dorrells Road) sought removal of Rose Farm development from the revised Neighbourhood Plan on flood-risk, infrastructure and heritage grounds; a detailed formal response was given (reproduced as an appendix), clarifying that the Neighbourhood Plan cannot "undo" the separately-consulted Rose Farm application (objected to by the Council on 21st August 2016), cannot allocate less housing than the Local Plan requires, and that CIL funding discussions were unrelated to the New Homes Bonus. A Thames Water letter regarding a Sports Club water account was noted pending the asset-transfer decision.

9/ PLAY EQUIPMENT FUNDRAISING

Cllrs Whitworth and McPherson to meet the resident fundraising group to discuss funds raised and preferred equipment. VM/SW

10/ PARISH COUNCIL LOGO

A resident was continuing to research logo designs; the Clerk to seek an update. SG/JM

11/ COUNCILLOR VACANCY BY CO-OPTION

Agreed to continue advertising the vacancy until 31st March 2017 via noticeboard, website and newsletter. SG

12/ LONGWICK SPORTS CLUB ASSETS

A Trustee attended and appealed for the Council to retain the Club's facilities (changing rooms, floodlights, groundsman's equipment, goal posts and pitch equipment) for future community sporting use rather than lose them. Following an update on talks with the Village Hall Committee, agreed to defer the purchase decision to January, with the Clerk to establish costs from the Trustees. JM

13/ PLAYING FIELD AND VILLAGE HALL

Considered a proposal from Risborough Rangers Junior Football Club to use the playing field for girls' and pan-disability training/matches (August–April); deferred to January.

14/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

No objections: 16/08289/FUL (40 Bell Crescent); 16/07997/FUL (8 Lower Icknield Way); 16/08189/FUL (The Old Vicarage, Ilmer). 16/08104/FUL (Old Orchard, Thame Road): Cllrs McPherson and Walker to site-visit and circulate comments. Approved: 16/07742/FUL (The Cobblers); 16/07465/FUL (The Rosary, Ilmer); 16/07622/LBC (Dock Farm); 15/08455/OUT (OS Parcels 6232 & 7428, Thame Road — 43 dwellings, permitted with planning obligation). No TPO made: 16/07659/CTR (The Cobblers). A new Tree Preservation Order (No. 29/2016) was made for trees within the OS Parcel 2075, Thame Road field boundary. Appeal dismissed: 16/05276/PNP3Q (Barn, Longwick Mill). Refused: 16/07950/MIN (Lyndale, Stockwell Lane, Meadle — non-material amendment).

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 17th January 2017 at 7.30pm. The meeting closed at 8.50pm.
