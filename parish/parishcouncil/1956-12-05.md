Longwick Cum Ilmer Parish Council meeting Held in School Room Longwick 5-12-56

Present Councillor W Harper Chairman, Councillors D Claydon, E Girling, O Tapping, F Williams & the Clerk. Apology From Councillor F Walker not bing able to attend owing to another meeting

[Margin: Minutes] The minutes of Last meetg where Read & Confirmed

[Margin: matters Arresing] Clerks expences For annual audit to be left over untill the begining of the next Financial Year

[Margin: Hungarian Relief Fund] the Council are Fully in sympathy with This Fund But owing to the Local Collections & Employers Given in the Local Fectoreis To their Employment We Feel We Cannot go any Further in the matter

[Margin: Trustee] Councillor O Tapping Was Reappointed Trustee To the Following Charities Monks Risborough (Recreation Ground) (Poors Allotment) & (Unknown Doners).

[Margin: Overbridge on Footpath No 22] It Was Pro F Williams Sec D Claydon the Clerk write the County Surveyor Re the Dangerous state of the Overbridge on Footpath No 22.

W J Harper March 20/57
