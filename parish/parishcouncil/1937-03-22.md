Meeting of the Longwick-cum-Ilmer Parish Council held in the Schoolroom, Longwick on Monday March 22nd 1937 at 7.15 pm. To read and confirm Minutes of last meeting. Payment of accounts. Present:- Councillors Wm A. Walker (Chairman) H. S. Pope. F. Williams. W. J. Harper. H. Rogers. A. J. Goodchild, E. J. Girling and Mr Robert Bailey (Clerk)

[Margin: Minutes] Minutes of last meeting read and confirmed.

[Margin: Payment of Accounts] Proposed by Councillor F. Williams, seconded by Councillor H. Rogers that Cheques be drawn in payment of the following accounts:- Mr. J. Redrup, Repairs to Foot bridge leading from Longwick to Bledlow 17/-d. Treasurer Village Hall, Longwick. Hire of Hall for Parish meetings. 10/-d. Correspondent Longwick School Donation for use of School-room for meetings 10/-d. Messrs Shaw & Sons Ltd. Forms for Parish Council Election, 14/3d. Cheverton & Laidler Printing Notices of Parish meeting re Coronation celebrations 6/6d. Robert Bailey One Quarters Salary as Clerk to 25/3/37. £1/5/-. Premium on Clerks Bond 3/-. Postages (one year) 8/6d. Total Cheque £1/16/6. Carried

[Margin: Impression of seal] An expression of appreciation of the services of Councillor H. Rogers who was retiring from the Council was made by the Chairman to which Councillor Rogers suitably replied. Wm A Walker
