PRESENT: Cllr Mrs Valerie McPherson BEM (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Sally Whitworth, Jane Rogers, Rolf van Apeldoorn and Brian Richards, Deputy Clerk Jayne Mylchreest, 3 members of the public.

No apologies were received. The Minutes of 20th December 2016 were approved and signed. No interests were declared. The resident researching a Council logo design was being followed up.

Cllr Bendyshe-Brown (BCC) reported on Bar Lane flooding, Owlswick 30mph signs, the Meadle bridleway, Owlswick Road, HS2 mitigation funding to include Longwick, VAS funding, Horsenden Lane, Meadle refuse vehicle access, and a 3–4 year roads/footpaths review.

FINANCE: Payments including salaries, pension, grass cutting, risk assessment, a skatepark repair (£576), Bucks Playing Field Association subscription, dog waste collection and Carol concert refreshments. Bank Balance £19,880.75.

CAPITAL ITEMS: A medium-risk skate ramp crack from December had been repaired; no other issues in the December Risk Inspection Reports.

9/ NEIGHBOURHOOD PLAN REPORT

Following the November public meeting's decision to continue, the Steering Group's consultant was progressing the plan ahead of solicitor review, with a further update expected in March. JM

10/ PLAY EQUIPMENT FUNDRAISING

Cllrs Whitworth and McPherson reported £1,655.64 raised to date; residents to investigate suitable equipment within space and safety constraints while continuing to fundraise.

11/ RISBOROUGH RANGERS JUNIOR FOOTBALL CLUB PLAYING FIELD USE

Agreed to permit use as previously outlined, excluding school holidays, subject to risk assessment, insurance and a signed playing field regulations policy, to be reviewed after one year. JM

12/ TRANSFER OF LONGWICK SPORTS CLUB ASSETS

Agreed (5–1 abstention) to accept the transfer of assets from the Sports Club Trustees for a nominal £1, on the understanding that the Trustees dispose of any equipment the Council does not require and clarify the dispersal of Trustee funds. VM/BR

13/ INTERNAL AUDITOR APPOINTMENT

Appointed Mr Don Timms for 2016/2017.

14/ BUDGET AND PRECEPT 2017/2018

Agreed the budget and a precept of £27,470.00, following a reduced WDC council tax base grant of £173.15. SG

15/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

Objected: 16/08471/FUL (Owlswick Barn — 4-bed dwelling conversion), for design, appearance, scale and proportion failing to reflect the local rural character. No objections: 16/08424/FUL (Folly Cottage, Ilmer — alternative scheme). Fully supported: 17/05023/FUL (Lyndale, Stockwell Lane, Meadle). Approved: 16/06864/OUT (Land rear of Hazeldene Lodge); 16/07898/FUL (36 Walnut Tree Lane); 16/08195/MIN (Land north of Bumpers Farm, Ilmer — solar farm amendment). Refused: 15/07604/FUL and 15/07389/FUL (both Land adjacent Home Farm, Thame Road).

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 21st February 2017 at 7.30pm. The meeting closed at 9.10pm.
