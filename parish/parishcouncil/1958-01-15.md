Longwick Cum Ilmer Parish Council meeting Held Schoolroom Longwick Jan 15th 1958

Present Councillors Harper Chairman, Claydon, Williams, Tapping & the Clerk. apologize Received From Councillor Walker owing to other Business unable to attend

[Margin: Minutes] The minutes of Last meetg where Read & Confirmed

[Margin: Re Stile on Footpath 3] It was agreed the Clerk get in touch with Mr Peirce on Repairing same also state of Road at the Garage Stockwell lane.

[Margin: Martin Banks Chamber Insurance Pamphlet] It Was agreed the Pamphlet Dealing With Councillors Insurance be left on the Table

Date of Annual Parish meeting to be march 20th meetg Closed 8.30pm W J Harper March 20/58
