LONGWICK. CUM. ILMER PARISH COUNCIL Meeting held Village Hall Wednesday 1st April 1964 at 7.0 p.m.

[Margin: Present.] Cllr Harper, Leuw, Gomme, Tapping, Mrs Brock & Miss Wootton. [Margin: Absent] An apology for absence Cllr Walker. [Margin: Mins.] Minutes of the last meeting were read and confirmed.

[Margin: 1d Rate] A query on the 1d rate. as the parish council precept for £25. and the 1d rate is said to bring in £88 what happens to the other £63. the clerk was asked to write to Mr Crisp.

[Margin: Litter Bin at Meadle] Cllr Brock informed the meeting that the litter bin which was placed on an electric light pole at Meadle was no longer there owing to a new pole being erected, and that as it was never used suggested it be placed at the Longwick cross roads bus stop.

[Margin: Finance] The following accounts were settled Clerks Salary Dec to March £4 - 0 - 0 together with 7/0 Postage and 5/0 Ocean Ins. The cost of Hiring the Village Hall 18/- for two meetings @ 9/- a meeting.

Meeting closed 7.50 p.m. W J Harper 25/5/64
