Longwick-cum Ilmer Parish Council Meeting Held 7.30 pm 29th May 61 School room Longwick

[Margin: Present] Councillors Harper, Claydon Gomme, Tappin, Mrs Brock

[Margin: Mins] Mins of last meeting were read and confirmed, matters arising dealt with

[Margin: Election of Officers for ensuing Year] Proposed by Councillor Gomme and seconded by Cllr Tappin that Councillor Harper continue as chairman carried. Proposal by Cllr Tappin seconded by Cllr Gomme that C Claydon continue as Vice chairman, carried

[Margin: Bridleway 29 Meadle] A letter from Mr Dickson of Meadle was read complaining of the installation of Bridleway signs over a footpath in Mr Dicksons field at Meadle. It was agreed that the clerk should write to the Bucks C.C. stressing this Councils concern in this matter, with a request that they take this matter up with Mr Dickson

[Margin: Ilmer Rd] It was proposed by Cllr Claydon and seconded by Cllr Gomme that the clerk should write to Mr Nobbs (Div Surveyor) regarding the road to Ilmer

village. It should be stressed that the road surface is bad, and due to the increased use of this road some form of warning boards "Caution" or "Danger" might help to avert an accident in the future Carried.

W J Harper
