PRESENT: Cllr Mrs Valerie McPherson BEM (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Jane Rogers, Sally Whitworth, Rolf van Apeldoorn and Brian Richards, Clerk Mrs Susanne Griffiths, Clerical Assistant Jayne Mylchreest, 4 members of the public.

Cllr McPherson was re-elected Chairman (proposed Rogers, seconded Whitworth) and signed the Declaration of Acceptance of Office. Cllr Richards was re-elected Vice Chairman (proposed Whitworth, seconded Walker). No apologies were received. The Minutes of 19th April 2016 were approved and signed. No interests were declared.

Cllr Bendyshe-Brown (BCC) reported on: fly tipping in Owlswick; a Local Area Forum application for Vehicle Activation Signs on Thame Road and Chestnut Way; Ilmer Road's inclusion in the resurfacing programme; repair of Scout Hut fencing (despite not being BCC-owned); Brexit/Remain campaign presentations in Princes Risborough; and an 11th June street party for the Queen's 90th birthday.

8/ OUTSIDE BODY REPRESENTATION

Agreed: Cllr Rogers (NW Chilterns Local Plan Area Forum); Cllr Walker (WDALC).

FINANCE: Payments including salaries, pension, grass cutting, risk assessment, the 2015/16 internal audit fee (£25) and the BALC annual subscription (£197.20). Receipts included an HMRC VAT repayment of £2,877.13 and £11,654.89 from WDC. Bank Balance £31,239.58.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No issues in the April Playing Field Risk Inspection. A medium-risk play area gate issue was resolved by approving (4–2) a £103.91 + VAT self-closing wooden gate. JM

CORRESPONDENCE: Thank-you letters were received from St Peter's Church, Ilmer and St Dunstan's Church for their grants. A WDC insurance/risk assessment training invitation was noted. Agreed to advise a street-trading applicant at the B4009 lay-by that they must keep it litter-free. JM. Agreed in principle to re-site the two Parish Council noticeboards to enable a granted planning application to progress, with the final location deferred. A Centenary Fields Programme invitation was noted. A Local Area Forum Transport for Bucks project application was deferred to the next meeting. The Cricket Club was reminded to give notice for playing field access following the padlock code change. JM

13/ TREE SURVEY REVIEW

Cllrs Walker and van Apeldoorn presented a numbered plan of the playing field trees, recommending ivy removal from some trees and monitoring of Tree 16, which leans at 45 degrees.

14/ FLOWER CONTAINERS AT VILLAGE ENTRANCES

A local company had offered free containers for autumn/winter planting, with another donating flowers/bulbs.

15/ INTERNAL AUDITOR'S REPORT 2015/16

Confirmed complete, with all found to be in order.

16/ ACCOUNTS AND ANNUAL GOVERNANCE STATEMENT 2015/16

The Council examined and approved the Annual Return; the Chairman read and signed the Annual Governance Statement.

17/ CHEQUE SIGNATORIES 2016/17

Resolved to retain the four existing councillor signatories.

18/ PLAYING FIELD PARKING POLICY

Agreed that requests to park on the playing field must be made directly to the Parish Council rather than via the Village Hall Committee, for individual consideration.

19/ NEWSLETTER ADVERTISING

Agreed to accept advertising from the Aylesbury Vale Natural Burial Ground and The Princes Centre. JM

20/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

No objections: 16/06109/FUL and 16/06110/LBC (Dock Farm, Meadle — internal works); 16/06084/FUL (Chiltern, Chestnut Way); 16/06046/FUL (Meadle Cottage — garage replacement). 16/06086/CTREE (Dock Farm): to abide by the Arboriculturalist's decision. Approved: 16/05611/FUL (OS Parcel 8955, Askett — chicken sheds/workshop).

22/ EXCLUSION OF PUBLIC AND PRESS

Agreed, under Section 12 of the Public Bodies (Admission to Meetings) Act 1960, to discuss the preferred playground equipment supplier in closed session.

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 21st June 2016 at 7.30pm. The meeting closed at 9.35pm.
