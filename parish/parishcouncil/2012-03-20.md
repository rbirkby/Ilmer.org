1/ PRESENT

Cllr G Harper (Chairman)

APOLOGIES FOR ABSENCE

No apologies were received.

2/ MINUTES

The Minutes of the previous meeting of the Parish Council on the 21st February 2012, having been circulated, were approved by the Meeting and signed by the Chairman.

3/ DECLARATIONS OF INTEREST

Members were asked to declare any personal interest and the nature of that interest which they may have in any of the items under consideration at this meeting. None were declared.

4. PRESENTATION ON THE VALUE OF A COMMUNITY RESILIENCE OR COMMUNITY PLAN, BY MR. A FOREMAN AT W.D.C.

Mr Foreman gave a short briefing and answered various questions raised by the councillors.

5/ MATTERS ARISING NOT OTHERWISE ON THE AGENDA

The trees in Ilmer have been pollarded.

Residents have asked permission to tidy the pond in Ilmer. All agreed.

Cllr V McPherson presented the choice of design for the Diamond Jubilee mugs and the cost involved. All agreed to allow Cllr McPherson to make the final choice.

Cllr B Richards reported on the meeting with RAP. RAP has requested a link to the Parish Council website. All present agreed.

11. PARISH EMERGENCY PLAN

Cllr G Walters offered to co-ordinate the Parish Emergency Plan for Longwick-cum-Ilmer Parish. Cllr G Harper agreed to contact the farmers in Ilmer, Cllr J Rogers in Owlswick and Cllr V McPherson in Longwick. Cllr G Walters agreed to write to Louise Willis at BCC to request the names of volunteers in the parish who had agreed to be included in the Snow Saviours.

12. PLANNING APPLICATIONS

New Applications to be considered:

- 12/05362/FUL: Field View Barn, Ilmer Lane, Ilmer. Proposal: Householder application for construction of new front porch and insertion of two velux windows into existing rear roof slope. The Parish Council will comment after the site visit.
- 12/05555/CTREE: The Old Vicarage, Ilmer Lane, Ilmer. Proposal: Remove one trunk of 1 twin-trunked Ash Tree (T1) and remove one large branch on 1 Ash Tree (T2). The Parish Council will abide by the decision of the Arboriculturalist.
- 12/05603/CTREE: Horsenden Manor, Horsenden Lane, Princes Risborough. Proposal: Crown lift to 6 metres and shorten mid crown laterals over The Stables roof by 1 metre to one Lime (T1); crown lift and reduce overhang to The Stables by about 1 metre from the boundary fence to one Yew (T2); crown lift to 7 metres, remove major dead wood and hangers to one London plane (T3) and remove low growth over garage roof; reduce crown spread over The Stables parking area by 25% to one Yew (T4). The Parish Council will abide by the decision of the Arboriculturalist.
- 12/05491/OUT: Sheredon, Thame Road, Longwick. Proposal: Outline application for demolition of existing bungalow & erection of a two storey 4-bed detached dwelling with attached double carport & car parking to front, and one pair of 3-bed semi-detached dwellings with car parking to front, all accessed via existing access. The Parish Council recommend refusal of this application for the planned single dwelling at the rear of the development. The proposed development is on undeveloped land, the residential garden of Sheredon, and is considered to be an overdevelopment of the plot.
- 12/05447/CTREE: Orchard Farm House, Meadle Village Road, Meadle. Proposal: Cut down to ground and poison stump on one Ash Tree (T1). The Parish Council has no objections.

Progress on applications already considered:

- 11/07826/FUL — Application Permitted. Address: Armour Farm Barn, Stockwell Lane, Little Meadle. Proposal: Construction of first floor extension over existing garage and associated external and internal alterations.

To discuss and decide on the Diamond Jubilee Celebrations. To receive an update on the order of mugs to celebrate the Diamond Jubilee.
