12\. PLANNING APPLICATIONS

A. To consider New Planning Applications

- 12/05362/FUL: Field View Barn, Ilmer Lane, Ilmer, Buckinghamshire HP27 9RA. Proposal: Householder application for construction of new front porch and insertion of two velux windows into existing rear roof slope. The Parish Council will comment after the site visit.
- 12/05555/CTREE: The Old Vicarage, Ilmer Lane, Ilmer, Buckinghamshire HP27 9RA. Proposal: Remove one trunk of 1 x Twin-trunked Ash Tree (T1) and remove one large branch on 1 x Ash Tree (T2). The Parish Council will abide by the decision of the Arboriculturalist.
- 12/05603/CTREE: Horsenden Manor, Horsenden Lane, Princes Risborough, Buckinghamshire HP27 9NF. Proposal: Crown lift to 6 metres. Shorten mid crown laterals over The Stables roof by 1 metre only to one Lime (T1), crown lift and reduce overhang to The Stables to about 1 metre from the boundary fence to one Yew (T2), crown lift to 7 metres. Remove major dead wood and hangers to one London plane (T3) and remove low growth over garage roof to give 1 metre clear over the roof. Reduce crown spread over The Stables parking area by 25%, ie: about 2 metres to be reduced off branch tips on this side only to one Yew (T4). The Parish Council will abide by the decision of the Arboriculturalist.
- 12/05491/OUT: Sheredon, Thame Road, Longwick, Buckinghamshire HP27 9SF. Proposal: Outline application (including details of means of access, appearance, scale & layout) for demolition of existing bungalow & erection of two storey 4-bed detached dwelling with attached double carport & car parking to front, one pair of 3-bed semi-detached dwellings with car parking to front & all accessed via existing access. The Parish Council recommend refusal of this application for the planned single dwelling at the rear of the development. The proposed development is on undeveloped land, the residential garden of Sheredon and is considered to be an overdevelopment of the plot.
- 12/05447/CTREE: Orchard Farm House, Meadle Village Road, Meadle, Buckinghamshire HP17 9UD. Proposal: Cut down to ground and poison stump on one Ash Tree (T1). The Parish Council has no objections.
