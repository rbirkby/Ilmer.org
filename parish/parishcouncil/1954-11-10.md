Longwick Cum Ilmer Parish Council meeting Held in School Room Longwick Nov 10th 1954 Present Councillor W Harper Chairman, Councillor Claridge, Claydon, Tapping, Miss Walker, F Williams & The Clerk. To Read & Confirm minutes of Last meeting matters arising, Correspondence, Any Other Business

The minutes of the Last meeting Where Read & Confirmed

[Margin: Water Supply Ilmer] It was agreed to Hold This matter over until Further Communication Was Received From the R D C

[Margin: No 7 Path Kingsey] It was agreed. That Councillors Harper & Claridge interview Mr Lane Kinsey & Report Developments To The Clerk.

[Margin: Any other Business] Pro Councillor Williams Sec O Tapping The Clerk To Write District Surveyor Re the The state of the Gutter on the Ilmer Road., The Road outside the Three Pigeons Public House & The General state of Footpath on Right Hand Side Through the Village of Longwick W J Harper Feb 23 / 55
