PRESENT: Cllr Val McPherson BEM (Chairman, arrived 7.50pm), Cllrs Ian Walker, Sally Whitworth, Jane Rogers, Rolf van Apeldoorn and Brian Richards, Clerk Susanne Griffiths, Deputy Clerk Jayne Mylchreest, 1 member of the public. Cllr Richards chaired until the Chairman's arrival.

No apologies were formally recorded beyond the Chairman's delay. The Minutes of 21st February 2017 were approved and signed. No interests were declared. Three applications had been received for the councillor vacancy; interviews were arranged for 10th April. JR/JM

Cllr Bendyshe-Brown reported on completed Horsenden Lane ditch works, ongoing VAS funding, a repaired Thame Road VAS, the Longwick Fete, footpath repairs (Chestnut Way to Walnut Tree Lane), the dismissed Park Mill Farm appeal, Devolved Services, Owlswick bridleway/lane resurfacing, and a blocked, designation-removed Footpath 21C.

FINANCE: Payments including salaries, pension, risk assessment, PAYE, and a year's postage/mileage. Bank Balance £18,141.12.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No issues in the February Risk Inspection Reports; a hedge-cutting contractor was being chased and a fallen low-risk tree ordered removed. SW/JM

CORRESPONDENCE: Approved solicitor's costs (not exceeding £500+VAT) to draw up the Sports Club asset transfer agreement, with future transfer costs borne by the transferor. SG. A resident's Youth Club query was referred to the Scout Group and BCC Youth Services. JM. Approved a new street-trading application for the Thame Road lay-by, subject to litter responsibility (5–1 abstention). A draft WDC Play Strategy 2017–2022 was noted for comment. Agreed playing field car parking for the Princes Risborough Music Society's summer concerts (29–30 June, 1 July). JM. Noted invitations to a BMKALC planning course, a CPRE planning roadshow (Cllr Rogers to attend), and a WW1 centenary co-ordination meeting. Agreed to publicise the Chilterns Conservation Board's "Beacons of the Past" project via the newsletter, without financial contribution. VM. A resident's query on the ~20% precept increase was answered (12½p/week for a Band D property, driven by Neighbourhood Plan costs), with an invitation to the Annual Parish Meeting. JM. Agreed a bouncy castle on the playing field (15th April), subject to standard documentation. JM. EON's transfer of the playing field electricity supply was noted, with the Clerk to establish running costs. JM. Cllr McPherson to liaise with Risborough Rangers on marking out the football pitch. VM. Agreed to arrange an informal meeting with Croudace Homes on their Reserved Matters proposals for the 43-dwelling Thame Road site (15/08455/OUT). JM

9/ PLAY EQUIPMENT FUNDRAISING

A bingo evening raised a further £239.60; suitable equipment still being investigated.

10/ NEIGHBOURHOOD PLAN REPORT

Progress remained on hold pending WDC advice following the Government's Housing White Paper and its effect on the Local Plan.

11/ INSURANCE POLICY REVIEW

Agreed to renew and increase cover by £4,000 to include the garage due for transfer from the Sports Club Trustees.

12/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

Objected: 16/08471/FUL (Owlswick Barn), as before, for design/character reasons. Objected strongly: 17/05474/FUL (The Old Stable, Meadle Village Road — retrospective stable/tack room), citing the applicant's disregard for planning process and a deliberate breach of footpath access (a missing footpath sign, a padlocked gate and electric fencing crossing Footpath 21c), recommending refusal until resolved. No objections: 17/05621/FUL and 17/05621/LBC (Meadle Cottage — driveway alterations). Approved: 17/05023/FUL (Lyndale, Meadle); 16/08189/FUL (The Old Vicarage, Ilmer); 16/08424/FUL (Folly Cottage, Ilmer); 16/08289/FUL (40 Bell Crescent). Withdrawn: 16/07958/FUL (Saddleback Barn — round chapel).

13/ EXCLUSION OF PUBLIC AND PRESS

Agreed, under Section 12 of the 1960 Act, to discuss future website provision in closed session.

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 18th April 2017 at 8.00pm, preceded by the Annual Parish Meeting at 7.00pm. The meeting closed at 9.10pm.
