Longwick Cum Ilmer Parish Council Annual meeting Held in Schoolroom Longwick 16-5-56 Present Councillor W J Harper Chairman Councillors D Claydon, E J Girling, O Tapping F m Walker, F Williams & The Clerk.

To Read & Confirm minutes of Last meeting matters arresing, Correspondance, Election of Chairman & Vice Chairman Payment of accounts Ay Other Business

[Margin: Minutes] The minutes of the Last meeting Where Read & Confirmd

[Margin: Chilton Creamatorium] A letter Was Read From the R D C Re Information Required For the amount of space available left For Burials in the Church yards in our Parish The Clerk Was instructed to Do necessary Information

[Margin: Accounts] Pro Councillor F Walker Sec Councillor Tapping That the Follany accounts be Paid 10/- Audit Stamp 1-7-6 affiliation County of Buckingham association of Parish Councils Carried

[Margin: Election of Chairman & Vice Chairm] Pro Councillr Girling Sec Councillor Walker that Councillor W J Harper be Relected Chairman Carried Pro Councillr Girling Sec Councillor Tapping That Councillor D Claydon be Relected Vice Chairman Carried

[Margin: Ay other Business] A Complaint Was Brought by Councillor Williams Re Barbed Wire ben across a Footpath on Mrs Macintyres Farm This Was left for Further investigation

W J Harper 10/10/56
