Longwick Cum Ilmer Parish Council meeting Held in School Room Longwick May 5th 1954 To Read & Confirm minutes of Last meeting, matters arising, Correspondence, Payment of Accounts & any Other Business Election of Chairman & V Chairman Present Councillor Harper Chairman, Councillor D Claydon L W. Claridge, O Tapping, Miss F M Walker F Williams & the Clerk

[Margin: Minutes] The minutes of the Last meeting Where Read & Confirmed

[Margin: Correspondence] Re Insurance. Pro Councillor Williams Sec D Claydon The Clerk Write Mr Stokes For a Premium explaining the Circumstances of the Parish Reguement Carried

[Margin: Payment of Accounts] Pro Miss F Walker Sec L Claridge the Following accounts be Paid One Pound For audit Stamp One Pound Ten shillings For affiliation Fees to the County of Buckingham association of Parish Councils

[Margin: Election of Chairman & V Chairman] Pro L Claridge Sec Miss F Walker that Councillor Harper Be Re Elected as Chairman Carried Pro L Claridge Sec F Williams That Councillor Claydon Be Re Elected as Vice Chairman Carried

[Margin: Any Other Business] Pro L Claridge Sec D Claydon The Clerk Write the Surveyor Re Heaps of material Left on Road Side For Indefinite Periods at Ilmer also the Condition of the Road at Owlswick Corner End of Bar Lane Carried W J Harper
