Members were asked to declare any personal interest and the nature of that interest which they may have in any of the items under consideration at this meeting. None were declared.

Cllr Bill Bendyshe-Brown was unable to attend.

7/ CAPITAL ITEMS AND COMMUNITY FACILITIES

Playing Field – there were no medium or high risk issues highlighted in the Risk Inspection Report for May 2015. All present agreed that the Choral Society be allowed to use the playing field for car parking as in previous years, subject to suitability at the time. 9/ CORRESPONDENCE

- Letter from Wycombe District Council requesting the Community Covenant to be signed. All present agreed that Cllr Brian Richards sign the Covenant, a statement of mutual support between the civilian and local armed forces community, aiming to help service personnel families and veterans within RAF High Wycombe access help and support.
- Email from the Longwick Village Hall Committee stating that trees belonging to the Parish Council are hanging over the car park, blocking a parking space. Cllr Val McPherson proposed that the Parish Council has no objection to the Village Hall Committee cutting back the trees themselves. Seconded by Cllr Brian Richards. All present agreed.
- Invitation from Wycombe District Council to attend Armed Forces Day – Flag raising ceremony, Monday 22nd June 2015. Noted; all present agreed for Cllr Brian Richards to attend.
- Email from Bucks County Council to advise that the footpath in Walnut Tree Lane has been declared "surplus to requirements" by Longwick School. It has been confirmed that BCC hold the title to the property, and BCC Property Services have confirmed they will undertake necessary resurfacing works with a view to the path being adopted by BCC Highways. Noted.
- Letter from The Pensions Regulator re Automatic enrolment, staging date 1/1/2016. All present agreed to include this as an agenda item for the next meeting.
- Email request to install a bouncy castle on grass outside the Village Hall, 25th July 2015. All present agreed, subject to a risk assessment and public liability insurance being provided in advance.
- Email received from a resident regarding the ownership of the plot of land outside Bell Crescent. The resident has been advised that the land is owned by Wycombe District Council.

10/ A REPORT FROM THE STEERING GROUP ON THE NEIGHBOURHOOD PLAN

Please see Appendix 1 for the new page on the Longwick-cum-Ilmer Parish website from the Neighbourhood Plan Steering Group.

11/ THE PREFERRED CANDIDATE TO FILL THE VACANCY FOR A COUNCILLOR BY CO-OPTION

Sally Whitworth applied to be considered to fill the vacancy for a councillor by co-option; there were no further applications. Cllr Jane Rogers proposed that the council co-opt Sally Whitworth. Seconded by Cllr Rolf van Apeldoorn. All present agreed.

12/ A REVIEW OF THE BANK SIGNATORY LIST

A review of the bank signatory list was undertaken. It was agreed to remove two signatories no longer Councillors and to add Cllr Sally Whitworth.

13/ THE REQUEST FOR A TRAFFIC CALMING INITIATIVE FOR CHESTNUT WAY TO BE FUNDED THROUGH THE LOCAL AREA FORUM

Cllr Rolf van Apeldoorn proposed that the Clerk request a meeting between Cllrs Val McPherson and Rolf van Apeldoorn, Bucks County Councillor Bill Bendyshe-Brown and the Local Area Technician to discuss the possible options. Seconded by Cllr Val McPherson. All present agreed.

14/ A REVIEW OF THE STANDING ORDERS

All present agreed for the Clerk to circulate a version of the updated standing orders for the members to consider and approve at the next meeting.

15/ COMMERCIAL ADVERTISING IN THE PARISH NEWSLETTER

All present agreed to trial commercial advertising in the newsletter to pay for an outside source to produce the newsletter. Cllr Brian Richards proposed that the Clerk arrange a meeting with Princes Risborough Town Council to discuss the options. Seconded by Cllr Rolf van Apeldoorn. All present agreed.

16/ LITTER IN THE VILLAGE

Cllr Val McPherson stated the litter in the village is a problem, particularly by the garage and the village shop, and proposed that the Clerk contact Wycombe District Council to request a free "Take your Litter Home Sign".

17/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

New Planning Applications:

- 15/06161/OUT: Boxer Road/Barn Road (OS Parcel 9166 Boxer Road & OS Parcel 6576 Walnut Tree Lane). Outline application (including details of access) for residential development of up to 160 dwellings with vehicle access from Boxer Road/Barn Road, pedestrian and cycle access to Walnut Tree Lane, parking, public open space and landscaping. Cllr Val McPherson proposed the following response, seconded by Cllr Brian Richards, all present agreed: Longwick cum Ilmer Parish Council urge the District Council to refuse this application, as it is not very different from the earlier application 14/06965/OUT which the District Council unanimously recently decided would have been refused. The reasons for objection: it does not conform to the visions, objectives or site allocations of the Draft Neighbourhood Plan, which proposes development on a number of smaller sites spread through the main area of the village rather than one larger site; it is out of scale and character within the settlement; it would increase traffic and risk to road safety due to site access concentrated through one route; it would not be sustainable, as there is no bus service and it would be too reliant on private cars; and it does not provide a direct safe cycle route to Princes Risborough rail station.
- 15/06445/MINAMD: The Old Stables, Meadle Village Road, Meadle. Proposed non-material amendment to householder application for construction of a single storey glazed rear infill extension linking store and replacement garage, granted under 12/07849/FUL. Noted.
- 15/06332/OUT: Land adjacent Holly Tree Poultry Farm, Askett Village Lane, Askett. Outline application with all matters reserved for demolition of all buildings and structures and redevelopment to provide up to 152 dwellings and public open space. Cllr Val McPherson proposed the same objection as above (visions/objectives/site allocations; scale and character; traffic and road safety; lack of bus service; no safe cycle route to the station). Seconded by Cllr Jane Rogers. All present agreed.
- 15/0658/FUL: Chiltern, Chestnut Way, Longwick. Householder application for construction of single storey front and rear extensions and associated alterations. All present agreed for Cllr Rolf van Apeldoorn to make a site visit before a decision could be made; the Parish Council has no objection.

Notice of Planning Applications Approved:

- 15/05872/FUL: Risborough Re-upholstery, Walkers Yard, Thame Road, Longwick. Change of use & alterations to Unit D from 1 B1 (Light Industrial) unit to create 2 x B1 (Light Industrial) units & construction of new porch canopy. Application Permitted.
- 15/05969/FUL: The Flint House, Owlswick. Householder application for construction of part two storey, part single storey extension to south elevation with extension to terrace and steps. Application Permitted.

Notice of Planning Applications Refused: none.

Notice of Applications Withdrawn: none.

Notice of Appeals in Progress:

- 14/06965/OUT: Boxer Road/Barn Road (OS Parcel 9166 Boxer Road & OS Parcel 6576 Walnut Tree Lane). Outline application for residential development of up to 160 dwellings.

16/ AGENDA ITEMS FOR THE NEXT MEETING

To receive a report from the Steering Group for the Neighbourhood Plan and decide on any actions to be taken. To approve the review of the Standing Orders. To discuss and decide on the action to be taken regarding The Pensions Regulator automatic enrolment.

17/ NEXT MEETING

The next meeting of the Parish Council will take place on Tuesday 21st July 2015 in Longwick Village Hall at 7.45pm. There being no further business to discuss the Meeting closed at 9.33pm.
