Meeting of Longwick Cum Ilmer Parish Council Held in School Room Longwick September 17th 1952 To Read & Confirm minutes of Last meeting matters arresing, Payment of accounts To Issue a Precept, any other Business Present Councillors J Harper Chairman, L W Claridge Owen Tapping, F Williams., apologies From Miss Walker & the Clerk.

[Margin: Minutes] The minutes of Last meeting where Read & Confirmed

[Margin: matters arising] Re Telephone Pole & Hydrants Owlswick where Dealt with no Further approach being Possible.

[Margin: Precept] Pro Councillor F Williams Sec Councillor Claridge That we Issue a Precept on the Rating authority For 10 Pounds

[Margin: Payment of accounts] Pro Councillor Tapping Sec Councillor Williams The Following accounts Be Paid Clerk Quarterly Salary 2 - 0 - 0 audit Stamp 10/- Employers Liability act 5/- Postag 5/4.

[Margin: Spl Parish meeting] Pro Councillor Claridge Seconde Councillor Williams That a Spl Parish meeting be Called on September Oct 7th at the Village Hall at 7.30 pm Re Coronation Festivities.

[Margin: Any other Business] Councillor Harper Volunteered to bring Forward The matter of Ilmer Water Extension at the Parish Council association meeting. W. J Harper
