LONGWICK-CUM-ILMER PARISH COUNCIL Meeting held 7.30 pm 22nd March 60 School room Longwick

[Margin: Present] Councillors Harper Huntley ~~Wootton~~ Tappin. Blaydon.

[Margin: Minutes] Minutes of last meeting were read and confirmed, matters arising were dealt with

[Margin: Election] It was agreed that the clerk should agree, to issue nomination papers for the coming Parish election, and that Mr Rysdale should be notified accordingly

[Margin: Precept] The question of the Precept for 1960/1 was discussed and it was agreed that £20 should be sufficient

[Margin: Hedges] It was proposed by C Walker and seconded by C Tappin that the clerk should contact the Div Surveyor regarding the hedge in the Bledlow Rd (believed owned by Mr Woods) with a request that it should be trimmed back

[Margin: Culverts] It was also proposed by these Councillors that the condition of certain culverts on the Monks Risboro Rd should be mentioned

[Margin: Finance] It was proposed by Councillor Claydon and seconded by Councillor Walker that the following accounts should be paid Clerks Salary Five months ended 31st March 1960 £5-0-0 Postage 1/9 Carried

Meeting Closed 8.25 pm W. J Harper
