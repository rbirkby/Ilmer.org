2/ MINUTES

The Minutes of the previous meetings of the Parish Council on the 21st February 2012 and 20th March, having been circulated, were approved by the Meeting and signed by the Chairman.

3/ DECLARATIONS OF INTEREST

Members were asked to declare any personal interest and the nature of that interest which they may have in any of the items under consideration at this meeting. The Clerk reminded the members that personal and prejudicial interests are defined fully in the Parish Code of Conduct: a parish councillor has a personal interest in a matter if it affects the well-being or financial position of themselves, a family member, a close associate or a connected organisation more than it affects the majority of other people in the parish, or if it relates to any interest which must be registered under the Code. A personal interest is prejudicial if it is so significant that a reasonable member of the public would think it likely to affect the member's decision. None were declared.

6. CAPITAL ITEMS AND COMMUNITY FACILITIES

A. Playing Field. Risk Inspection Report: Cllr G Harper confirmed that there was one issue highlighted as a low risk, that paviors are becoming a little overgrown with weeds and moss, which he has weeded himself. Cllr I Walker confirmed that the Scout Path has been trimmed and that three or four Laurel bushes planted by the council had lost bark around the base, probably due to rabbits; all present agreed to replace the bushes in the autumn. Cllr G Harper informed the members that there had been problems with the bins being emptied in the playing field due to a new operative working for the company; Cllr G O'Neill proposed that the situation is monitored and evidence gathered if the bins are found not to be emptied as per the contract. Cllr G Harper has received a letter from a resident apologising for damage caused to the fence by the scout hut in an accident with her car; all present agreed the Clerk should write to advise there will be a cost for repair, and Cllr G Harper agreed to obtain quotes.

B. Children's Play Area. Risk Inspection Report: Cllr G Harper confirmed that there were no issues highlighted.

7. ROADS, VERGES AND RIGHTS OF WAY

10. FORMAL ADOPTION OF A DATA PROTECTION POLICY

All present agreed to adopt the Data Protection Policy as previously circulated. Cllr G Harper proposed that the Clerk is the nominated person to be the Information Officer. Seconded by Cllr J Rogers. All present agreed.

11. AN UPDATE ON THE ORDER OF THE MUGS TO CELEBRATE THE DIAMOND JUBILEE

Cllr V McPherson presented a mug with the Longwick-cum-Ilmer logo for consideration by the members. All present agreed on the choice and quality and thanked Cllr V McPherson for her hard work in organising the order. Cllr V McPherson agreed to contact David Shuttles to ascertain if more mugs could be purchased by residents if required.

12. THE DIAMOND JUBILEE CELEBRATIONS

The following actions were agreed by all present: Cllr G O'Neill to contact a resident who has offered to help organise the celebrations on the 5th June 2012; the Clerk to ascertain the delivery date of the bunting; Cllr G O'Neill to discuss the possibility of distributing the mugs at the school on June 1st with the headmistress.

13. PLANNING APPLICATIONS

New Applications to be considered:

- 12/05825/LBC: Stockwell Lane Farm, Stockwell Lane, Little Meadle. Proposal: Listed Building application for blocking north west window (first floor), rebuilding north west/south west chimney stack to half the width, replacement windows to south west/south east elevation, repairs to brickwork and lintels, replacing gutter/fascia board and installation of anti-vermin guard to south west elevation. The Parish Council will abide by the decision of the Conservation Officer.
- 12/05687/ADV: Princes Risborough Railway Station and other sites, Station Approach, Princes Risborough. Proposal: Display of double sided post mounted information signs (x4) in various locations around Princes Risborough (retrospective). After discussion, Cllr G O'Neill proposed the council respond with "No objections", seconded by Cllr B Richards; a counter proposal by Cllr G Walters to respond with concerns about the sign in the Longwick parish was seconded by Cllr V McPherson. A vote was taken on each proposal and the motion was carried by the Chairman's casting vote to respond with "No objections"; one member abstained.
- 12/05676/AGI: Ray Farm, Thame Road, Longwick. Proposal: Agricultural notification for erection of building to be used for the storage of hay/straw. The Parish Council has no objections.
