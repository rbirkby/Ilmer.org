PRESENT: Cllr Mrs Valerie McPherson BEM (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Jane Rogers, Sally Whitworth, Rolf van Apeldoorn and Brian Richards, Clerk Mrs Susanne Griffiths, Clerical Assistant Jayne Mylchreest, 3 members of the public.

No apologies were received. The Minutes of 15th March 2016 were approved and signed. No interests were declared. No matters arose outside the agenda. Cllr Bendyshe-Brown (BCC) sent apologies.

FINANCE: Payments including salaries, pension, dog waste/litter bin servicing, £75 grants each to St Dunstan's Church (Owlswick), St Peter's Church (Ilmer) and Bledlow PCC, a £250 Village Hall defibrillator grant, the newsletter print run, ICO data protection registration, tree pollarding at Ilmer, and grass cutting/risk assessment. Bank Balance £18,065.03.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No medium or high risk issues in the March Risk Inspection Reports. A new issue-reporting form was introduced for councillors. The Clerk to report a faulty Thame Road VAS and three potholes (Mill Lane, "Restawhile", and the Owlswick Lane/Bar Lane junction), reported under BCC reference numbers. JM

CORRESPONDENCE: A resident's query on uneven outlying verge cutting was referred to Cllr Bendyshe-Brown as a BCC devolved-services matter. JM

10/ PLAY EQUIPMENT WORKING GROUP

A Quiz Night and Bingo Night raised £716.26 and £214.00 respectively. Agreed to sign the Tesco Groundwork landowner permission form to allow the new play equipment to be installed. JM

11/ OS PARCEL 8955 — REPRESENTATION TO THE PRINCES RISBOROUGH TOWN PLAN

Cllr Whitworth to draft a request that WDC consider the land's future use as allotments in the Town Plan. SW/SG

12/ TREE SURVEY REVIEW

Cllrs Walker and van Apeldoorn to prepare a plan identifying the playing field trees for which the Council is responsible, for the next meeting.

13/ VARIABLE BANKERS STANDING ORDERS

Agreed to use variable standing orders for salaries, pension contributions and monthly risk assessments.

14/ COUNCILLOR VACANCY BY CO-OPTION

WDC confirmed no by-election had been requested; agreed to advertise the vacancy on the noticeboard and website until 30th June 2016, with interested applicants invited to the July meeting. SG

15/ FUTURE MAINTENANCE OF ILMER GREEN

Following a resident's withdrawal from voluntary grass cutting, agreed (4–2) to let the grass grow in a natural state. SG

16/ TRANSPARENCY FUND APPLICATION

Agreed to apply for Transparency Fund funding towards a computer for the Clerk. JM

17/ SHELL GARAGE ROUNDABOUT HEDGE AND ADVERTISING

Agreed to ask BCC's Road Safety Officer to site-visit and advise. JM

18/ ANNUAL PARISH CAROL SERVICE

Agreed 20th December 2016 at 6pm. BR

19/ THE BIG LUNCH

Update given for the 12th June event; agreed to permit a separate Ilmer Green "Big Lunch" on 11th June. IW

20/ PARISH NEWSLETTER

Princes Risborough Town Council would prepare the next edition under Cllr McPherson's guidance.

21/ FLOWER CONTAINERS AT VILLAGE ENTRANCES

Cllr Whitworth to obtain a revised quotation for the next meeting. SW

22/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

16/05611/FUL (OS Parcel 8955, Askett Village Lane — chicken sheds, chattel and workshop, retrospective): the Council supported the agricultural/allotment use but objected pending a BCC Highways ruling on the safety and legality of the newly created access onto the B4009 opposite the Stockwell Lane junction, considering it dangerous and unnecessary given existing Askett Lane access. No objections: 16/05852/FUL and 16/05873/FUL (5 Lower Icknield Way; 14 Dorrells Road). Approved: 16/05232/FUL (5 Lower Icknield Way). Noted: the Land off Barn Road appeal (14/06965/OUT) inquiry would sit from 12th April; a separate appeal for Old Oaks, Thame Road (APP/K0425/W/16/3145192) was also in progress.

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 17th May 2016 (Annual Parish Council Meeting) at 7.30pm. The meeting closed at 9.04pm.
