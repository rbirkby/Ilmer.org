8. CORRESPONDENCE

Letters from the Risborough Area Partnership (RAP) concerning the new Notice Board. Cllr G O'Neill proposed that the Parish Council accept the apologies from RAP and agree to take no further action to remove the notice board in the verge on the Risborough Road. Cllr G Walters made a counter proposal that a question is put to the enforcement officer at WDC enquiring whether the correct planning permission had been obtained; the counter proposal did not receive a seconder. A vote was taken; the motion to accept the apologies was passed 4 votes for, none against, with 3 abstentions. All present agreed that the Clerk write to RAP requesting a meeting with the Chairman, Peter Keen, to discuss how the Parish Council can best work with the RAP in the future, with Cllrs G Harper and B Richards to represent the Parish Council at this meeting.

Exhibition on plans for wind turbine at Lower Waldridge Farm, Ford. Cllrs J Rogers and B Richards attended the exhibition and informed the members that a planning application is due to be submitted in the near future.

12. CELEBRATING THE DIAMOND JUBILEE IN THE PARISH

All present agreed that on Tuesday 5th June the Playing Field is to be made available and the Village Hall reserved for the use of residents to meet and bring their own picnics. The free bunting will be used to decorate the field and hall.

Cllr Mrs V McPherson circulated copies of various mugs for councillors to consider; other suggestions included official coins, pens and book markers. A vote was taken and the motion passed 6-1 to purchase mugs. Cllr Mrs V McPherson agreed to obtain prices for mugs similar to those issued by the Parish Council celebrating the Golden Jubilee.

13. A REQUEST FOR MORE DOG WASTE BINS

Cllr G Walters has received a request from a parishioner for the Parish Council to provide dog bins in the village. The Clerk was advised by WDC that there is no automatic system for placing dog bins as a result of requests, that WDC has 200 dog waste bins in the district but no funds for more, and that it is acceptable for dog owners to use their normal refuse bins for dog waste disposal. If the Parish Council decided to purchase a bin, it would need permission from the County Council to erect it, and would be responsible for its maintenance and emptying. Cllr G O'Neill proposed that the Parish Council take no action in response to purchasing dog waste bins. Seconded by Cllr V McPherson. A vote was taken and the motion passed 6-1.
