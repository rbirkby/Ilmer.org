Longwick Cum Ilmer Parish Council meeting Held in School Room Longwick march 30 1954 To Read & Confirm minutes of Last meeting matters arising, Correspondence, Payments of Accounts Election of Parish Constables Any other Business Present Councillors Harper Chairman, D. Claydon Claridge, Girling, Tapping, F Williams & The Clerk, apology Was Received From Councillor F M Walker.

[Margin: The Minutes] The Minutes of the Last meeting Where Read & Confirmed

[Margin: Re Water Supply Ilmer] It Was agreed the Chairman bring this up at the County Executive meeting at Aylesbury

[Margin: Payment of Accounts] Pro Councillor Williams Sec Councillor Tapping That the Followy accounts be Paid Clerks Bond 3/0 Clerks Salary 2-10-0 Postage 8/7 Rent of School 10/0 Carried

[Margin: Election of Parish Constables] Pro Councillor Claydon Sec E Girling The Parish Constables be Elected En Bloc Carried

[Margin: next meeting] It Was agreed the Parish meeting & the next Council meeting Be Held the First week in may W J Harper May 5 / 54
