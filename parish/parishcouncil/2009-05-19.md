11/ CAPITAL ITEMS AND COMMUNITY FACILITIES

Playing Field – Cllr G Harper informed the members that there had been a report that the waste operatives had disposed of rubbish into the hedge. The Clerk had contacted the company, who investigated the allegation. The operative admits throwing green waste from the bin by the cricket square into the hedge as there was no normal rubbish in the bin. After a short discussion, all present agreed that Cllr I Walker would contact the Sports Club who use that bin for green waste to request that it is removed, as it gives the wrong impression to members of the public and is not a litter bin.

Children's Play Area – Cllr G Harper had noticed that the novelty litter bin in the Children's Play Area is being well used and is often full. All present agreed to request the Clerk to order another novelty litter bin for this area. The Risk Inspection Report highlighted that a square of safety surface appears to have been cut out under the swings. All present agreed for the Clerk to arrange for the repair to the safety surface. Cllr I Walker enquired about the timescale for the new memorial bench requested. The Clerk is requested to contact the company to ascertain the delivery date.

12/ ROADS, VERGES AND RIGHTS OF WAY

The Clerk distributed monitoring forms to record excessive movement of HGV/Lorries on village and unsuitable roads. The aim is to use the information to give a local perspective to the size and importance of the issue and thus influence County Council HGV strategy.

The Clerk is requested to raise the following concerns with the relevant local authority:

- The pot holes under the bridge on the Lower Icknield Way opposite Longwick Mill are very dangerous.
- The road sign on the Lower Icknield Way by the garage has been damaged.

13/ CORRESPONDENCE

To be discussed: a request for support from the Hemley Hill Action Group. After a short discussion, all present agreed to oppose the part retrospective planning application for the change of use of land to include the stationing of caravans for 9 gypsy pitches with utility/day room buildings and hard-standing ancillary to that use. The land is near the parish boundary and the members agreed to oppose the application, as it is the opinion of the Parish Council that the proposed development would result in an unsatisfactory and inappropriate form of development out of keeping with the character of the surrounding area. The development would have an adverse impact upon the special character, appearance and enjoyment of the Chilterns AONB and would result in the loss of open space that would detract from the openness of the Green Belt.

A request to use a bouncy castle on the Playing Field: Cllr G Harper has received a request from a resident to place a bouncy castle on the Playing Field as part of a birthday celebration. All present agreed. Cllr G Harper will advise the resident of the Parish Council's decision.

Letters of thanks from the Churches for the grants towards the maintenance of the Churchyards – comments noted.

14/ A REPORT FROM THE WEBSITE WORKING GROUP

The Clerk had contacted all the local organisations to confirm that it was in order for their details to be included on the Parish Council website.
