PRESENT: Cllr Val McPherson BEM (Chair), Cllrs Brian Richards, Ian Walker, Jane Rogers, Richard Myers and Rolf van Apeldoorn, 12 members of the public.

Apologies were received from Clerk Frank Post. The Minutes of 21st May 2019 were approved.

Matters arising: clarification was needed on why the P1 seat appeared on the risk assessment report. A contractor had been asked to prune the play area's overhanging cherry tree, subject to no nesting birds. Cllr van Apeldoorn agreed to open the gates for Princes Risborough Choral Society's playing field use as needed. RvA

Cllr Bendyshe-Brown's report was circulated.

PLANNING: No objection: 19/06309/FUL (11 Walkers Road); 19/06165 (Old Orchard). FP

6/ COUNCILLOR VACANCY

Two of three interested applicants had submitted applications and were asked for CVs; Cllr McPherson to arrange interviews, provisionally 8th July. VMcP

7/ MUGA PURCHASE

Dependent on playing field drainage being resolved. FP

8/ EASY ACCESS GATES

Councillors to bring proposed gate sites to the next meeting. ALL

9/ WELCOME PACKS

Could proceed subject to minor agreed alterations. JR

10/ OWLSWICK TRAFFIC CALMING

Cllr Rogers, having declared an interest, took no part. Agreed to fund the extra £1,500 recommended by TfB; road markings and VAS decisions were deferred pending Cllr Bendyshe-Brown's Bar Lane report and TfB's parish-wide traffic calming advice. FP

11/ VILLAGE HALL PROPOSALS

Cllrs Rogers and Walker, as Trustees, took no part. Following a Village Hall Trust meeting, the proposal remained in abeyance pending further communication.

12/ PLAYING FIELD DRAINAGE

The Clerk had identified a drainage professional, with a meeting scheduled for July. FP

13/ GRASS CUTTING SCHEDULE

The Clerk to request a cutting schedule for the Walkers Road community space and Ilmer Green, and establish tree-maintenance responsibility for Walkers Road. FP

14/ RISBOROUGH EXPANSION PLANS

Cllr McPherson to respond, noting the buffer zone lies within the parish Neighbourhood Plan area, with allotments, a rugby pitch and part of a sports hub proposed within the parish; response deadline 21st July 2019. VMcP

FINANCE: Payments including the Clerk's salary/allowance/expenses, Devolved Services grass cutting, risk assessment, and Q2 bin collections. Total £1,221.86. A £51,400.01 WDC receipt was noted for May; the internal audit report remained pending. FP

16/ CORRESPONDENCE

National Lottery outdoor gym funding had been declined; Cllr Myers to seek supplier advice. RM

17/ COUNCILLOR REPORTS

Cllr Walker reported from WDALC; Cllr Myers reported on a unitary authority meeting.

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 16th July 2019 at 7.00pm. The meeting closed at 9.00pm.
