PRESENT: Cllr Mrs Valerie McPherson BEM (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Jane Rogers, Rolf van Apeldoorn, Graham Walters, Sally Whitworth and Brian Richards, Clerk Mrs Susanne Griffiths, Clerical Assistant Mrs Jayne Mylchreest. No members of the public were present.

No apologies were received. The Minutes of 20th October 2015 were approved and signed. No interests were declared.

Matters arising: the tree-felling agreed in September had been completed. The Village Hall Committee had noted the Council's concerns about the unauthorised playground fence signage and agreed to review it.

FINANCE: Routine payments including grass cutting, salaries, risk assessment, dog waste/litter collection, and the Remembrance Sunday bugler and refreshments. Bank Balance £24,988.71.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No issues in the Risk Inspection Report. The ten trees for planting had been ordered.

ROADS: The Clerk to chase BCC on the lay-by CCTV request, the reversed Bar Lane weight sign, and outstanding hedge cutting notices. SG. Cllr Rogers reported the abandoned wheelchair on Green Lane had been removed.

CORRESPONDENCE: A Chiltern Society response on the Ilmer-to-Kingsey footpath was awaited. Christmas tree and lights arrangements were confirmed with thanks to the volunteer resident. Play equipment costings for the December estimates meeting were being obtained. SG

12/ NEIGHBOURHOOD PLAN STEERING GROUP

The Steering Group reported ongoing work finalising the Submission Version following the September consultation-response decisions, with the Landscape Visual Impact Assessment from Whiteleaf Cross still awaited. Cllr van Apeldoorn also raised concerns about flooding risk arising from proposed development sites, to be fed into the Plan's evidence base.

13/ BUDGET AND PRECEPT 2016/2017

A first estimate was tabled; final figures to be agreed at the December meeting.

14/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

Objected: 15/07817/OUT (OS Parcel 2075, Thame Road — outline housing), on the grounds of conflict with the emerging Neighbourhood Plan, its location outside the settlement boundary, and traffic and drainage concerns. No objections: 15/07846/FUL (1 Williams Way).

Noted: an Appeal Allowed decision reversing WDC's earlier refusal of 14/06582/FUL, permitting the solar farm at Bumpers Farm.

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 15th December 2015 at 7.45pm. The meeting closed at 9.35pm.
