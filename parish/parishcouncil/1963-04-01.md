LONGWICK. CUM-ILMER PARISH COUNCIL Meeting held school room Longwick Monday 1st April 63 7.30pm.

[Margin: Present.] Cllr Harper, Walker, Leuw, Gomme, Tapping, Mrs Brock, Miss Wootton.

[Margin: Mins.] Minutes of the last meeting were read and confirmed.

[Margin: Meadle Pond.] The chairman Cllr Harper informed the meeting that the trees round Meadle pond had been cut back, there-fore no longer obstructing the view of the bend in the road.

[Margin: Ilmer Church Sign] A further letter of refusal to erect a sign for Ilmer church was read by the clerk. The chairman suggested a sign be put up on Mr David Claydons land if he was agreeable.

[Margin: Election of Parish Councillors] All Cllrs were willing to seek re-election. The clerk was expecting the nomination papers to arrive any day and would issue them out to who-ever wanted one.

[Margin: Hiring the School Room for Council Meetings.] A letter was received from Mr Wood re the hiring of the school and the subject of wages for the care taker, which would cost (if hired for one hour) 13/-. After a lot of consideration it was proposed by Cllr Walker, seconded Mrs Brock that as it would be cheaper the meeting should be held in the Village Hall, carried.

[Margin: Dinner and Cabaret] A letter re a dinner and cabaret from the County of Buckingham Assc of Parish Councils was read by the Chairman.

[Margin: Finance] Clerks Salary Feb. March £2 - - Postage 2/- together with £1 - 7 . 7 for printing of letter headings by Cheverton & Laidler Ltd. also £2 - - to Messrs W & J Harper for lopping trees and clearing of same round Meadle Pond.

Meeting closed 8.15 pm. W. J. Harper 27/5/63
