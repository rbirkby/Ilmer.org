PRESENT: Cllr G Harper (Chairman), Cllrs I Walker, Mrs J Rogers, Mrs P Priestley and G O'Neill, Mrs S Griffiths (Clerk).

Apologies were received and accepted from Cllrs Mrs D Brock and Mrs V McPherson. The Minutes of the previous meeting were approved and signed. Cllr Mrs P Priestley declared an interest in Planning Applications.

Matters arising: a meeting with BALC's County Executive Officer was arranged for 4th February. The website's "Live News" and "Last Page Updated" features had been added; hits varied from 3 to 31 a week, so it was agreed to advertise the site in the newsletter and ask the school to use it in IT lessons. VM/GON. The Golden Jubilee Seat's broken bench slat was being repaired by Briants.

FINANCE: Payments for risk assessment, a website upload, Carol Service refreshments, and a £100 Section 137 grant to the Coombe Hill Monument Appeal (which commemorates former resident Albert Wooster). Bank Balance £13,632.14.

CAPITAL ITEMS: Skate park graffiti (medium risk) to be removed by Cllr Harper; a fallen tree guard had been fixed. GH. The Clerk to arrange raising of sinking paviors at the Play Area entrance, and repainting of wooden and metal seats across the playing field, play area, Ilmer and Owlswick. SG

ROADS: The Clerk to raise several Bar Lane issues (knocked directional signs, blocked drains causing flooding, missing ditch-warning posts, an obsolete road closure sign) via a meeting Cllr Rogers will hold with the Local Area Technician, plus a displaced Thame Road milestone and fallen trees by the Scouts Path. JR/SG. A litter-picking session was arranged for 7th March, with the Scouts invited to take part. SG/ALL

CORRESPONDENCE: Agreed a £100 donation to the Coombe Hill Monument Appeal. Confirmed permission for a wedding reception marquee on the Playing Field (18th August) and a fireworks display for a 25th anniversary party (26th June), the latter subject to a risk assessment, a 10.30pm cut-off and next-morning debris clearance. SG/GH

10/ ZIP WIRE CONTRACTOR

Of three quotations, Safe and Sound offered the best value at £8,500.00 for a 25m Zip Wire; installation to proceed in April. SG

11/ URGENT POTHOLE REPAIRS

The Clerk to report a list of potholes across the parish to Highways, including sites near the Red Lion, Chestnut Way, the B4009 (several stretches), Owlswick Lane, and Ilmer. SG

12/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

New application: 09/07444/FUL, Hatchmead Farm, Lower Icknield Way, Great Kimble (change of use of an egg house and pole barn section) — no objections.

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 16th February 2010 at 7.30pm. The meeting closed at 9.00pm.
