Longwick Cum Ilmer Parish Council Annual meeting held School Room Longwick May 18th 1955

Present Councillors Harper Chairman, Councillors Williams, Girling, Tapping, Claydon & the Clerk to sign Declarations of Office, to Elect Chairman & V Chairmn To Read & Confirm minutes of Last meeting matters arresing any Other business

[Margin: Minutes] minutes of Last meeting Read & Confirmed

[Margin: Election of Officers] Pro Councillor Claydon Sec Councillor Tapping That Councillor Harper be Re Elected Chairman & that His Health would soon Improve Carried Pro Councillor Girling, Seconded Councillor Williams That Councillor Claydon be Re Elected Vice Chairmn Carried

[Margin: Re School, Rummage Sale & outings] Pro Councillor Girling, Seconded Councillor Tapping, That Councillor Williams Interview Parties Concerned & Report to next Council meeting Carried

W J Harper June 22/55
