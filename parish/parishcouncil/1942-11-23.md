Meeting of the Longwick-cum-Ilmer Parish Council held in the Schoolroom, Longwick on Monday November 23rd 1942 at 6.30. pm. To read and confirm Minutes of last meeting To appoint a Trustee to Monks Risborough Charities Payment of accounts. Present:- Councillors H. S. Pope (in the chair) F. Williams. W. J. Harper, H. W. Lewis, E. J. Girling and the Clerk.

[Margin: Apology for absence] Apology for absence received from Councillor Wm A. Walker.

[Margin: Minutes] Minutes of last meeting read and confirmed.

[Margin: Trustee to Monks Risborough Charities] Proposed by Councillor E. J. Girling seconded by Councillor H. W Lewis, that Mr Chas Tucker be appointed a Trustee to the Monks Risborough Charities, subject to his consent to serve, failing his consent, Mr. Owen Tapping of the Bungalow Owlswick, be appointed. Carried

[Margin: Payment of accounts] Proposed by Councillor W. J. Harper seconded by Councillor F. Williams, that the following accounts be passed for payment:- H. R. East, Premium on Employers Liability Insurance 10/-d. Commons Preservation Society, Annual Subscription 10/6d. Robert Bailey, Half years Salary as Clerk to 29/9/42 £2/10/-. Carried Wm A Walker Feby 15th 1943
