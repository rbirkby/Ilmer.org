APOLOGIES FOR ABSENCE

No apologies were received.

2/ MINUTES

The Minutes of the previous meeting of the Parish Council on the 17th January 2012, having been circulated, were approved by the Meeting and signed by the Chairman.

3/ DECLARATIONS OF INTEREST

Members were asked to declare any personal interest and the nature of that interest which they may have in any of the items under consideration at this meeting. None were declared.

4/ MATTERS ARISING NOT OTHERWISE ON THE AGENDA

A presentation was made on behalf of Longwick Scout Group by Alan Inglis (Treasurer) and David Floyd (Beavers Leader). The group is facing possible closure because of a lack of available leaders and is carrying out a mail-shot to all addresses in the Parish. They pointed out that the Scout Group is a valuable and popular asset to the Parish and asked if the Parish Council could help in the search for leaders. Cllrs V McPherson and B Richards suggested including a brief job description with the mail-shot, and Cllr McPherson offered to put a piece in the next Parish newsletter, copy needed by 19th March 2012. Cllr G O'Neill recommended that the Parish Council write to BCC explaining the situation and asking for a rent holiday. The Scouts will need to decide whether or not to close by mid-May 2012.

Mr Jeremy Elgin gave a presentation about his application to erect a wind turbine at Lower Waldridge Farm, Ford, outlining his reasons for installing the wind turbine.

Cllr B Richards gave an update on his recent visit to RAP (Risborough Area Partnership), which is divided into 5 subgroups; 'Communications' will be the most relevant to the Parish Council. There will be a meeting of the Communications subgroup on Tuesday 28th February, which Cllr Richards will attend.

Cllr V McPherson reported a lot of litter by the skate park despite bins being supplied; it appears that either the bins are full or people are not using them.

6/ CAPITAL ITEMS AND COMMUNITY FACILITIES

A. Playing Field. Risk Inspection Report dated 28th January 2012: Cllr I Walker reported that the only issue highlighted is that the paviors going down to the gate are becoming quite overgrown with weeds and moss. Cllr G Harper replied that it is too early to spray but he will attend to this when appropriate. The hedges will be cut in the next few weeks.

B. Children's Play Area. Risk Inspection Report: Cllr G Harper confirmed that there were no medium or low risk issues highlighted.

7/ ROADS, VERGES AND RIGHTS OF WAY

Cllr J Rogers reported that the only matters attended to since the last meeting were that the flashing speed sign now works, the white lining at the junction of Chestnut Way has been done, and the tyres in Ilmer have been picked up.

8/ CORRESPONDENCE

Invitation to attend the Chairman of Wycombe District Council's Civic Service on the 25th March 2012. This was not addressed.

The Parish Council has received an e-mail from the Ford Action Group Against Turbine (FAGAT) to elicit its opinion on the proposed wind turbine at Lower Waldridge Farm. Cllr J Rogers had attended a meeting at Waldridge Manor at which approximately half the residents of Owlswick expressed their concern; she also understands that the residents of Dinton and Ford are very much against the proposal. Cllr G Walters pointed out that the Parish Council will be notified of this as a normal planning application and should delay discussion until it knows it is to be consulted, suggesting that a member of FAGAT be invited to present to the Parish Council so it can see both sides of the issue.

Cllr G Harper has received an e-mail from Ralph Dadswell about Wycombe Cycling Club hiring Longwick Village Hall on 14th April, concerned about parking and asking to use the Playing Field. All agreed that using the Playing Field for parking would be OK as long as actual playing surfaces are not used.

Cllr G Walters has received an e-mail from Louise Willis of BCC on the success of the Snow Saviours Appeal, wondering whether the data gathered would be valuable for a Community Plan. Cllr Walters proposed writing back to say that the Parish Council would be interested in the data when a decision has been reached as to whether to prepare a Community Plan. All agreed.

Cllr V McPherson asked if the Parish Council could have a word with the people doing a barn conversion at Walkers, as they are leaving a lot of litter blowing over a wide area. Cllr G Harper replied that work on the barn is now complete, so the problem should stop; he will keep an eye on it.

9/ REPORTS

School Governors. Cllr V McPherson told the meeting that children at the local primary schools have made 'Slow Down' posters and that Longwick has won first prize.

NW Chilterns Local Area Forum. Cllrs G Walters and G O'Neill are going to the meeting on 22nd February; the reduction of the speed limit is on the list for discussion as a local priority for the year commencing April 2013.

WDALC. Cllr B Richards informed the meeting that the next meeting will be on 15th March.

N.A.G. Nothing to report.

Newsletter. Cllr V McPherson reminded the meeting that she will need the piece on the Diamond Jubilee soon; Cllr G O'Neill is producing this.

Cllr G Walters proposed adding an 'F' to 'Reports' — 'To Receive a Report on Meeting with RAP'. All agreed.

10/ THE DESIGN OF THE MUG TO CELEBRATE THE DIAMOND JUBILEE IN THE PARISH

There was much discussion on the best way to distribute the mugs and to whom they should be given. Cllr G O'Neill recommended that mugs should be given to all children at the school, with a note in the Newsletter for any child not at school who would like one. Cllr V McPherson suggested Pre-School children be included. Cllr G Harper proposed mugs be given to every child in the Parish aged 0-11 years (approximately 110 in the age range 5-11). Cllr J Rogers said it would be nice to include the elderly of the Parish. Distribution and age range is still to be definitely decided. Cllr V McPherson gave estimated prices from David Lockett for bone china mugs: £4.50 a mug × 200 = £900 plus £40.00 delivery = £900.40, or 250 × £4.50 = £1,125 + £40 = £1,165. Cllr G Harper said a figure of £1,200.00 had been budgeted for the whole Diamond Jubilee event. All agreed to let Cllr McPherson decide on the final design and to order 220 mugs.

Regarding making the field available for a Diamond Jubilee event, Cllr G Harper has received expressions of interest from 34 people; two, Catherine Trout and Emma Thornsley, have volunteered to organise an event. Cllr O'Neill agreed to contact them and let them know £450.00 would be available.
