PRESENT: Cllr Mrs Valerie McPherson BEM (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Jane Rogers, Rolf van Apeldoorn, Graham Walters, Sally Whitworth and Brian Richards, Clerk Mrs Susanne Griffiths, Clerical Assistant Mrs Jayne Mylchreest, 3 members of the public.

No apologies were received. The Minutes of 19th January 2016 were approved and signed. No interests were declared.

Cllr Bendyshe-Brown (BCC) reported on: Bar Lane flooding; Thame Road lay-by fly tipping (soil analysed and now clearable); Ilmer Road flooding issues resolved, with resurfacing due next financial year; Stockwell Lane/Askett Lane planning enforcement being monitored by WDC; BCC budgets; bus pass renewals; the Queen's birthday celebrations; and a new Community Bus arriving in March 2016.

Matters arising: the LOGS-led "Clean for the Queen" litter pick was arranged for 5th March, meeting at two points (Bell Crescent and Walkers Road/Thame Road junctions) at 10.30am. The Playing Field project had been shortlisted for a Tesco Bags of Help grant of £8,000–£12,000, the final amount to be decided by customer voting at local Tesco stores 27th February–6th March; the Council noted Tesco's branding/PR guidelines. The Internal Auditor would report on 7th April 2016.

FINANCE: Routine payments including salaries, pension, risk assessment, and the annual Local Council Insurance policy (£1,349.28). Bank Balance £19,147.43.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No issues in the January Playing Field Risk Inspection. A medium-risk bench/fence repair (£167.47) was approved for the children's play area. JM

ROADS: The Clerk to report a rubbish-filled caravan in the Lower Icknield Way lay-by (TFB ref 46013627), a dirty directional sign at the Thame Road roundabout, a request to lower the Blenheim Close hedge, a dead Elder on Dorrells Road, a pothole at Sans Paddock, Owlswick, and a displaced manhole cover/damaged posts at Resta While, Owlswick. JM

CORRESPONDENCE: The Princes Risborough Town Plan Draft Plan Consultation Document was received, with a Council response to be agreed at the next meeting. An exhibition on an outline residential scheme at Rose Farm, Thame Road was noted for 29th February.

10/ NEIGHBOURHOOD PLAN STEERING GROUP

The Examiner's Report was due for publication shortly; WDC had requested a meeting on it. Agreed the Steering Group would attend and handle minor decisions, with an extraordinary Parish Council meeting to be called for any major decision.

12/ LOCAL COUNCIL AWARD SCHEME QUALITY STATUS

Agreed to apply, at a cost of £30. SG

13/ PLAY EQUIPMENT WORKING GROUP

A quiz night and village fete stall were arranged to raise funds, to be advertised in the newsletter and on the website/noticeboards. SW/JM

14/ FLOWER CONTAINERS AT VILLAGE ENTRANCES

Agreed to accept local suppliers' offer to make and supply containers for the four village entrances, subject to BCC permission. JM/SW/VM

15/ NEWSLETTER ADVERTISING RATES

Agreed charges of £50 (full page), £30 (half page) and £20 (quarter page) including VAT, to be reviewed after one year.

16/ PLAYING FIELD VEHICLE ACCESS

Following tyre-mark damage from an unauthorised vehicle, agreed (5–1, one abstention) to change the padlock code. VM

17/ 16/00050/OP — ALLEGED UNAUTHORISED ACCESS CREATION

Police had attended the site; WDC continues to monitor.

18/ INSURANCE POLICY REVIEW

Agreed the existing cover remained sufficient.

19/ "CLEAN FOR THE QUEEN" EQUIPMENT

Agreed to purchase 15 hi-vis jackets and 28 litter pickers, cost not to exceed £100. SG

20–21/ SPEED LIMITS IN MEADLE AND STOCKWELL LANE / NO OVERTAKING ON THE B4009

Following resident concerns about vehicle speeds, agreed to request a reduced speed limit in Meadle and along Stockwell Lane, and a "No Overtaking" restriction on the B4009 for 50 metres either side of the Stockwell Lane junction; the Clerk to liaise with Cllr Bendyshe-Brown. SG

22/ PLAYING FIELD WEED AND FEED TREATMENT

Approved a quotation (£230 herbicide + £276 fertiliser, plus VAT) for spring treatment, with the play area treated in the first week of May. JM

23/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

15/08455/OUT (OS Parcels 6232 & 7428, Thame Road — up to 43 dwellings): the Neighbourhood Plan Steering Group to draft a response for member approval. 16/05151/CTREE and 16/05175/CTREE (Ilmer): to abide by the Arboriculturalist's decision / no comment (declared interest). 16/05232/FUL (5 Lower Icknield Way): Cllrs McPherson and Whitworth to site-visit and report back. Approved: 15/08392/FUL and 15/08393/LBC (Meadle Cottage); 15/08269/CLE (Chadwell Hill Farm); 15/08433/TPO (Upper Manor Farm, Ilmer — fell Weeping Willow). Noted: the 14/06965/OUT appeal (Land off Barn Road) would be heard at a WDC public inquiry from 12th April, sitting six days.

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 15th March 2016 at 7.30pm. The meeting closed at 9.50pm.
