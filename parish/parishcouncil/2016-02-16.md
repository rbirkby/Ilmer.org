5\. MATTERS ARISING FROM LAST MEETING NOT OTHERWISE ON THE AGENDA

The Queens 90th Birthday Celebrations – Clean for the Queen litter-pick

The LOGS intend to organise a litter pick in the Village on the 5th March to coincide with the national "Clean for the Queen" event that is planned nationally. They are hoping to encourage extra people from Longwick to join the LOGS in their efforts as well as the Scouts. There will be two parties both meeting at 10.30am. One will be at the end of Bell Crescent and the second at the junction of Walkers Road with Thame Road.

An email has been received from the "Tesco Bags of Help" Grants

The 'Longwick-cum-Ilmer Parish Council – Playing Field' project has been successful in being shortlisted for a Tesco Bags of Help grant. The project is guaranteed a funding grant of either £8000, £10000 or £12000. The next stage of the process is the results from customers voting in selected local Tesco stores on the 27th Feb-6th March to determine the amount the Parish Council will receive.

The email included guidelines summarising how Tesco would like to work together with the Parish Council on public relations and social media, including how to use the Tesco branding, use of logos and social media handles.

Appointment of the Internal Auditor

The Internal Auditor has been appointed and will carry out the audit on the 7th April 2016.
