PRESENT: Cllr Mrs Valerie McPherson BEM (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Jane Rogers, Rolf van Apeldoorn and Brian Richards, Clerk Susanne Griffiths, Deputy Clerk Jayne Mylchreest. No members of the public.

Apologies were received and accepted from Cllr Whitworth. The Minutes of 20th September 2016 were approved and signed. Cllr Rogers declared an interest in item 12.

Matters arising: Cllr Richards updated on the Neighbourhood Plan. Cllr Bendyshe-Brown (BCC) reported on the Wellington House development, the Wycombe District Local Plan, the refusal by Transport for Bucks of two VAS applications (Chestnut Way, Thame Road) with alternative funding being sought, winter-programme area requests, and a 25th October Streamlining Local Government presentation.

FINANCE: Payments including salaries, pension, grass cutting/maintenance duties, risk assessment and newsletter production. Bank Balance £25,279.69.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No issues in the September Risk Inspection Reports. Following the risk inspector's advice, agreed to take no action on a low-risk skateboard ramp crack (temporary Cretex EP Mortar repair quoted at £360+VAT) unless it widens.

CORRESPONDENCE: A Bucks Playing Fields Association AGM invitation was noted. Resolved to oppose the Government's proposal to extend council tax referendum principles to parish and town councils, and to write to John Bercow MP. SG. Noted BCC's move toward LED street lighting; the Council's one footpath light (installed c.1989 following a fatality) was reported accordingly. SG. The Longwick Sports Club's Extraordinary General Meeting on possible closure was noted, pending formal outcome.

9/ FLOWER CONTAINERS UPDATE

Briants of Risborough had made and installed containers free of charge at three village entrances, with Penns Flowers of Longwick donating flowers and spring bulbs; thank-you letters to be sent. JM

10/ NEW NOTICEBOARD

Agreed a specification, with quotations to be obtained for the November estimates meeting; agreed to sell the old parish map noticeboard for £50. JM

11/ 2017 MEETING DATES

Agreed the 2017 schedule, including the Annual Parish Meeting and Council Meeting on 18th April and the Annual Parish Council Meeting on 16th May.

12/ VILLAGE HALL POLYANTHUS PLANTING

Cllr Rogers having declared an interest, agreed to the Village Hall Management Committee's request to plant polyanthus on the drive edge. JM

13/ WEED SPRAYING POLICY

Following Transport for Bucks' withdrawal from routine weed spraying (citing pollinator impact and reduced herbicide effectiveness under new legal constraints), resolved that the Parish Council also withdraws from weed spraying in the parish, to be communicated via the newsletter and website. JM

14/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

Objected: 16/07465/FUL (The Rosary, Ilmer Lane, Ilmer — detached garden building), as inappropriate in open countryside. To abide by the Arboriculturalist's decision: 16/07610/CTREE and 16/07659/CTREE (Meadle Cottage; The Cobblers, Meadle). 16/07622/LBC (Dock Farm, Meadle): an extension was sought to allow further consideration. Approved: 16/06835/FUL (Ray Farmhouse); 16/06994/FUL (Folly Cottage, Ilmer). Refused: 16/06732/OUT (Crownridge, Longwick Road). Noted: an appeal in progress for Barn, Longwick Mill (16/05276/PNP3Q).

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 15th November 2016 at 7.30pm. The meeting closed at 8.55pm.
