PRESENT: Cllr Mrs Valerie McPherson (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Graham Walters (joined 8.10pm), Mrs Jane Rogers, Rolf van Apeldoorn, Brian Richards and Sally Whitworth, Clerk Mrs Susanne Griffiths, County Cllr B Bendyshe-Brown, 2 members of the public.

No apologies were received. The Minutes of 18th November 2014 were approved and signed. Cllr Bendyshe-Brown reported on parish matters including ongoing clearance of the Longwick-to-Ilmer footpath. Cllr Val McPherson was still waiting for further dog-fouling signs. RvA

FINANCE: Routine payments (a Poppy Appeal wreath £25.00, newsletter/admin expenses £141.77, village hall hire £54.00, PAYE £5.60, dog waste/litter collection £308.88, Clerk's December salary £433.37, Carol Service refreshments £68.82) were examined and signed off. Bank Balance £17,859.62.

CAPITAL ITEMS: High-risk issues were flagged in the November Playing Field inspection — a damaged goal post and a car-hit fence post. Cllr Ian Walker confirmed the post/fence section had been repaired immediately; Cllr Sally Whitworth and the Clerk are obtaining further quotations (per financial regulations) for full fence replacement next year, and the sports club had been told the goal post needed urgent repair. SW/IW/SG. No issues in the Children's play area inspection.

ROADS: Nothing to report.

CORRESPONDENCE: A letter from the Rt Hon John Bercow MP on a £15m listed-church roof scheme was forwarded to St Peter's, Ilmer. UK Power Networks' request to lay a new cable along the Playing Field's eastern edge and Blenheim Close access road was approved, subject to reinstatement and advance notice to the Sports Club, in return for a wayleave agreement and payment. SG. A resident's offer to organise a LOGS litter pick around March was gratefully accepted, with a photograph requested for the newsletter. SG. The Pensions Regulator confirmed the Council's auto-enrolment staging date as 1st January 2016; the Clerk has registered the Council's details. SG. A grant request from Risborough Area Community Bus for a new bus was deferred to the January budget-setting meeting.

10/ THE FUTURE OF LONGWICK — WHETHER TO PREPARE A NEIGHBOURHOOD PLAN

Cllr Sally Whitworth proposed applying to designate the whole Parish as a Neighbourhood Area and holding a public meeting to consult residents on a Neighbourhood Plan. Seconded by Cllr Val McPherson; all present agreed. Actions: the Village Hall to be booked for a public meeting on 13th January 2015 at 7.45pm (SG/JR); Cllr Graham Walters to invite a District Council officer to answer residents' questions and draft a leaflet (GW); Cllr Val McPherson to arrange printing of 650 leaflets once the text is agreed (VM); all councillors to hand-deliver leaflets across the parish; the Clerk to publicise via the website, notice board and local papers. SG

11/ LITTER IN THE VILLAGE

The Clerk to obtain "Please take your litter home" signs from WDC. SG

12/ DEVOLVED SERVICES UPDATE

Cllr Sally Whitworth reported that Longwick-cum-Ilmer would be included in the first tranche to receive Devolved Services, including grass cutting.

13/ COMMUNICATIONS QUESTIONNAIRE RESPONSES

90 of 145 replies received to date had been processed; the remainder to be input by the next meeting. SG

14/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

New applications, all with no objections or deferring to the Arboriculturalist: 14/08200/TPO (5 Woodbine Close — fell and replant an Ash); 14/08173/FUL (Front Barn, Hatchmead Farm — change of use to storage; Cllr Jane Rogers declared an interest and took no part); 14/08072/PAMB (Owlswick Barn — change of use to a 3-bed dwelling); 14/08030/FUL (Bracknell, Lower Icknield Way — single storey side infill extension).

Approved/no objection: 14/07928/CTREE (The Stables, Horsenden Manor — tree works); 14/07401/FUL (1 Central Cottage — rear extension); 14/07220/DMI (Armour Farm — partial demolition); 14/07801/CONSA (replacement overhead line pole near Footpath 12).

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 20th January 2015 at 7.45pm. The meeting closed at 9.35pm.
