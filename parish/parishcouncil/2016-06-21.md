PRESENT: Cllr Mrs Valerie McPherson BEM (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Jane Rogers, Sally Whitworth, Rolf van Apeldoorn and Brian Richards, Deputy Clerk Jayne Mylchreest, 47 members of the public.

No apologies were received. The Minutes of 17th May 2016 were accepted. No interests were declared. No matters arose outside the agenda.

Cllr Bendyshe-Brown (BCC) reported by note: the Wellington House gunnel closure had been temporarily reopened by the developer without WDC planning or BCC Rights of Way intervention, with WDC investigating why a 1996 refusal on footway grounds appeared to have been overlooked in 2016, and BCC awaiting legal advice on an immediate stop pending formal assessment (not expected before September); footpath fencing between the Village Hall and school had been repaired, though responsibility for ditch-side overgrowth remained unresolved; VAS costings and repairs were in hand; and the Red Lion-to-Green footway was in for urgent repair.

FINANCE: Payments including salaries, pension, grass cutting, risk assessment, playing field weed and feed treatment, PAYE, weed spraying, a replacement play area gate, and playing field hedge cutting. Bank Balance £29,738.19.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No issues in the June Playing Field Risk Inspection. The play area gate replacement agreed in May had been completed.

CORRESPONDENCE: A resident's concern that BCC/Amey no longer treated the Meadle school bus as a dedicated route was referred to Cllr Bendyshe-Brown. The Bledlow-cum-Saunderton Neighbourhood Plan pre-submission consultation was noted. Agreed playing field access for the Princes Risborough Music Society (29 June–2 July), the Cricket Club's fixtures, and a school "Race for Life" (10th July). JM. A resident's concern about the Wellington House right-of-way closure was referred onward, with the Clerk asked to seek an urgent update from BCC, WDC and the Head of Planning. JM

9/ RE-SITING THE PARISH NOTICEBOARDS

Agreed (4–2) to site the noticeboards on the grass verge by the village shop and Yew Tree Cottage, subject to the resident's views. JM

10/ LOCAL AREA FORUM BUDGET PROJECT

Deferred to the next meeting pending discussion with Cllr Bendyshe-Brown; TfB proposals were due by 31st August.

11/ WYCOMBE DISTRICT COUNCIL LOCAL PLAN

Following WDC's apology for not consulting the Council, the Clerk was asked to arrange a meeting with Head of Planning Penelope Tollitt to explain how a potential 130 new homes had grown to 400 without further infrastructure; a public meeting would follow. JM

12/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

16/06375/FUL (Land to north/rear of The Old Pigeons, Thame Road — 5-bed dwelling) and 16/06084/FUL (Chiltern, Chestnut Way, alternative scheme): site visits and draft responses to be prepared. 16/06682/CTREE (Bank Farm, Meadle): to abide by the Arboriculturalist's decision. 16/06623/FUL (Malbaret, Bar Lane, Owlswick): an extension was requested to allow further consideration. Approved: 16/05852/FUL and 16/05873/FUL (5 Lower Icknield Way; 14 Dorrells Road); 16/06086/CTR (Dock Farm — no TPO).

Noted with significant concern: the 14/06965/OUT appeal for up to 160 dwellings at OS Parcel 9166 Boxer Road & OS Parcel 6576 Walnut Tree Lane/Barn Road was **Allowed**.

14/ EXCLUSION OF PUBLIC AND PRESS

Agreed, under Section 12 of the 1960 Act, to discuss the preferred playground equipment supplier in closed session.

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 19th July 2016 at 7.30pm. The meeting closed at 9.40pm.
