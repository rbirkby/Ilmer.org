PRESENT: Cllr Mrs Valerie McPherson BEM (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Jane Rogers, Rolf van Apeldoorn, Graham Walters and Brian Richards, Clerk Mrs Susanne Griffiths, Clerical Assistant Mrs Jayne Mylchreest, County Cllr B Bendyshe-Brown.

Apologies were received and accepted from Cllr Sally Whitworth. The Minutes of 21st July 2015 were approved and signed. Cllrs Jane Rogers and Ian Walker declared an interest in agenda item 11.

Cllr Bendyshe-Brown reported that footpath resurfacing and ditch clearance between the Scout Hut and Dorrells Road was complete and reopened, with some school access-road/car park defects also addressed using surplus budget. A Vehicle Activation Sign application was being assessed by the Local Area Forum. Winter works requested: reducing the hedge height by the scout path/Blenheim Close; tidying the Thame Road roundabout (removing a temporary sign, replacing hazard signs); siding out footways; and a hedge cutting notice for Summerleys Road where hedges obscure the 30mph sign. SG

FINANCE: Routine payments (grass cutting, salaries, risk assessment, dog waste/litter collection, external audit fee £120, an age restriction sign, and £447.64 of Neighbourhood Plan stationery). Bank Balance £23,813.91.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No issues except a Category 1 high-risk offensive graffiti report on the playing field ramp (removed immediately).

ROADS: Following discussion of BCC's reduced Devolved Services funding (£3,102.14, with grass cutting cut to four cuts/year), Cllr Richards proposed continuing with Devolved Services for the next financial year and reviewing again in September 2016; agreed. Issues to report: an obscured 30mph sign at the Longwick end of Owlswick; Bridle Path 14 blocked by brambles/fly tipping; an uncleared Thame Road lay-by with further dumping; misaligned fingerposts on Bar Lane; and a hedge cutting notice for Maplesfield, Owlswick Lane. SG

CORRESPONDENCE: A WDC Gambling Act 2005 licensing policy review was noted. A resident's concern about Council-owned overhanging trees was referred to item 12. Deferred until studied: WDC's Devolved Services 2016/2020 contracts and a website hosting terms change. Agreed to a bouncy castle/games/ice cream van event on the Playing Field (9th July 2016) subject to risk assessments and insurance. JM. A resident's request to upgrade play equipment prompted the Clerk to ask WDC about funding, with Cllr Richards to canvass the school for ideas. BR/SG

10/ NEIGHBOURHOOD PLAN STEERING GROUP

The Steering Group's 11th August meeting reported that the Draft Plan consultation (8 June–21 July, with an exhibition) had closed, with online and paper responses summarised; a noise assessment survey and an NPIERS Health Check had been commissioned to inform the next steps toward the Submission Version.

11/ DEFIBRILLATOR DONATION

Should the Village Hall proceed with installing a defibrillator, Cllr Richards proposed the Parish Council match its fundraised donations up to £250; seconded by Cllr McPherson; carried (Cllrs Rogers and Walker having declared an interest and taken no part).

12/ TREE SURVEY WORKING GROUP

Following the WDC Tree Survey Report 2015, Cllr Walters' counter-proposal to fell three trees immediately (a leaning Ash and ivy-covered Sycamore in the ditch, and a Larch near the Scout HQ) with the remainder deferred to March 2016 was carried over Cllr van Apeldoorn's original six-tree proposal. SG

13/ STANDING ORDERS

Agreed to adopt the updated standing orders. SG

14/ EXTERNAL AUDIT CERTIFICATE 2014/2015

Received with no matters raised.

15/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

Objected: 15/07008/OUT (OS Parcel 9166, Boxer Road — up to 50 dwellings) and 15/07209/OUT (OS Parcel 4446, Longwick Road — 9 dwellings), both on the grounds of conflicting with the emerging Neighbourhood Plan (which itself derives from WDC's Longwick Village Capacity Study 2015), increased traffic risk without junction improvements, lack of a safe cycle route to the station, and inadequate sewage/drainage capacity; the Boxer Road scheme additionally omitted the Plan's proposed open space west of the Village Hall, and the Longwick Road scheme also encroached on the green buffer between Longwick and Princes Risborough.

Deferred pending a site visit: 15/07389/FUL (Land adjacent to Home Farm) and 15/07508/FUL (Old Oaks). No objections: 15/07444/FUL (Lyndale, Stockwell Lane). Noted: 15/06638/MINAMD and 15/06678/CLP.

Approved: 15/06587/FUL (Chiltern, Chestnut Way); 15/06893/FUL (3 Woodbine Close); 15/06930/FUL (St Peters Chapel, Owlswick); a Tree Preservation Order was made for a Beech on Ilmer Lane, Ilmer. Refused: 15/06161/OUT (the 160-dwelling Boxer Road/Walnut Tree Lane outline scheme).

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 20th October 2015 at 7.30pm. The meeting closed at 9.50pm.
