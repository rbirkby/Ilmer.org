PRESENT: Cllr Mrs Valerie McPherson (Chairman), Cllrs Ian Walker, Graham Walters, Mrs Jane Rogers and Brian Richards, Mrs S Griffiths (Clerk), County Cllr B Bendyshe-Brown and Wycombe District Councillor Steve Lacey.

Apologies were received and accepted from Cllr Sally Whitworth. The Minutes of 20th May 2014 were approved and signed. Cllr B Bendyshe-Brown reported on roads and footpaths in the parish.

Matters arising: the £120 gate post hinge repair and hedge-cutting quotation were being progressed; the graffitied bridge had been reported to Network Rail (ref 3986524); BCC agreed to clear the ditch bordering Longwick School once the Playing Field section was cleared, work due to commence in July. The website free-makeover offer requires a 5-year commitment letter; deferred to the next meeting. SG

FINANCE: Routine payments (website notice £18.00, Risk Assessment £15.03, grass cutting £442.88, PAYE £5.60, herbicide treatment £372.00, Clerk's June salary £433.37) were examined and signed off. Bank Balance £15,251.31.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No issues in the May Risk Inspections. Cllr Ian Walker asked that the Playing Field grass-cutting contract go out to tender before the Estimates meeting. SG

ROADS: The two fallen Thame Road signs were still outstanding; footpath 4 beyond the railway bridge reported as overgrown. SG

CORRESPONDENCE: Cllr Sally Whitworth to attend WDALC basic planning training (£15). Agreed in principle to a Communications Questionnaire (paper and web) on resident engagement, with Cllr Brian Richards to review content. BR. Cllrs Graham Walters and Jane Rogers to attend the WDC Planning Forum. GW/JR

10/ THE GENERATION OF INCOME FROM THE PLAYING FIELD

An outside football team had asked to hire a pitch regularly. All present agreed it was premature to commit the pitch to an outside team while it remained unclear whether the local team would need it this winter; the Clerk to decline for this season. SG

11/ THE PROPOSED B.C.C. DEVOLUTION PROGRAMME

All present agreed in principle to participate. SG

12/ FILLING THE VACANCY FOR A COUNCILLOR BY CO-OPTION

Following WDC confirmation no by-election was required, agreed to advertise the vacancy with a closing date of 11th July. SG

13/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

New applications: 14/06351/CTREE (Water Springs, Meadle — tree works; to abide by the Arboriculturalist's decision); 14/06523/FUL (Hatchmead Farm, Lower Icknield Way, Great Kimble — change of use of a cow barn to workshop/storage; recommended for approval).

Approved: 14/05659/CLP (Kingswood, Chestnut Way — rear extension).

NEXT MEETING: Tuesday 15th July 2014 at 8.00pm. The meeting closed at 9.30pm.
