11/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

Progress on applications already considered:

- 09/05583/FUL: Brook Cottage, Horsenden Lane, Princes Risborough. Construction of single storey side extension. Permitted.
- 09/05576/FUL: 5 The Green, Longwick. Erection of single storey conservatory to rear. Permitted.

New Applications to be considered:

- 09/06046/FUL: Land Adjacent Hazel Trees, Lower Icknield Way, Longwick. Erection of 1 x 2 bed chalet bungalow and detached garage (alternative scheme to pp 07/06603/FUL). The Parish Council has no objections.
- 09/05790/FUL: Land at Hemley Hill, Upper Icknield Way, Saunderton. Amended plans for the (part retrospective) application for the change of use of land to include the stationing of caravans for 9 gypsy pitches with utility/day room buildings and hard-standing ancillary to that use. The Parish Council abides by its original decision to recommend that the application is refused. The land is near the parish boundary and the members oppose the application as it is the opinion of the Parish Council that the proposed development would result in an unsatisfactory and inappropriate form of development out of keeping with the character of the surrounding area. The development would have an adverse impact upon the special character, appearance and enjoyment of the Chilterns AONB and would result in the loss of open space that would detract from the openness of the Green Belt.

Alleged Breach of Planning Permission: a member of the public has reported apparent 'unlawful' use of a building known as the Stables, just within the Longwick Parish boundary on Mill Lane. When the application was approved, it was made clear that the building was for horses only, and not to be used for human habitation. Over the last few months, a mailbox and a nameplate have appeared on the gate in Mill Lane and cars have been parked on the far side of the building, partially out of sight from passers by. The Parish Council has not seen any subsequent applications for a cancellation of conditions imposed on the applicant in respect of its use. The Clerk is requested to contact the Enforcement Officer.
