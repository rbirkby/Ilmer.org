PRESENT: Cllr Mrs Valerie McPherson (Chairman), Cllrs Brian Richards, Ian Walker, Mrs Jane Rogers and Graham Walters, Mrs Susanne Griffiths (Clerk).

No apologies were received. The Minutes of the meeting on 18th March 2014 were approved and signed. Cllr Bill Bendyshe-Brown's report was given at the preceding Annual Parish Meeting.

FINANCE: Routine payments (Risk Assessment £15.03, data protection registration £35.00, clerk cover fee £31.80, three £75 churchyard maintenance grants to St Michael's Horsenden, St Dunstan's Owlswick and St Peter's Ilmer, a £250 grant to the Risborough Area Community Bus, Clerk's April salary £433.37, grass cutting £442.88, Bucks Playing Fields subscription £20.00) were examined and signed off. Bank Balance £8,525.04.

CAPITAL ITEMS: No issues were highlighted in the March Risk Inspection Reports. A quotation of £310+VAT for selective weed killing on the Playing Field was accepted, to be carried out after the Village Fete on 3rd May. The Clerk was asked to arrange clearing of the Playing Field ditch and check the next grass-cutting date. SG. Cllr Ian Walker will arrange for Briants to repair a gate post hinge in the play area. IW

ROADS, VERGES AND RIGHTS OF WAY: The Clerk was asked to report two fallen signs on the Thame Road near the old milestone between Ray Farm and Penns Flower Nursery. SG

CORRESPONDENCE: WDC requested details for a district play areas audit informing a new Play Strategy; the Clerk will reply. SG. An invitation to a BALC Parish Liaison Meeting was noted. Community Impact Bucks asked parishes to maintain a 'Register of Interests' for the Bucks Home Choice Rural Exception Schemes; agreed to support this. SG

10/ UPDATING THE PARISH COUNCIL WEBSITE

Deferred to the next meeting when Cllr James Butler is present.

11/ THE METHOD OF APPROACHING LANDOWNERS ABOUT CLEARING DITCHES

Cllr Jane Rogers agreed to prepare a list of local landowners for the Clerk to contact. JR

12/ AN UPDATE ON THE NAG

Cllr Valerie McPherson reported the Outer Risborough Group had ceased to exist due to lack of interest.

13/ AN UPDATE ON THE NEWSLETTER

The newsletter had been delivered later than planned due to late article submissions and a printing problem; agreed to change the front cover design. VM

14/ ADDING AN EXTRA AREA TO THE EXISTING OUTLINE TO ILMER VILLAGE GREEN

Deferred to the next meeting when Cllr James Butler is present.

15/ ON WHETHER TO REQUEST THE CHILTERN SOCIETY TO MAINTAIN THE PATHS AS PART OF THE PARISH PATHS CLEARANCE SCHEME

Agreed to request the Chiltern Society maintain the paths devolved from Rights of Way, donating the BCC funding received for path maintenance to the Society. SG

16/ FILLING THE VACANCY FOR A COUNCILLOR BY CO-OPTION

Following WDC confirmation that no by-election was required, agreed to advertise the vacancy until 19th May and invite interested residents to the next meeting. SG

17/ PLANNING APPLICATIONS

New application: 14/05785/FUL, Noahs Ark, Thame Road, Longwick (removal of a side lean-to extension and erection of a single storey front & side extension) — no objections.

Approved: 14/05659/CLP and 14/05214/HP (Kingswood, Chestnut Way — single storey rear extension); 14/05276/FUL (Sheredon, Thame Road — two storey 4-bed detached dwelling, alternative scheme).

NEXT MEETING: The Annual Meeting of the Parish Council, Tuesday 20th May 2014 at 8.00pm. The meeting closed at 10.00pm.
