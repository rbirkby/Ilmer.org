On Friday March 26th 1897 at seven oclock in the evening a meeting of the Electors of the Parish was held in a room hired for the occasion of Mr James Burnham for the purpose of electing a Chairman & two Overseers as the Governing Body of the Parish Meeting for the ensuing year - the Revd J. Ormond was re-elected Chairman & Messrs Richard Belgrove & Alfred Hill were re-elected Overseers.

Signed Richard Belgrove\
Chairman.
