A Meeting was held in the Parish Room Ilmer on Feb 29 1916 at 6.30 p.m.

There were present the Rev H M Gorham Messrs Thomas Goodchild, W Hill, Albert Goodchild, G. Gomme, FW Kingham.

The Minutes of the last meeting were read and confirmed.

Mr Alb Goodchild proposed, Mr Gomme seconded Mr Walker be written to again to make good the Bridges.

Chairman. Rev H M Gorham. Propd by Mr Alb Goodchild. seconded by Mr Thos Goodchild. carried nem con.

Overseers. Rev H.M. Gorham and G. Gomme be overseers propd by Mr Alb Goodchild, Seconded by Mr Thos. Goodchild.

Constables. Messrs Thos Goodchild and F. Kingham be Constables for ensuing year propd by Alb Goodchild seconded by Mr G Gomme.
Carried nem. con.

H.M. Gorham.
