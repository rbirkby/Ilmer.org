A Parish Meeting was held at Fritz W Kingham house Illmire on Febry 25th 1903 at six o clock in the evening for the Purpose of Appointing a Chairman and also appointing two Overseers and also for Nominating Parish Constables

On a Motion of Mr G Kingham seconded by Mr R Belgrove the Revd J E Varley was appointed Chairman

the Majority at the Meeting appoint George Kingham and George Gomme Overseers

the Majority at the Meeting Nominate George Kingham & Thomas Goodchild & Fritz W Kingham as Parish Constables

George Kingham\
Richard Belgrove
