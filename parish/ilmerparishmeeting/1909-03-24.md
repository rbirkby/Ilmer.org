On Wednesday March 24th a Parish Meeting was held in the Parish Room at 6.30 pm There were present Messrs D. Cook, G. Gomme, J. Stratford, T. Goodchild A. Hill, Fritz W. Kingham, W. Kingham, & the Rev J.E. Varley.

On the motion of Mr. Hill, seconded by Mr Fitzwilliam Kingham Messrs George Kingham and Thomas Belgrove were appointed Overseers.

On the motion of Mr F. W. Kingham seconded by Mr. W. Kingham that Rev J.E. Varley was re-appointed Chairman.

On the motion of Mr Goodchild seconded by Mr. Fritz W. Kingham Messrs Stratford & W. Kingham were appointed Constables.

On the motion of Mr Thos Goodchild seconded by Mr. W. Kingham a vote of thanks was made to the outgoing Overseers for the service they have done to the Parish.

J. E. Varley\
Chairman\
March 24th 1909.
