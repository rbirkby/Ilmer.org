On Saturday March 28th 1896 at half past six in the evening a Meeting of the Parochial Electors was held in a room hired for the occasion of Mr James Burnham for the purpose of electing a Chairman & two Overseers as the Governing Body of the Parish Meeting for the ensuing year. --

The Revd John Ormond was unanimously elected Chairman & Messrs Richard Belgrove & Alfred Hill were unanimously elected Overseers.

Signed John Ormond.\
Chairman.
