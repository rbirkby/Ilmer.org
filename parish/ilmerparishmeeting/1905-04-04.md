On Tuesday April 4th 1905, a Parish Meeting was held at the house of Mr. Thos. Goodchild. There were present Messrs R. Belgrove, G. Gomme, T. Goodchild & Rev. J.E. Varley

On the proposal of Mr. Belgrove seconded by Mr. Gomme the Rev J.E. Varley was re-appointed Chairman.

On the proposal of Mr. Gomme seconded by Mr. Goodchild Messrs R. Belgrove & Alfred Hill were appointed overseers.

On the proposal of Mr. Goodchild seconded by Mr. Belgrove outgoing overseers were thanked for their services.

Mr. Gomme proposed & Mr. Goodchild seconded that the attention of the Rural District Council be called to the extremely bad state of the road from Illmer to Cold Harbour.

J. E. Varley\
Chairman of the Meeting.
