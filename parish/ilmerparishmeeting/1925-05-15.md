A Parish meeting was held at the Parish Room, Illmire on Friday May 15th 1925 at 6.30 pm.

To read and confirm Minutes of last meeting.\
To confirm the resolution moved at the last meeting respecting the diversion of a footpath at Illmire.

Present:- The Revd Wm M Tuke (Chairman) Messrs Alfred Hill, Fritz Wm Kingham and Robert Bailey (Assistant Overseer).

Minutes of last meeting read and confirmed.

Proposed by Mr. Fritz Wm Kingham seconded by Mr. Alfred Hill that the resolution as moved at the last meeting relating to the diversion of a footpath in the Parish of Illmire be now confirmed.
Carried

W.M. Tuke, Chairman.\
1 June 1925.
