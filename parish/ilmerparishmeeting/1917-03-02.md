[Margin: 1917.] A Parish Meeting was held in the Parish Room, Illmire, on Friday, the 2nd of March, 1917, at 6.30 p.m. There were present - the Rev. H. M. Gorham (in the chair), Messrs. Fritz Kingham, George Gomme, Thomas Goodchild and T. Stratford.

The Rev. H. M. Gorham was appointed Chairman for the ensuing year.

The Rev. H. M. Gorham and Mr. T. Goodchild were appointed Overseers, and Messrs. T. Goodchild and F. Kingham Parish Constables for the ensuing year.

W. O'Connor,\
Chairman.
