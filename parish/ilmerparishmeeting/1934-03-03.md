The Annual Parish Meeting was held in the Parish Room, Illmire, on Saturday March 3rd 1934 at 7.0 p.m.

To read and confirm Minutes of last meeting\
To nominate Parish Constables.\
To pass accounts.\
Any other business.

Present:- Messrs George Gomme (Chairman), Harry Goodchild, Harry Herbert Gomme and Robert Bailey (Clerk)

Minutes of last meeting read and confirmed.

[Margin: Shelter at Railway Halt] The Clerk reported that with reference to the provision of a shelter at the Railway Halt the Secretary of the Railway Company had written to the effect, that the matter had received careful consideration, but, having regard to the very small user by the public of Ilmer Halt at the present time, it is felt that the expenditure involved in the provision of a shelter could not be justified.

[Margin: Matter to be referred to new Parish Council of Longwick-cum-Ilmer] It was agreed that the matter should be referred to the new Parish Council of Longwick-cum-Ilmer as soon as it was possible, to see if they could persuade the Railway Company to re-consider their decision, as it was still felt that a Halt was necessary.

[Margin: Parish Constables] It was agreed that Messrs Harry Goodchild and William Frederick King of Illmire be nominated to serve the office of Parish Constables for the ensuing year.

[Margin: Accounts] Proposed by Mr Harry Goodchild and seconded by Mr George Gomme that the accounts as paid to date by the Treasurer be approved, and that he be authorised to pay any other accounts which may become due to March 31st 1934 and that this meeting authorises the sum of Five shillings to be paid to the Churchwardens of Illmire for use of Parish Room for meetings.
Carried

[Margin: Financial Statement] The Financial Statement of accounts for the year ending March 31st 1933 as certified by the District Auditor was laid before the meeting.

[Margin: Vote of thanks] A vote of thanks was accorded to the Chairman and Clerk for their services.

(District Audit Stamp)
