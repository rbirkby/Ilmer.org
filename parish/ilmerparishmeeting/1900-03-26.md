on Monday March 26th 1900 at 6 oclock in the evening a Parish Meeting was held at James Burnhams House for the Purpose of appointing a Chairman for the ensuing year and appointing two Overseers

on the Motion of Mr Alfred Hill seconded by Mr George Gomme the Revd J E Varley Rector of the Parish was appointed Chairman for the ensuing year, Richard Belgrove being Chairman of the Parish Meeting in Mr Varleys absence

George Kingham and Thomas Goodchild was appointed Overseers to collect half years Rates each
this motion was Carried Unanimously

Signed\
Richard Belgrove\
Chairman of the Parish Meeting
