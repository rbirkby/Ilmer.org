A Parish Meeting was held in the Parish Room on Wednesday March 22.

Proposed by Geo Kingham Seconded By Thos Goodchild that Geo Gomme was Elected Chairman,

Proposed by Mr. A Hill seconded by Mr Thos Goodchild that Mr Geo Kingham be Elected Overseer for the ensuing year,

Proposed by Mr A Goodchild seconded by Mr Thos Goodchild that Mr F W Kingham be Elected Overseer for the ensuing year

Proposed that Mr Thos Goodchild Mr F W Kingham be elected Constables for the ensuing year =

Proposed by Mr A Goodchild seconded by Mrs Geo Kingham that the Foot Bridge in the Parish be put into a passable Condition

Geo Gomme\
March 22 1911

All these were carried unanimously.

H. M. Gorham.
