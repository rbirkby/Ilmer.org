A Parish Meeting was held in the Parish Room, Illmire on Friday February 28th at 6.30 pm.

To read and confirm Minutes of last meeting.\
To nominate Parish Constables for the ensuing year.

Present:- Messrs George Gomme (in the chair), Harry Goodchild and Robert Bailey (Clerk).

Minutes of last meeting read and confirmed.

[Margin: Parish Constables] Messrs Geoffrey Brian Quint and Harry Goodchild were nominated to serve as Parish Constables for the ensuing year.

A. Goodchild
