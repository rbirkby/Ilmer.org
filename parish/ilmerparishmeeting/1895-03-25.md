On Tuesday the 25th day of March 1895 A Meeting of the Electors of the Parish was held in a room hired for the occasion of Mr. James Burnham to Elect two Overseers to serve for the ensuing year - Mr. Alfred Hill & Mr. George Gomme were unanimously elected to that office.

Signed. Richard Belgrove\
Chairman.
