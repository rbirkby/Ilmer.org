on Tuesday March 25th 1902 at 6 o clock in the evening. a Parish Meeting was held for the Purpose of appointing a Chairman it was held at Mr James Burnhams House, and also for appointing two Overseers for the Parish of Illmire

on the Motion of Mr R Belgrove seconded by Mr A Hill the Revd. J E Varley was appointed Chairman for the ensuing year on the motion of Mr Belgrove seconded by Mr Hill Messrs Kingham and G Gomme ware appointed Overseers

Signed\
George Kingham\
Chairman of the Parish meeting
