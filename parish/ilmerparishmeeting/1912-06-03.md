A meeting was held in the parish Room at 7pm. 3rd June.

Present Rev H.M. Gorham in the Chair, Miss Phillips, Mr. A Goodchild & Mr G. Kingham

The Minutes of the last Meeting were read and confirmed.

The Chairman then read to the meeting the tenders for bridges by G Gomme and J Walker.

It was proposed by Mr G. Kingham seconded by Miss Phillips that the whole question be postponed until after the property is disposed of.

The ensuing discussion showed that there were many good reasons for taking this step and no opposition being offered the proposal was carried nem. con.

The Meeting then terminated.

H.M. Gorham.\
Chairman.
