A meeting was held on Mar [blank] 1913 at 7 oclock.

There were present Rev H M Gorham in the chair Messrs G. Gomme, George Kingham, Fitzwilliam Kingham, Thomas Goodchild W. Kingham

The minutes of the last meeting were read and confirmed.

On the motion of Mr G. Kingham seconded by Mr G. Gomme, the Rev H M Gorham was elected chairman for the ensuing year.

Messrs Thos Goodchild & FW Kingham were elected Constables, proposed by G. Kingham seconded by G. Gomme.

Mr Albert Goodchild and Mr George Kingham Elected Overseers on the proposition of Mr G. Gomme, seconded by Mr Thos Goodchild.

A proposition that the Bridges mentioned above (Min for 1912) be made by Mr. G Gomme Seconded by Mr Thos. Goodchild was carried unan.

Mr Walker's tender was accepted.

H.M. Gorham.
