A meeting was held on May 30, 1901 at half past 6 O clock in a room of George Kingham to consider altering the Bridle path & foot path leading from Illmere to Long Wick proposed by Mr Belgrove and seconded by Mr Goodchild that the alteration should be made. Carried.

Signed\
George Kingham\
chairman of the paris meeting
