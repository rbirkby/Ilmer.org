On Friday March 25th 1898 at Seven O'Clock in the evening a Parish meeting was held in a room at James Burnhams for the Purpose of appointing a Chairman for the ensuing year and appointing two Overseers

Richard Belgrove } was appointed Overseers\
& Alfred Hill } each to Collect Half years Rates

Signed) Richard Belgrove\
Chairman
