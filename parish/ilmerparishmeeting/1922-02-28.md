A Parish Meeting was held in the Parish Room Illmire on Tuesday February 28th 1922 at 7.30 pm.

To appoint a Chairman for the ensuing year.\
To read and confirm Minutes of last meeting.\
To appoint Two Overseers of the Poor for the ensuing year.\
To make out a list of men liable and qualified to serve the Office of Parish Constables for the ensuing year.\
To pass accounts.

Present:- The Revd W. M Tuke, Messrs A. Hill. G. Gomme. Fredk Ward, Fritz Wm Kingham. Wm Kingham, Wm Claydon and Thomas Bradshaw.

Proposed by Mr. Fritz Wm Kingham seconded by Mr. George Gomme that the Revd Wm M Tuke be appointed Chairman for the ensuing year. Carried.

The Minutes of the last meeting were read and confirmed.

Proposed by Mr A. Hill seconded by Mr. Geo Gomme that Mr. Fredk Ward be re-appointed an Overseer of the Poor for the ensuing year. Carried.

Proposed by Mr. George Gomme seconded by Mr. Fredk Ward that Mr. Alfred Hill be appointed an Overseer of the Poor for the ensuing year. Carried.

Proposed by Mr. Wm Claydon seconded by Mr. Fredk Ward that Messrs Thomas Goodchild & Fritz Wm Kingham of Illmire be re-appointed to act as Parish Constables for the ensuing year. Carried.

Proposed by Mr. Fritz Wm Kingham seconded by Mr. Wm Claydon that the accounts of the Parish Meeting for the year be passed as correct. Carried.

Proposed by the Revd W. M Tuke seconded by Mr. Fredk Ward that a letter be forwarded to the District Council as follows:-
The Parish meeting of Illmire desires to draw your attention to the very heavy calls made on the Parish and to express their dis-satisfaction with the way the roads in the Parish are maintained. The road leading to Illmire, is being at the present time in a very bad state of repair and dangerous to the public. Although a very small Parish they have been called upon to contribute during the past year no less than £292 from the Rates to the Wycombe Union and District Council respectively, and the meeting considers that in view of the amount contributed more attention should be paid to the just requirements of the Parish and they would urge the District Council to give immediate attention to this complaint.
Carried Unan:

W.M. Tuke. 1. 3. 1923
