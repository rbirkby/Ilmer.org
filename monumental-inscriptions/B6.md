Hanc Eccelsiam \
inter antiques antiquissimam \
annorium longa serie \
labefactam et inclinatam \
propria cura \
instauravit ac ornavit \
Guilielmus E PARTRIDGE A.B. \
Vicarius idem et patronus \
Anno Christi \
MDCCCLX \
Solideo honor et gloria

(This Church \
among the most ancient \
be length of years seriously \
weakened and decayed \
at his own proper charge \
restored and furnished \
William E PARTRIDGE B.A. \
Vicar of the same and patron \
in the year of Christ \
1860 \
to God alone be the honour and glory)
