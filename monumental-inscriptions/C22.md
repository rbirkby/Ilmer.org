In \
loving memory \
of Martha BELGROVE \
who died September 10th 1909 \
aged 44 years \
for 25 years organist at this church \
if Thou shouldst call me to resign \
what most I prize it ne'er was mine \
I only yield Thee what is Thine \
Thy will be done

MB 1909
