To \
the blessed memory of \
John Thomas CLAYDON \
of Longwick \
died Aug 14 1936 aged 71 years

And Charlotte his beloved wife \
who died Nov 14th 1943 aged 77 years

Also Ruth their daughter \
who died Jan 6th 1937 \
aged 42 years \
worthy of everlasting remembrance
